## The permit

You need a Texas sales and use tax permit from the Texas Comptroller of Public Accounts before you sell taxable goods. The permit is **free (seen November 2026; confirm current fee)**, but you may be asked to post a security bond. Apply online and allow 2–3 weeks to receive it. You need one permit for each place of business. Your phone number on the permit is public, so expect calls from vendors.

## The rate

The state rate is **6.25%**. Cities, counties, transit authorities and special districts can add up to 2%, for a combined rate of up to 8.25%. Use the Comptroller's Sales Tax Rate Locator to find the rate for an address. Ask an accountant how local rates apply to items you ship.

## Filing

The Comptroller tells you whether to file monthly, quarterly or yearly. Returns are **due on the 20th** of the month after the period ends. File a return even if you made no sales. Late returns carry a $50 penalty plus a percentage of the tax (seen November 2026; confirm current amounts).

## Buying stock to resell

Give your suppliers a completed **Texas Sales and Use Tax Resale Certificate (Form 01-339)** so you do not pay sales tax on stock you resell. Your permit number alone is not enough. Keep copies for 4 years. See step 13.

## Sellers outside Texas

Out-of-state sellers must get a Texas permit once their **Texas revenue reaches $500,000** in the past 12 months. For selling into other states yourself, see step 6.

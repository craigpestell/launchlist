## No general state license

**Texas does not require a general business license.** Your assumed name certificate or LLC certificate of formation (step 2) fills that role. Some activities do need a state license or permit, for example selling food, alcohol or certain regulated products. Check the Texas Business Licenses and Permits Guide from the Governor's Business Permit Office.

## City and county rules

Local rules **vary from place to place**. Contact your city, or your county if you are outside city limits, and ask about:

- Home business (home occupation) rules if you work from home
- Zoning and a certificate of occupancy if you rent a shop
- Health permits if you sell food
- Sign permits and markets or pop-up events

Do not assume another city's rules apply to you.

## Keep track

Write down each license number and when it renews. The Business Permit Office offers a free tracker template and can help if an application gets stuck.

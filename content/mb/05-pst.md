## What it is

Manitoba charges **7% retail sales tax (RST)** on most goods sold at retail in Manitoba. It is separate from GST and is registered, collected and filed with **Manitoba Finance**, not the CRA. Registration is free.

## Who must register

Manitoba says a business that sells taxable goods at retail in Manitoba must register. There is a small-business exception: you do not have to register if your taxable sales are **under $30,000 a year and you buy all your goods from sellers registered for Manitoba RST**. The exception does not apply if you buy from out-of-province suppliers that do not collect Manitoba RST, which is common for online stores. If in doubt, register.

## How to register

Register online through **TAXcess**, Manitoba's online tax service. You will receive a **7-digit RST number**.

## Your supplier needs this number

Manitoba says vendors quote their RST number when buying goods **for resale without paying RST**, so the tax is charged once, to your customer. The number does not let you buy things for your own use tax-free.

## Collecting and filing

You collect RST from Manitoba customers on the price before GST, then file and remit on the schedule Manitoba Finance gives you. **File even if you collected nothing** for the period.

## Who has to register

In Manitoba you register a **business name** when you trade under a name other than your own first and last name. If you trade as just "Jane Smith", you can skip this step.

## Reserve the name first

The Companies Office says a **name reservation** is needed in most cases before you register. The request costs **$45**, and you can file it online. If the name is rejected, you pick another and pay again. An approved name is held for **90 days**.

## Then register it

File the Business Name Registration online, using the reservation number from your approval. It costs **$60**, and online filing is the fastest. The registration lasts **three years**, and renewing it costs another $60.

## Your details may be public

Ask the Companies Office what appears on a business name search. If you work from home and do not want customers to see your address, ask whether you can use a **PO box or a mailing address service** instead.

## Keep it

Save the confirmation. You will need it for the bank account and your supplier account.

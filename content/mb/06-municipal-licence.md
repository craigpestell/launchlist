## It depends on your municipality

There is no general Manitoba business licence. Each municipality decides what it licenses.

- In **Winnipeg**, the city licenses only a short list of business types, such as used goods dealers, flea markets and precious metals dealers. It says you **do not need a business licence if you work from home**.
- If you sell used goods, check Winnipeg's used goods dealer licence before you start.

Look up your own municipality rather than assuming Winnipeg's answer applies to you.

## Home-based business

Winnipeg requires a **home-based business development permit** under its zoning by-law if business mail comes to your home or you do the business's administrative work there. That covers most home-based online stores, so check the zoning rules for your address before you start.

## Be upfront about what you sell

Some products or business types carry extra by-laws or requirements, including food and used goods. Tell your municipality exactly what you will sell so you know before you start, not after.

## Keep the answer

Save your permit or licence. If the municipality confirms you need neither, get that in writing or save the page, because banks and suppliers sometimes ask.

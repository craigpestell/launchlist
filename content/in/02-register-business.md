## Sole proprietor: assumed business name

If you trade under a name that does not include your surname, file a **certificate of assumed business name** (often called a DBA) with the **county recorder** in each county where you have a place of business or office. It gives the business name and your full name and address. Monroe County charges **$25 (seen November 2026; confirm current fee)**, and other counties checked charge the same, but each recorder sets its own process. Ask your recorder whether the form must be notarized. There is no newspaper notice to publish.

The certificate is public and shows your address. If you work from home, ask the recorder what address you can use. File a notice with the recorder when you stop using the name.

## LLC: Articles of Organization

If you chose an LLC in step 1, file **Articles of Organization with the Secretary of State on INBiz** ($100 by mail, about $95 online, seen November 2026; confirm the current fee on INBiz). An LLC trading under another name files its assumed name with the Secretary of State, not the county ($20 online or $30 by mail by statute, seen November 2026; confirm current fee). From 2026, a business that works entirely remotely can ask to keep a home address off the public record. Check the Secretary of State's guidance when you file.

## LLC: business entity report every two years

An LLC files a **business entity report every other year, in the month it was formed**. It costs $31 online or $50 by mail (seen November 2026; confirm current fee), and you can file up to 90 days early. If you miss it, the Secretary of State sends reminders and then dissolves the LLC once the report is five months overdue. Indiana has no franchise tax or minimum tax for a single-member LLC.

A sole proprietor has no yearly state filing for the business name. Ask an accountant about anything else that applies to you.

## Keep it

Save the filed certificate or your LLC's filing receipt. You will need them for the bank account (step 8) and your supplier account (step 13).

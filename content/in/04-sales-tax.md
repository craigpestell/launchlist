## The Registered Retail Merchant Certificate

Indiana's sales tax permit is called a **Registered Retail Merchant Certificate (RRMC)**. It comes from the Indiana Department of Revenue (DOR), and you apply through INBiz with the Business Tax Application. You need it before you make retail sales, including online and at shows. The fee is **$25 one-time per location (seen November 2026; confirm current fee)**. Display one certificate at each business location. DOR renews it every two years at no extra step if your returns and payments are up to date.

## The rate

The rate is **7% everywhere in Indiana**. Counties and cities do not add their own sales tax. Some places charge a separate food and beverage tax on prepared food, which usually does not affect a retail store. Ask an accountant how sales tax applies to what you sell.

## Filing

DOR sets **how often you file based on your average monthly tax** and reviews it each year. Check your filing schedule when your registration is approved (thresholds not confirmed on DOR's pages, November 2026). You must file a return even if you had no sales. A late return can cost a penalty of up to 20%, with a minimum of $5 (seen November 2026; confirm current penalty).

## Buying stock to resell

Give your suppliers a completed **Form ST-105, General Sales Tax Exemption Certificate**, marked for resale, so you do not pay sales tax on stock you resell. Do not use it for things you keep for the business. See step 13.

## Sellers outside Indiana

Out-of-state sellers must register once their **sales into Indiana exceed $100,000** in the current or previous calendar year (seen November 2026; confirm current threshold). For selling into other states yourself, see step 6.

## State licenses

The state's Business Owner's Guide says **Indiana does not have one general business license** for every business (checked November 2026). Some products and activities need their own state license. The State Information Center (317-233-0800) can tell you whether yours does.

## County and city permits

Indiana's cities and counties set many of their own rules, so **check with the local offices where you are based and where you sell**. The Department of Revenue says its sales tax certificate is not a vendor's license and points sellers to the county clerk's office for one, so ask your county clerk whether your county requires it. Some places also license traveling sellers who have no fixed business location, which can matter if you sell at markets or shows.

## Working from home

Zoning and sign rules are almost always local. If you work from home, **ask your city or county about home business rules** before you start, for example whether customers can pick up orders.

## Keep them

Save copies of any license or permit and note the renewal dates. Your insurer (step 7) may ask for them.

## The Certificate of Registration

Tennessee's sales tax permit is called a **Certificate of Registration**. It comes from the Tennessee Department of Revenue, and you register online through TNTAP, the Tennessee Taxpayer Access Point. Register before you start doing business, whether you sell from home, online or at markets. You need one for each location. The Department's sales tax manual lists no fee for a regular registration (seen November 2026; confirm when you apply).

## The rate

The state rate is **7%** on most goods (4% on food). Every city or county adds a local rate of up to 2.75%. For sales within Tennessee, the local rate is generally the one where your business is located. Local tax applies only to the first $1,600 of any single item, and an extra 2.75% state tax applies to the part of a single item's price between $1,600 and $3,200. Ask an accountant how the rates apply to what you sell.

## Filing

Tennessee sets **monthly filing as the default** for most sellers, due on the 20th of the following month. Once your tax has averaged $1,000 or less a month for 12 months in a row, you may be able to file quarterly. You file online, and you file a return even if you made no sales.

## Buying stock to resell

When you register, you are automatically issued a **Tennessee Sales and Use Tax Certificate of Resale**. Print it from TNTAP and give a copy to your suppliers so you do not pay sales tax on stock you resell. Do not use it for things you keep for the business. See step 13.

## Sellers outside Tennessee

Out-of-state sellers must register once they have **more than $100,000 in sales** to Tennessee customers in the previous 12 months (seen November 2026; confirm current threshold). For selling into other states yourself, see step 6.

## Sole proprietor: no state name filing

The Secretary of State says Tennessee **does not recognize DBAs or fictitious names**, and its assumed name filing is for LLCs and corporations only. As a sole proprietor you are registered under your own legal name. You enter your trade name when you register with the Department of Revenue for sales tax (step 4) and on your county business license (step 5). Ask your county clerk whether your county keeps any other record of trade names (not confirmed on an official page).

## LLC: Articles of Organization

If you chose an LLC in step 1, file Articles of Organization (Form SS-4270) with the Secretary of State, online through TNCaB or by mail. The fee is $50 per member, **$300 minimum (seen November 2026; confirm current fee)**, and a card or e-check fee applies online. You need a registered agent with a physical Tennessee address, and what you file is public record. An LLC that trades under another name files an Application for Registration of Assumed Name ($20, seen November 2026; confirm current fee), which lasts five years and can be renewed.

## LLC: annual report

Every LLC files an annual report with the Secretary of State by the first day of the fourth month after its fiscal year ends, which is **April 1 for a calendar year**. The fee is $300 minimum, up to $3,000 (seen November 2026; confirm current fee). If you do not file, the state can dissolve the LLC. The Secretary of State warns about private companies sending official-looking annual report notices, so file through its own site.

## LLC: franchise and excise tax

Tennessee charges LLCs **franchise and excise tax, even a single-member LLC owned by one person**. Franchise tax is 0.25% of the LLC's Tennessee net worth, with a **$100 minimum** each year, and excise tax is 6.5% of its Tennessee taxable income (seen November 2026; confirm current rates). The return is due on April 15 for a calendar year. A sole proprietor does not pay this tax. Ask an accountant how it applies to you.

## Keep it

Save your filed documents and note the April 1 and April 15 dates if you have an LLC. A sole proprietor has no yearly state filing for the business name. You will need your records for the bank account (step 8) and your supplier account (step 13).

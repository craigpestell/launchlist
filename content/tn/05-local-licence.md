## Business license

Most in-state businesses need a **business license from the county clerk, and from the city clerk** if the business is inside a city that issues one. The Department of Revenue says **each license is $15 (seen November 2026; confirm current fee)**, and you must have it before you start doing business. Apply in the county where your business is located.

## Which license you need

The license depends on your yearly gross sales:

- **Under $3,000:** no business license.
- **$3,000–$100,000:** a minimum activity license. You do not file business tax, and you renew it with your county clerk.
- **Over $100,000:** a standard business license. You register for business tax in TNTAP and file a yearly return, which renews the license.

Business tax is a small tax on gross sales with a $22 minimum, and some cities add their own. Ask an accountant which applies to you.

## Other licenses and local rules

Some products need their own license, for example alcohol from the Alcoholic Beverage Commission. Rules on home businesses and zoning are set by your city or county, so **ask your local planning or zoning office** before you run the business from home or sell at a market in another place.

## Keep them

Post your licenses where you do business and note their renewal dates. Your insurer (step 7) may ask for them.

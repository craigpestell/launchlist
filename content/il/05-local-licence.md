## State licenses

Illinois does **not have one general state business license** for every business (checked November 2026; confirm with DCEO). Some products and activities need their own state license, for example alcohol, tobacco or food. The Illinois Department of Commerce and Economic Opportunity (DCEO) handbook lists the agencies to ask, and its Business Information Center can help.

## County, city and village permits

DCEO tells new owners to check with the **county clerk and the city or village hall** for local business licenses, zoning, and building or health permits. The rules differ from place to place, so check with the offices where your business is based and where you sell, for example at a market or show. If you work from home, ask about home business and zoning rules.

## Chicago

The City of Chicago has its own business licenses, which apply only inside the city. If you are based or sell there, check with the city before you start.

## Keep them

Save copies of any license or permit and note the renewal dates. Your insurer (step 7) may ask for them.

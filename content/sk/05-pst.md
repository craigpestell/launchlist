## What it is

Saskatchewan charges **6% PST** on most goods sold or used in Saskatchewan. It is separate from GST and is registered, collected and filed with the **Ministry of Finance**, not the CRA.

## Who must register

Saskatchewan says **all businesses making retail sales in Saskatchewan must register** for a PST number. There is no sales threshold for a retailer. The small-trader exception only covers home crafters who sell from their own home, not online stores.

## How to register

Register online through **Saskatchewan eTax Services (SETS)** with the New Business Registration form. You receive a **vendor's licence number**. We found no registration fee, but the ministry may ask for security before it issues a licence.

## Your supplier needs this number

Licensed vendors buy goods **for resale without paying PST** by quoting their vendor's licence number. There is no separate certificate. The number does not let you buy things for your own use tax-free.

## Collecting and filing

You charge PST on the price before GST. You file monthly, quarterly or annually depending on how much tax you collect. **File even if you collected nothing** for the period.

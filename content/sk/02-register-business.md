## Reserve the name first

You need a **name reservation number** before you register. Reserve the name online in the Corporate Registry application, choosing "Saskatchewan business name registration" and "sole proprietor". Choosing the wrong purpose means paying again. A reservation costs **$50** and is held for **90 days**.

## Register online

Registering the business name costs **$65**. The same application can also register you with the CRA for a Business Number and with the Ministry of Finance.

## Renew every three years

The registration lasts **three years**, and renewing costs $60. You must renew even if nothing has changed, and report an address change within 15 days.

## Your details are public

A registry search shows your mailing address and owner details. If you work from home and do not want customers to see your address, use a PO box or a mailing address service.

## It depends on your municipality

We found no general Saskatchewan business licence for a small retailer, but cities set their own rules.

In **Saskatoon**, every business needs a licence. A **home-based business licence** costs **$135**, then $95 a year to renew, and the City takes 2–3 weeks to review it. Saskatoon doesn't allow on-site sales of goods from a home business, and it limits visits, including deliveries, to 8 a day. Ask the City whether online sales shipped from home fit its rules before you start.

Look up your own municipality rather than assuming Saskatoon's answer applies to you.

## Be upfront about what you sell

Some products or business types carry extra by-laws or requirements, including food. Tell your municipality exactly what you will sell so you know before you start, not after.

## Keep the licence

Save it. Banks and suppliers sometimes ask to see it.

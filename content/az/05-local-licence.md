## City and county licenses

The Arizona Corporation Commission's start-up steps say to register with **the city where your business is located, or the county if you are in an unincorporated area**. That covers city TPT (handled through your TPT license in step 4) and any business or occupational license the city requires. Some Arizona cities have a general business license and others do not, so check with your own city.

## State licenses

No single Arizona business license for every business was found (checked November 2026; confirm with the Arizona Commerce Authority). Some products and activities need their own state license, for example food, alcohol or tobacco. If you sell anything like that, check with the state agency for it.

## Home businesses and events

If you work from home, ask your city about **home business and zoning rules**. If you sell at a market, fair or show, ask the organizer and that city what permits you need there, since each place sets its own rules.

## Keep them

Save copies of any license or permit and note the renewal dates. Your insurer (step 7) may ask for them.

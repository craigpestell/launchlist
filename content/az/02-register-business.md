## Sole proprietor: trade name

If you trade under a name other than your own, you can register it as a **trade name with the Arizona Secretary of State**. Arizona law says you "may" register, so it is not required, but it gives you a public record of the name and banks often ask for one (step 8). The fee is **$10 (seen November 2026; confirm current fee)**, and a registration lasts 5 years. You can renew it in the 6 months before it ends.

The Arizona Corporation Commission (ACC) does not register DBAs. Its FAQ says you can also record a DBA with the Maricopa County Recorder. Other counties may differ, so ask your county recorder if you want a county record too.

## LLC: Articles of Organization and statutory agent

If you chose an LLC in step 1, file Articles of Organization with the ACC online or by mail ($50, or $85 expedited, seen November 2026; confirm current fee). You must name a **statutory agent with a physical Arizona street address**, who must accept the appointment. You can be your own agent if you live in Arizona. Everything you file with the ACC is public, including your principal address, so the ACC suggests a P.O. Box or your agent's address if you sell online.

## LLC: newspaper publication

Within **60 days after the ACC approves your articles**, some LLCs must publish a notice in a newspaper in the county of their statutory agent, three times in a row. This does not apply if your agent's street address is in a county with more than 800,000 people. The ACC posts the notice on its own website instead, at no charge. The ACC sends you a letter saying whether you must publish. Newspapers set their own prices, so ask a few before you file.

## What you file each year

- **Arizona LLCs do not file an annual report**, and no yearly state LLC fee was found.
- A sole proprietor has no yearly state filing for the business name. Renew a trade name every 5 years.

Your yearly state tasks are your sales tax license renewal and returns (step 4) and your income tax return. Ask an accountant about anything else that applies to you.

## Keep it

Save your trade name certificate or your filed articles. You will need them for the bank account (step 8) and your supplier account (step 13).

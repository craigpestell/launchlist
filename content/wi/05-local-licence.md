## State licenses

Wisconsin does not appear to have one general business license for every business (checked November 2026; confirm with the state). Instead, some products and activities need their own license. **The Department of Safety and Professional Services (DSPS) lists many state credentials**. Other state agencies may license specific products, so check what applies to what you sell. Your seller's permit (step 4) is a tax registration, not a general license.

## County, city, village and town permits

Wisconsin cities, villages, towns and counties **set their own permit rules**, and they differ from place to place. Check with the local office where your business is based and where you sell, for example at a market or show. If you work from home, ask about home business and zoning rules.

## Keep them

Save copies of any license or permit and note the renewal dates. Your insurer (step 7) may ask for them.

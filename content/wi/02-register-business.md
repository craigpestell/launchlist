## Sole proprietor: trade name registration

Wisconsin **does not require you to register a trade name**. DFI says registration is a good way to tell the public you use a name, but it gives you no ownership; your rights come from using the name. If you do register, file with DFI online or on paper. The form must be notarized. The fee is **$15 for 10 years (seen November 2026; confirm current fee)**, and you can renew in the six months before it expires. Search DFI's records first. Your bank may ask how you trade, so check with them (step 8).

## LLC: Articles of Organization

If you chose an LLC in step 1, file Articles of Organization with DFI. It costs **$130 online or $170 on paper (seen November 2026; confirm current fee)**. You name a registered agent who is based in Wisconsin. Wisconsin LLCs can also use the state's One Stop Business Registration portal, which covers DFI and your Department of Revenue registration in one place.

## LLC: annual report

Each year after the year you formed it, your LLC files an **annual report by the end of the calendar quarter of its formation anniversary**. For example, an LLC formed in May files by June 30. It costs $25 online or $40 on paper (seen November 2026; confirm current fee). If you miss it, DFI can dissolve the LLC. No franchise tax or LLC minimum tax was found for a single-member LLC.

## What a sole proprietor files each year

A sole proprietor has **no yearly DFI filing**, unless you renew a trade name after 10 years. Your yearly state items are sales tax returns, the $10 seller's permit renewal every two years (step 4) and your income tax return. Ask an accountant about anything else that applies to you.

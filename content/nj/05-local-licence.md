## State licenses

New Jersey does not appear to have one general business license for every business (checked November 2026; confirm on Business.NJ.gov). Instead, some products and activities need their own state license. The **Licensing and Certification Guide on Business.NJ.gov** lets you search more than 500 state licenses, permits and certifications by industry.

## Municipal and county permits

The state's Small Business Manual says towns may charge fees to businesses, **for example fire protection or mercantile licenses**. Whether you need one depends on your municipality, so contact your municipal clerk. The rules differ from town to town, and some towns have none for a home-based online store. If you work from home, ask about home business and zoning rules. If you sell at a market or show, ask the organizer and the town about permits.

## Keep them

Save copies of any license or permit and note the renewal dates. Your insurer (step 7) may ask for them.

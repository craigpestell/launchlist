## The Certificate of Authority

New Jersey's sales tax permit is the **Certificate of Authority for Sales Tax**. You get it by registering with the Division of Revenue and Enterprise Services on Form NJ-REG (step 2). Register **at least 15 business days before you start business**. This applies even if you sell only now and then, for example at a flea market or craft show. No fee is listed (seen November 2026; confirm when you apply). Display the certificate where you do business.

## The rate

The rate is **6.625% statewide**, and local governments generally do not add their own sales tax. A few exceptions exist, such as reduced rates for certified Urban Enterprise Zone sellers and some in-person sales in Salem County, and special taxes in Atlantic City and Cape May County. Most clothing and footwear and most groceries are exempt. Ask an accountant how the rules apply to what you sell.

## Filing

You **file a quarterly return (Form ST-50) online by the 20th** of the month after each quarter: April 20, July 20, October 20 and January 20. You file even if you had no sales. Monthly payments are needed only once you collect more than $30,000 a year (seen November 2026; confirm current threshold). New Jersey also charges retailers of certain products a small litter control fee, with an exemption for smaller sellers. Check the Division of Taxation's page if it might apply.

## Buying stock to resell

Give your suppliers a completed **Form ST-3, Resale Certificate**, so you do not pay sales tax on stock you resell. Do not use it for things you keep for the business. See step 13.

## Sellers outside New Jersey

Out-of-state sellers must register once they have **more than $100,000 in sales or 200 or more separate sales** delivered into New Jersey in the current or prior calendar year (seen November 2026; confirm current threshold). For selling into other states yourself, see step 6.

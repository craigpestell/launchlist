## Sole proprietor: trade name certificate

If you trade under a name other than your own, register a **trade name with the county clerk** in each county where you do business. The forms must be notarized. Fees are set by each county, for example **$54 in Union County and $50 in Gloucester County (seen November 2026; confirm current fee)**. Your registration protects the name only in the counties where you file. Some clerks ask you to search their trade name records first.

## Everyone: register for taxes (NJ-REG)

**Every business doing business in New Jersey must register** with the Division of Revenue and Enterprise Services on Form NJ-REG, including sole proprietors. You can file it online. As a sole proprietor you can use your Social Security number or an EIN (step 3). This is also how you get the sales tax certificate in step 4. No fee is listed (confirm when you apply).

## LLC: Certificate of Formation

If you chose an LLC in step 1, file a Certificate of Formation online ($100, seen November 2026; confirm current fee) and name a registered agent with a New Jersey address. Then file NJ-REG. An LLC that trades under another name registers an alternate name with the state ($50, seen November 2026; confirm current fee).

## LLC: annual report

An LLC files an **annual report every year, $75 (seen November 2026; confirm current fee)**. It is due by the last day of the month in which the LLC was formed, and it keeps your registered agent and address up to date. Missing it can lead to the LLC being revoked.

A sole proprietor has no annual report and no yearly state filing for the trade name. You do file sales tax returns (step 4) and your income tax return. Ask an accountant about anything else that applies to you.

## Who has to register

The Northwest Territories requires you to register a business name unless you trade under your **exact legal name**. Adding a word such as "Designs" to your name still needs registration.

## Paper only

You can't register online. Corporate Registries only accepts **original signed forms**, mailed or delivered in person, and not faxes or email. Include the name reservation form and its fee if you haven't reserved the name already.

## What it costs

A name reservation is **$25** and the registration is **$50**, so $75 in total. These fees are from a 2018 fee schedule, so confirm them with Corporate Registries before you pay.

## Renew every four years

The registration lasts **four years** and costs $50 to renew. An expired registration can't be renewed. You would have to reserve and register the name again.

## Keep it

Save the registration. You will need it for the bank account, your business licence and your supplier account.

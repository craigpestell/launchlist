## Structure

Start as a **sole proprietorship**. It is the cheapest and simplest option, and you can incorporate later if the business grows.

The trade-off is that you are **personally liable** for the business. The Northwest Territories' Corporate Registries describes a proprietorship as one where there is no legal distinction between you and the business. Insurance (step 6) is how you manage that risk.

## Name

- Only a business run under your **exact legal name** (for example "Jane Smith") is exempt from registration.
- Any other name, even "Jane Smith Designs", **must be registered** in the next step.

Search the Corporate Registries online system before you settle on a name. A basic search is free.

## Keep it practical

Choose a name you are comfortable seeing on a bank statement, a licence and a shipping label.

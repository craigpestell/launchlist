## State licenses

The PA Business One-Stop Shop says **there is no general business license that every Pennsylvania business needs** (checked November 2026; confirm on the site). Instead, some products and activities need their own state license. The One-Stop Shop has a business registration checklist and guides for types of business, such as clothing stores and homemade crafts.

## Township, borough and city rules

Licenses and local business taxes are set by each municipality, so **check with your township, borough or city** where you are based and where you sell. Some charge a business privilege or mercantile tax, and some need a permit for a home business. Almost every municipality also has a local earned income tax that covers your business profit. DCED's lookup shows your local tax collector by address. Ask an accountant what you need to file.

## Philadelphia

These rules apply only if you do business in Philadelphia. You need a **Commercial Activity License**, which has no cost (seen November 2026; confirm current fee), and a Business Income and Receipts Tax account. Every business with a license files a BIRT return each year, even with no profit. Sole proprietors and LLCs also pay the city's Net Profits Tax. Check the City of Philadelphia's pages or ask an accountant.

## Keep them

Save copies of any license or permit and note the renewal dates. Your insurer (step 7) may ask for them.

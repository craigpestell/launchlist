## The sales tax license

Pennsylvania's sales tax permit is called a **Sales, Use and Hotel Occupancy Tax License**. It comes from the Pennsylvania Department of Revenue, and you apply through the "Pennsylvania Online Business Tax Registration" in myPATH. New customers can register without logging in. Apply before you make taxable sales, even if you sell from home, online or at a few craft shows. The state's business portal says **there is no fee to apply (seen November 2026; confirm before you apply)**. The license renews every five years if your returns are up to date. Display it where you do business.

## The rate

The state rate is **6%**. Two places add a local tax: 1% in Allegheny County (7% total) and 2% in Philadelphia (8% total). There are no other local sales taxes. Most clothing and food that is not ready to eat are exempt, but digital products are taxable. Ask an accountant how the rates apply to what you sell.

## Filing

Your filing schedule depends on how much tax you collect. A small new seller often **files quarterly or twice a year**, with returns due on the 20th of the month after each period. You file a return even if you made no sales, and filing in myPATH is free. Filing and paying on time earns a small vendor discount.

## Buying stock to resell

Give your suppliers a completed **Pennsylvania Exemption Certificate, form REV-1220**, with your sales tax license number, so you do not pay sales tax on stock you resell. Do not use it for things you keep for the business. See step 13.

## Sellers outside Pennsylvania

Out-of-state sellers must register once they have **more than $100,000 in sales into Pennsylvania** in the previous calendar year (seen November 2026; confirm current threshold). For selling into other states yourself, see step 6.

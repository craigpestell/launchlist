## Reserve the name first

If you trade under a name other than your own legal name, you must first get the name approved with a **Name Request**. It costs $30, and an approved name is reserved for 56 days, so register within that time.

## Register online

Register a **sole proprietorship** through BC Registry Services. The registration fee is $40. You will need your legal name, address and the approved business name from your Name Request.

## Your address becomes public

Business registration details are public record. If you work from home and do not want strangers or customers to see your address, use a **PO box or a mailing address service** instead.

## Keep it

Save the confirmation. You will need it for the bank account, the municipal licence and your supplier account.

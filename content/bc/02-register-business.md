## Reserve the name first

If you trade under a name other than your own legal name, you need an approved **Name Request** before you register. It costs **$30**, and an approved name is held for 56 days, so register within that time. If you trade under just your own name, you need neither.

## Register online

Register a **sole proprietorship** through BC Registry Services. Registration costs $40. You will need your legal name, address and the approved name from your Name Request.

## Your address becomes public

Business registration details are public record. If you work from home and do not want strangers or customers to see your address, use a **PO box or a mailing address service** instead.

## Keep it

Save the confirmation. You will need it for the bank account, the municipal licence and your supplier account.

---
title: Check state and local licenses
summary: Find out whether your products or your city or town need a license or permit beyond your sales tax registration.
time: 30–60 minutes
cost: Varies by place and product (often none for a small online store)
checklist:
  - Ask your city or town clerk which local permits a business like yours needs
  - Check zoning and home business rules with your building or zoning office
  - Check whether what you sell, or where you sell it, needs its own license
  - Apply for any license you need before you start selling
  - Keep copies of any licenses and their renewal dates
---

## No general state license

Massachusetts does not appear to have one general business license for every business (checked November 2026; confirm with your city or town). Your business certificate from step 2 is a public record of your name, **not a license to operate**. Some products and activities need their own license, for example food, alcohol, or selling on the street or door to door.

## City and town rules

Cities and towns set their own rules, so **check with the clerk where your business is based** and anywhere you sell in person, such as a market or fair. Some towns ask for a zoning sign-off before they issue a business certificate, and some need a lease or a landlord letter. If you work from home, ask about home business and zoning rules.

## Keep them

Save copies of any license or permit and note the renewal dates. Your insurer (step 7) may ask for them.

## No registration needed

The province says it **does not have legislation governing a business name registry**. A sole proprietor has no business name to register, no fee and no renewal.

## When this changes

The Registry of Companies only registers corporations and some other entities. If you incorporate later, you will need a name approval and an incorporation through the registry.

## Keep a record

Write down the name you trade under and the date you started. Your bank, the CRA and suppliers may ask how the business is set up.

## Structure

Start as a **sole proprietorship**. It is the cheapest and simplest option, and you can incorporate later if the business grows.

The trade-off is that you are **personally liable** for the business. Creditors can take your personal assets to pay business debts. Insurance (step 6) is how you manage that risk.

## Name

Newfoundland and Labrador has **no business name registry**, so there is nowhere provincial to register or reserve a sole proprietorship's name. That also means nothing stops someone else using a similar name.

Before you commit, search the federal trademarks database and check that the domain name is free. Pick a name that is unique, easy to spell and easy to say.

## Keep it practical

Choose a name you are comfortable seeing on a bank statement, a licence and a shipping label.

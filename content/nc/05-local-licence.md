## State licenses

**North Carolina has no single state business license** that covers every business. Some products and activities need their own state license or permit. The Economic Development Partnership of North Carolina, which works with the state Department of Commerce, keeps a directory of licenses and offers free help from its Small Business Advisors.

## City and county rules

North Carolina cities and counties stopped charging general local business license taxes in 2015, but a **local permit or zoning approval may still apply**. The rules differ from place to place. If you work from home, ask your city or county planning or zoning office about home business rules. If you sell at a market or event, ask the organizer and the local office what is needed there.

## Keep them

Save copies of any license or permit and note the renewal dates. Your insurer (step 7) may ask for them.

## The Certificate of Registration

North Carolina's sales tax permit is a **Certificate of Registration**, issued by the North Carolina Department of Revenue. Anyone selling goods at retail in the state must register, including online sellers and vendors at flea markets, craft shows and other events. You can register online with your Social Security number or EIN (step 3). **There is no fee to apply (seen November 2026)**, and the Department warns about third-party sites that charge for this. Most online applicants get an account number at once, and the certificate is mailed within about 10 business days.

## The rate

The state rate is **4.75%**. Every county adds a local rate of 2% or 2.25%, a few add a 0.5% transit tax, and Mecklenburg County added another 1% from July 1, 2026. Totals run from 6.75% to 8.25% (seen November 2026; confirm current rates). Use the Department's county rate table for the counties you sell into. Qualifying food has its own lower rate. For orders you ship, ask an accountant or the Department which county's rate applies.

## Filing

The Department assigns your filing frequency. If your tax is **consistently under $100 a month, you file quarterly**, due by the last day of January, April, July and October. From $100 to under $20,000 a month, you file monthly by the 20th. You must file a return even if you owe nothing. Filing online is free.

## Buying stock to resell

Give your suppliers a completed **Form E-595E, Streamlined Sales and Use Tax Certificate of Exemption**, so you do not pay sales tax on stock you resell. North Carolina is a Streamlined Sales Tax member, so many suppliers in other states know this form. If you use the item yourself instead of reselling it, you owe use tax on it. See step 13.

## Sellers outside North Carolina

Out-of-state sellers must register once they have **more than $100,000 in gross sales** sourced to North Carolina in the current or previous calendar year (seen November 2026; confirm current threshold). For selling into other states yourself, see step 6.

## Who has to register

In Nunavut you register a business name when you trade under any name other than your own. You must file within **60 days** of first using the name.

## What it costs

Registration is **$50**. No name search is required. We could not confirm whether the registration has to be renewed, so ask Legal Registries when you file.

## Keep it

Save the confirmation. You will need it for the bank account, your business licence and your supplier account.

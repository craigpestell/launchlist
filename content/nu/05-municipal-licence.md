## Territorial licence

Nunavut requires a **territorial business licence**. It costs **$50** and runs for one fiscal year, from April 1 to March 31, so it has to be renewed every spring.

## Municipal licence

Your community may need its own licence as well. **Iqaluit** does. Its fee depends on how many hours a week you operate: $50 for a new licence under 30 hours a week, or $100 for 30–90 hours. Renewals cost half that. These fees date from 2018, so confirm them with the City.

## Home-based business

Check the zoning rules for your address before you start. Iqaluit's home business rules were not confirmed, so ask the City what applies to storing stock and shipping orders from home.

## Keep the licences

Save both licences. Banks and suppliers sometimes ask to see them.

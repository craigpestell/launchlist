## Territorial licence

Nunavut requires a **territorial business licence**. It costs **$50** and runs for one fiscal year, from April 1 to March 31, so it has to be renewed every spring.

## Municipal licence

Your community may need its own licence as well. **Iqaluit** does. Its fee depends on how many worker hours a week the business uses: $50 for a new licence under 30 hours, or $100 for 30–90 hours. A renewal costs half that if you file it by January 31, and the full fee after. These fees are from the City's Business Licensing Bylaw No. 859, passed in 2018 (seen November 2026). We could not find a newer fee schedule, so confirm current fees with the City before you apply.

## Home-based business

Check the zoning rules for your address before you start. Iqaluit's home business rules were not confirmed, so ask the City what applies to storing stock and shipping orders from home.

## Keep the licences

Save both licences. Banks and suppliers sometimes ask to see them.

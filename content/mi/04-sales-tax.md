## The sales tax license

You need a **sales tax license from the Michigan Department of Treasury** before you sell taxable goods. Selling without one is a misdemeanor. Register online through Michigan Treasury Online, or on paper with Form 518, which takes longer. No fee is listed for the license (seen November 2026; confirm when you apply). In some cases, mainly after unpaid taxes, Treasury can ask for a bond or deposit.

## The rate

The rate is **6% everywhere in Michigan**. Michigan has no county or city sales taxes, so you charge the same rate on every taxable sale delivered in the state. Ask an accountant which of your products are taxable.

## Filing

Treasury tells you whether to file monthly, quarterly or yearly, based on how much you expect to sell. Returns are **due on the 20th** of the month after the period ends. File a return even if you made no sales. Treasury's pages also describe a yearly return due February 28 for every filer, so check your filing calendar in Michigan Treasury Online (seen November 2026).

## Buying stock to resell

Give your suppliers a completed **Form 3372, Michigan Sales and Use Tax Certificate of Exemption**, marked for resale, so you do not pay sales tax on stock you resell. Include your sales tax license number. Do not use it for things you keep for the business. See step 13.

## Sellers outside Michigan

Out-of-state sellers must register once they have **more than $100,000 in sales, or 200 or more transactions,** into Michigan in the previous calendar year (seen November 2026; confirm current threshold). Michigan is a Streamlined Sales Tax member. For selling into other states yourself, see step 6.

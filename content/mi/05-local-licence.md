## State licenses

Michigan does not appear to have one general business license for every business (checked November 2026; confirm with the state). Your county assumed name certificate or LLC filing (step 2) and your sales tax license (step 4) cover most small shops. Some products and activities, for example food, alcohol or tobacco, need their own state license. **Check the rules for what you sell** before you start.

## City, township and village rules

Michigan is split into cities, townships and villages, and **each sets its own rules**. Some require a local business license or registration and some do not. Contact the clerk where your business is based, and anywhere you sell in person, and ask about:

- A local business license or registration
- Home business (home occupation) and zoning rules if you work from home
- Sign permits and permits for markets or pop-up events

Do not assume another city's rules apply to you.

## Keep them

Save copies of any license or permit and note the renewal dates. Your insurer (step 7) may ask for them.

## It depends on your municipality

We found no general Quebec business licence for a small retailer. Each municipality sets its own rules.

In **Montréal**, a **certificate of occupancy** is required to use part of your home for professional purposes, and each borough sets its own fee. In Le Plateau-Mont-Royal, for example, it costs $376. Ask your borough's permit counter whether an online store with no customer visits needs one before you start.

Look up your own municipality rather than assuming Montréal's answer applies to you.

## Be upfront about what you sell

Some products or business types carry extra by-laws or requirements, including food. Tell your municipality exactly what you will sell so you know before you start, not after.

## Keep the answer

Save your certificate. If the municipality confirms you do not need one, get that in writing, because banks and suppliers sometimes ask.

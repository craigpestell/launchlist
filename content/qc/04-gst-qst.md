## Two taxes, one registration

Quebec customers pay **5% GST** and **9.975% QST**, both worked out on the price before tax. Unlike in the rest of Canada, **Revenu Québec**, not the CRA, administers both, so you register once with Revenu Québec for the two.

## When you must register

You must register once your taxable sales pass **$30,000 in a single calendar quarter or over four consecutive calendar quarters**.

## Why register earlier

We recommend registering from the start. As a registrant you can **claim back the GST and QST you pay** on inventory and business expenses. Once you opt in, you must stay registered for at least a year.

## How to register

If you are new to Revenu Québec, use its **Register a New Business** online service, which also sets up your clicSÉQUR access. You can also apply on paper with form LM-1-V.

## Selling outside Quebec

The rate you charge depends on where the customer receives the goods, not where you are based. A customer in Ontario pays 13% HST, for example, and some provinces have their own sales taxes. Ask your accountant before you ship outside Quebec, or begin by selling to Quebec customers only.

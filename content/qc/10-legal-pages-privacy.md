## Pages you need

- **Privacy policy**
- **Terms of sale**
- **Shipping and returns policy**

## Privacy

Quebec has its own private-sector privacy law, the **Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector**, strengthened by **Law 25**. You are the person in charge of privacy unless you name someone else in writing, and you must publish that person's title and contact details and a plain-language privacy policy on your website. Customer data needs care, so:

- Collect only what you need.
- Never share it with advertising networks.
- Say clearly in your policy what you keep and for how long.

## Marketing email

Canada's anti-spam law, **CASL**, requires clear consent and an unsubscribe link in every marketing email.

## Consumer rules

Quebec's **Consumer Protection Act** has rules for distance contracts: you must show the required information before the customer buys, let them review and correct the order before paying, and send a copy of the contract within **15 days**. Your website and terms must also be available in **French**. Check the current requirements with the Office de la protection du consommateur or a lawyer before you publish your terms of sale.

## Structure

Start as a **sole proprietorship**. It is the cheapest and simplest option, and you can incorporate later if the business grows.

The trade-off is that you are **personally liable** for the business. Quebec's own guidance says a sole proprietorship has no legal existence separate from its owner. Insurance (step 6) is how you manage that risk.

## Name

- If you trade under a name that includes your first and last names (for example "Jane Tremblay"), you do not have to register, though you can choose to.
- If you use any other name, for example "Boutique Jane", you **must register** in the next step.

Under the Charter of the French language, your business name **must be in French**. It also must not be confusing with another business's name, so search the enterprise register before you settle on it.

## Keep it practical

Choose a name you are comfortable seeing on a bank statement, a licence and a shipping label.

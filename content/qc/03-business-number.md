## What it is

The **Business Number (BN)** is a nine-digit number the Canada Revenue Agency uses to identify your business. You use it for federal income tax. In Quebec your GST and QST accounts (step 4) are with Revenu Québec instead.

## Separate from your Quebec number

Registering with the Registraire des entreprises (step 2) gives you a Quebec enterprise number (NEQ). It does **not** replace the federal BN, so register with the CRA separately.

## How to get it

Register with the CRA using **Business Registration Online (BRO)**.

## Sign in with a CRA account

BRO is only available through your **CRA account**. Sign in first, then complete the registration online. If you don't have a CRA account yet, you can create one from the CRA sign-in page. You will need it later to file returns and make payments.

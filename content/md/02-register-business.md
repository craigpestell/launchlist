## Sole proprietor: SDAT number and trade name

Maryland Business Express says a sole proprietor needs an **SDAT identification number** to open a business bank account. You also need one if you hold business property or need a license, such as the trader's license in step 5. Apply on Maryland Business Express or with SDAT's paper Form 20. SDAT does not list a fee for it (confirm when you apply).

If you trade under a name other than your own, file a **trade name** with SDAT before you start. It is a state filing, not a county one, and it needs your SDAT number. The fee is **$25 (seen November 2026; confirm current fee)**, plus $50 if you want it expedited. A trade name lasts **5 years**, and you renew it in the last 6 months. SDAT checks the name only against other trade names.

## LLC: Articles of Organization

If you chose an LLC in step 1, file Articles of Organization with SDAT on Maryland Business Express or by mail ($100, seen November 2026; confirm current fee). Standard processing takes 6–8 weeks online and 4–6 weeks on paper. Expedited service is $50 more, and online card payments carry a 3% service fee. The articles name a resident agent and a principal office in Maryland, and both are public. An LLC trading under another name also files a trade name.

## Every year by April 15

- **LLC:** file the Annual Report (Form 1) with SDAT by **April 15**, with a **$300 fee** (seen November 2026; confirm current fee). It is due even if the LLC owns no property, and missing it can lead to the LLC being forfeited.
- **Sole proprietor:** if you hold business property, including stock, or need a license, file the business personal property return (Form 2) by April 15. SDAT lists no filing fee. If all your business property cost less than $20,000, it is exempt from the tax, but you still file each year.

You can get a 60-day extension online for either return. A sole proprietor pays no yearly fee for the name itself until the 5-year renewal. Ask an accountant about anything else that applies to you.

## Keep it

Save your SDAT number and the trade name acknowledgement. You will need them for the bank account (step 8), the trader's license (step 5) and your supplier account (step 13).

## The sales and use tax license

Maryland's sales tax permit is called a **sales and use tax license**. It comes from the Comptroller of Maryland, and you apply with the Combined Registration Application on Maryland Business Express or Maryland Tax Connect. Maryland law says you need it **before you start selling** as a retailer, and you need one for each place of business. It does not expire, so there is no renewal. No fee is listed on the pages we could read (confirm when you apply).

## The rate

The state rate is **6%**. We found no local sales taxes added by counties or cities on the official pages we read (confirm with the Comptroller). Some goods are taxed differently or exempt, so ask an accountant how the rate applies to what you sell.

## Filing

By default, returns are **due monthly, by the 20th of the next month**. The Comptroller can give you a less frequent schedule if you expect to owe under $100 a month. Check the schedule on your license. You file a return even when you owe no tax.

## Buying stock to resell

Maryland has no required resale form. A **resale certificate** in any format works if it shows your name, address, Maryland sales and use tax number and that the goods are for resale. Suppliers may refuse it for purchases under $200 unless the goods are delivered to your retail place of business. See step 13.

## Sellers outside Maryland

Out-of-state sellers must collect Maryland tax once they have **more than $100,000 in sales or 200 or more sales** delivered into Maryland in the current or previous calendar year (seen November 2026; confirm current threshold). For selling into other states yourself, see step 6.

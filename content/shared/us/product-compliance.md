## Product safety certificates

The **CPSC** sets safety rules for many consumer products. The manufacturer or importer must certify that products meet them: a **General Certificate of Conformity (GCC)** for general products, or a **Children's Product Certificate (CPC)** for products made for children, based on tests at a CPSC-accepted lab. As a reseller you aren't the certifier, but ask your supplier for the certificate. **If you import products yourself, you become the importer** and the certificate is your job.

## Cosmetics and food

Cosmetics fall under the FDA. The company named on the label has to list products and report serious problems, and makers must register their facilities. Food has its own FDA rules, and {region} and your local health department may add more. Ask your supplier what applies before you sell either.

## Claims

Only say **"Made in USA"** if all or virtually all of the product is made in the US. Don't repeat claims such as "non-toxic", "organic" or "BPA-free" unless your supplier can back them up in writing.

## Clothing and textiles

Most clothing and textile products need a label with **fiber content, country of origin and the maker or seller**, plus care instructions. Online listings must say where the item was made.

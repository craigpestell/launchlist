## What it is

The **Employer Identification Number (EIN)** is your business's nine-digit federal tax ID. You need one if you form an LLC, hire employees or file certain federal returns. A sole proprietor with no employees can often use their Social Security number instead, but most banks and suppliers ask for an EIN, and it keeps your SSN off invoices and forms.

## It is free

**The IRS never charges for an EIN.** Sites that ask for a fee are third parties filling in the same free form. Apply only on irs.gov.

## Applying online

The online application gives you the number straight away. It has to be finished in one go and **times out after 15 minutes** of inactivity, so have your details ready. Each responsible person can get only one EIN per day.

It is open Monday to Friday from 6 a.m. to 1 a.m. the next day, Saturday 6 a.m. to 9 p.m. and Sunday 6 p.m. to midnight, Eastern time (seen November 2026). You can also fax or mail Form SS-4, which takes days or weeks.

## Ignore BOI letters

US-formed companies no longer file a beneficial ownership (BOI) report with FinCEN (rule in effect August 2026). Treat any letter or site asking you to pay to file one as a scam.

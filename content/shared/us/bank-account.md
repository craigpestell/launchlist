## Why separate

Mixing business and personal money makes bookkeeping and taxes harder. **If you have an LLC, keeping its money separate helps protect your personal assets.** Even as a sole proprietor, one account for the business makes your Schedule C much easier.

## What the bank asks for

Usually your **EIN** (or Social Security number for a sole proprietor), photo ID, and the documents from {step:register-business}, such as your assumed name filing or your LLC formation filing. Some banks also ask for a business license. Ask the bank what it needs before you go.

## Fees to compare

Monthly fees, minimum balance rules, transaction limits, cash deposit fees and wire fees. Many banks and credit unions waive the monthly fee for small balances or online-only accounts.

## Connect your payment processor

Your payment processor ({step:payment-processor}) pays out to this account. Open it first.

## Pages you need

- **Privacy policy**
- **Terms of sale**
- **Shipping and returns policy**

## Shipping promises

The FTC's Mail Order Rule covers online orders. **Ship within the time you state, or within 30 days if you don't state one.** If you can't, tell the customer and offer to cancel with a prompt refund.

## Privacy

There is no single federal privacy law for online stores. Several states have their own. Some apply only above a size threshold, including to businesses outside the state, and some require any site that collects personal information to post a privacy policy. Check what {region} requires in {step:structure-and-name}. Whatever applies:

- Collect only what you need.
- Don't sell or share it with advertising networks without saying so.
- Say clearly in your policy what you keep and for how long.

## Marketing email

The **CAN-SPAM Act** covers every marketing email, including to businesses. Use honest subject lines, include your **valid postal address** (a PO box or registered private mailbox works), and give a clear way to unsubscribe. Honor opt-outs promptly.

## Reviews and influencers

If you give someone free product or pay them to post, they must disclose it clearly. Never post fake reviews.

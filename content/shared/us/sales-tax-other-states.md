## No federal sales tax

There is no national sales tax. Each state sets its own, and many cities and counties add local tax. Your {region} permit from {step:sales-tax} covers sales delivered in {region} only.

## Economic nexus

Since the Supreme Court's 2018 Wayfair decision, a state can make an out-of-state seller collect its sales tax once sales into that state pass a threshold, even if you have no office or stock there. **Each state sets its own threshold**, commonly a dollar amount of sales in the current or previous year. Thresholds change, so check each state's revenue website rather than a summary table.

## Marketplaces

Most states make marketplaces such as Amazon or Etsy collect and pay the tax on sales made through them. That doesn't cover sales on your own website, and some states still want you registered. Check how each state treats marketplace sales before you rule it out.

## One registration for many states

The **Streamlined Sales Tax** system lets you register in its 24 member states with one free online application. You still file a return with each state, even when you had no sales there. States outside the program have their own registration.

## Get help

Sales tax software can track thresholds for you. An accountant can tell you which states to register in and when.

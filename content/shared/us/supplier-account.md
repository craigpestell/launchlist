## What your supplier needs

Your business name, EIN and your **{region} sales tax permit number** from {step:sales-tax}.

## Resale certificate

You don't pay sales tax on stock you buy to resell. To show this, give the supplier a **resale certificate**: usually {region}'s own form. Suppliers in other states may accept the Multistate Tax Commission's uniform certificate or the Streamlined Sales Tax exemption certificate instead. Keep a copy of each one you give.

## Questions to ask

- What are the pricing, minimum order and shipping terms?
- Can you use the supplier's product images and descriptions?
- Is there a **minimum advertised price** you must follow?
- Is dropshipping available?
- Can you have the product safety certificates from {step:product-compliance}?

## Start small

Place a small test order to check the product, the packaging and the delivery time before you commit to a larger one.

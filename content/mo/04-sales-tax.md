## The retail sales tax license

Missouri's sales tax permit is called a **Retail Sales Tax License**. It comes from the Missouri Department of Revenue. Apply online through its business registration, or on Form 2643. You need it before you make retail sales, including online or at craft shows, and selling without one brings daily penalties. The Department's pages and Form 2643 do not list a fee (seen November 2026; confirm when you apply). Since August 28, 2018, a bond is no longer required unless the Department asks for one. Allow 2–3 business days online, or 10 business days on paper.

## The rate

The state rate is **4.225%**. Cities, counties and special taxing districts such as fire districts add their own local sales tax, so the total depends on the location. Use the Department of Revenue's sales and use tax lookup tool. Ask an accountant how rates apply to what you sell.

## Filing

The Department sets your filing frequency in your welcome letter, based on the state tax you collect. Most small sellers **file quarterly or annually**. Quarterly returns are due on the last day of the month after each calendar quarter, and annual returns are due January 31. You file a return even if you had no sales. A 2% timely payment allowance applies when you file and pay on time.

## Buying stock to resell

Give your suppliers a completed **Form 149, Sales and Use Tax Exemption Certificate**, ticking the box for purchases for resale and adding your Missouri Tax ID. Do not use it for things you keep for the business. See step 13.

## Sellers outside Missouri

Out-of-state sellers must register once they have **more than $100,000 in taxable sales** into Missouri over the past 12 months, checked at the end of each calendar quarter (seen November 2026; confirm current threshold). For selling into other states yourself, see step 6.

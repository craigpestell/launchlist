## State licenses

Missouri does not appear to have one general business license for every business (checked November 2026; confirm with the state). Instead, some professions and activities need their own state license. The **Division of Professional Registration** lists the licensed professions, and other state agencies license specific products.

## City and county licenses

Many Missouri cities and some counties **require their own business license**, and the rules differ from place to place. For example, Kansas City says every business operating in the city needs a city business license, sole proprietors included (check with the city). Check with the city or county where your business is based and where you sell, for example at a market or show. If you work from home, ask about home business and zoning rules.

## Certificate of No Tax Due

Missouri law says cities, counties and state agencies must see a **Certificate of No Tax Due** before issuing or renewing certain licenses. The Department of Revenue says it is free, and you print it online with your Missouri Tax ID and PIN from step 4.

## Keep them

Save copies of any license or permit and note the renewal dates. Your insurer (step 7) may ask for them.

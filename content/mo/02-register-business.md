## Sole proprietor: fictitious name registration

If you trade under a name other than your own, register a **fictitious name with the Missouri Secretary of State**. This is a state filing, not a county one, and no newspaper notice is required. You can file online through the Secretary of State's business services site, or mail or deliver Form Corp. 56. The form asks for the business name and address, each owner's name and share of ownership, and every owner's signature. The fee is **$7 (seen November 2026; confirm current fee)**. Not registering is a misdemeanor.

Your registration is public and shows your business address. If you work from home, think about which address you use.

## Renewing every five years

A fictitious name registration is **good for five years**. Renew it in the six months before it expires ($7, seen November 2026; confirm current fee). If it lapses, it cannot be renewed and you file a new registration instead.

## LLC: Articles of Organization

If you chose an LLC in step 1, file Articles of Organization with the Secretary of State ($50 online or $105 on paper, seen November 2026; confirm current fee). You name a registered agent with a Missouri street address. Missouri **LLCs do not file an annual report**, and no franchise tax or yearly LLC fee was found. An LLC trading under a different name also registers a fictitious name ($7).

A sole proprietor has no yearly state filing for the business beyond renewing the name every five years. Ask an accountant about anything else that applies to you.

## Keep it

Save the registration and note when it expires. You will need it for the bank account (step 8) and your supplier account (step 13).

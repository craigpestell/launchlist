# US Spanish glossary

Terms the Spanish guides must use, so every guide says the same thing the same way. Style rules (usted, infinitive titles, number format) are in the `writing-style` skill, section "US Spanish (es-US)". Add a term here whenever you settle on one.

Rules specific to translations:

- Keep `{step:<slug>}` and `{region}` tokens exactly; use `{in-region}` where Spanish needs the region with its preposition ("en Texas"), never "en {region}".
- `linkLabel` keeps the English pattern "Organization — page" with an em dash: "IRS — Solicitar un EIN en línea".
- Brand and product names stay as they are. Buttons and menus on an English-only site stay in English in quotes, with a Spanish gloss the first time: "Apply Online" (solicitar en línea).
- A state agency, program or law with no official Spanish name keeps its English name, with a Spanish explanation the first time: "Texas Comptroller of Public Accounts (la oficina de impuestos de Texas)".

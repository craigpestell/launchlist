## Who has to register

New Brunswick requires anyone who trades under a business name other than their own name to register it. If you sell as "Jane Smith", you can skip this step. If you sell as "Jane's Gift Shop", you **must register** within two months of starting.

## Name search first

Before you register, you need a **New Brunswick NUANS name search report**. Private name search firms sell these, and their prices vary. The report must be no more than 90 days old when the registry receives it.

## Register online

File a **Certificate of Business Name** through the Corporate Registry's online filing centre and attach your NUANS report. The fee is **$112**, which includes a $12 Royal Gazette publication fee.

## Renew every five years

The registration lasts **five years**. Renewing costs $62. Missing a renewal is an offence and can get your registration cancelled, so put the date in your calendar.

## Your address may be public

Business name registrations are a public record. If you work from home and do not want customers to see your address, ask the registry what a search shows before you file, and consider a PO box or a mailing address service.

## No general state license

**California does not issue a general state business license.** Your seller's permit (step 4) is a state registration, and some activities need a state license, for example selling food, alcohol or certain regulated products. CalGold, the state's permit assistance tool, lists the permits for your city and type of business. CalGold says it is still being built, so confirm with each agency it lists.

## City and county rules

Most cities ask businesses, including home businesses, to get a city business license or business tax certificate, but **rules and fees vary from place to place**. If you are outside city limits, your county may license you instead. Contact your city or county and ask about:

- A business license or business tax certificate
- Home business (home occupation) rules if you work from home
- Zoning and permits if you rent a shop
- Health permits if you sell food, and permits for markets or pop-up events

Some cities also want a license if you sell there but are based elsewhere. Do not assume another city's rules apply to you. A fictitious business name (step 2) is not a license.

## Keep track

Write down each license number and **when each one renews**, since cities and counties set their own renewal dates and fees.

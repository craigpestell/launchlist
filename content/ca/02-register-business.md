## Sole proprietorship: county clerk

If you trade under a name that does not include your surname, file a **fictitious business name statement with the county clerk** of the county where your business is based. There is no statewide registry. Fees are set by each county, for example $35 for the first name in Fresno County (seen November 2026; confirm current fee), so check with your county clerk. Fresno County says to file within 40 days of starting business.

After filing, you usually have to **publish the statement in a newspaper** in your county once a week for four weeks in a row, starting within 30 days of filing. Check your county's deadline and keep the proof of publication. The statement lasts five years and then needs to be renewed.

## Your address becomes public

The statement lists your **residential address**, and it is public record. Counties do not accept a PO box in its place. If you work from home, keep this in mind when you choose a name.

## LLC: Secretary of State

You form an LLC by filing Articles of Organization (Form LLC-1) with the California Secretary of State, online through bizfileOnline. The fee is **$70 (seen November 2026; confirm current fee)**, and online filings usually take five days or less. You name an agent for service of process in California. If the LLC trades under a name other than its legal name, it files a fictitious business name with the county too.

## Yearly costs for an LLC

- **Statement of Information:** $20, due within 90 days of forming the LLC and every two years after. Missing it can bring a $250 penalty (seen November 2026; confirm current amounts).
- **Annual LLC tax:** $800 to the Franchise Tax Board every year, with the first payment due by the 15th day of the 4th month after you form the LLC (Form FTB 3522). It is owed even if the LLC makes no sales, until you cancel it.
- **LLC fee:** an extra fee starts at $900 once the LLC's California income reaches $250,000 (seen November 2026; confirm current amounts).

Ask an accountant to confirm what your LLC files. A sole proprietorship has **no yearly state filing fee**, only your income tax and sales tax returns (step 4).

## The permit

You need a **seller's permit** from the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration (CDTFA) if you sell goods in California, including online. The permit is **free (seen November 2026; confirm current fee)**, but the CDTFA may ask for a security deposit depending on your type of business and expected sales. Apply online, and you may get your permit the same day. If you only sell through a marketplace such as Etsy or Amazon, check the CDTFA's rules for marketplace sellers; sales on your own website need a permit.

## The rate

The statewide rate is **7.25%**. Many cities and counties add district taxes of 0.10% to 2.00%, and some areas have more than one (seen November 2026; district rates change, so check the current rate). Use the CDTFA's "Find a Sales and Use Tax Rate by Address" tool to find the rate for an address. Ask the CDTFA or an accountant which district taxes apply to goods you ship to other parts of California.

## Filing

The CDTFA assigns you a filing schedule, usually quarterly, monthly or yearly, based on your expected sales. Quarterly returns are due April 30, July 31, October 31 and January 31. **File a return even if you made no sales.**

## Buying stock to resell

Give your suppliers a completed **CDTFA-230 General Resale Certificate** so you do not pay sales tax on stock you resell. Only use it for goods you will resell; misusing it carries penalties. See step 13.

## Sellers outside California

Sellers outside California must register and collect California use tax once their **sales into California exceed $500,000** in the current or previous calendar year. For selling into other states yourself, see step 6.

## City and county business licenses

Many Georgia cities and counties charge a **local business license, often called an occupation tax certificate**. If your business is inside city limits, ask the city. If it is outside any city, ask the county. Each place sets its own rules, fees and renewal dates, and some base the tax on your gross receipts or number of employees. Ask where your business is based, and where you sell in person, for example at a market or show.

## Working from home

Many cities and counties have **home business or zoning rules** that apply even to an online store run from your home. Ask your local planning or zoning office when you apply for the business license.

## State licenses

Georgia does not appear to have one general state business license for every business (checked November 2026; confirm with the Secretary of State). Instead, **some products and trades need their own state license**, for example alcohol. Georgia.gov points to the Secretary of State's licensing information to check what applies to you.

## Keep them

Save copies of any license or certificate and note the renewal dates. Your insurer (step 7) may ask for them.

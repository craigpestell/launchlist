## Who needs it

Common reasons are a web host or payment provider that only allows logins from known addresses, a firewall rule, or technical support checking why a connection fails. Share it with them, not in a public post or review.

## It can change

Most home and small business internet plans have a **dynamic address**. It can change when your modem restarts or after a power outage. If a login or allowlist that used to work stops working, look the address up again and send the new one. Your internet provider can usually sell you a static address if you need one that never changes.

## Is it private

An IP address can show your approximate area and your internet provider, but not your name or street address. Still, treat it like a phone number: fine to give to a provider you trust, not something to post publicly.

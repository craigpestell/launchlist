---
title: Find your browser and its version
summary: Support often asks which browser you use, because a site can work in one and break in another.
time: 2 minutes
cost: Free
checklist:
  - Open the browser where you saw the problem
  - Open its About page from the browser's menu
  - Note the browser's name and the version number it shows
  - Install any update it offers, then restart the browser
  - Note your device and its system version (Windows, macOS, iOS or Android)
  - Send the browser, version and device to support
---

## Start in the browser with the problem

Open the browser where the site isn't working. If you use more than one, check the one you saw the problem in. The browser's **About** page shows its exact version and checks for updates at the same time. The version is a number with dots, like 140.0.1234.56. Copy the whole thing.

## Chrome, Edge and Firefox on a computer

In Chrome, click the three dots at the top right, then **Help** and **About Google Chrome**. In Edge, click the three dots, then **Help and feedback** and **About Microsoft Edge**. In Firefox, click the three lines at the top right, then **Help** and **About Firefox**. On a Mac, Firefox's About page is also in the **Firefox** menu at the top left of the screen.

## Safari on a Mac

Open Safari, click the **Safari** menu at the top left of the screen, then **About Safari**. Safari updates come with macOS updates, in **System Settings**, **General**, **Software Update**.

## iPhone, iPad and Android

On an iPhone or iPad, Safari's version follows the system version. Open the **Settings** app, then **General** and **About**, and note the **iOS Version** (or iPadOS Version). If you use Chrome on an iPhone, tap the three dots, then **Settings** and **Google Chrome**.

On Android, open Chrome, tap the three dots, then **Settings** and **About Chrome**. To find the Android version, open the **Settings** app and search for **Android version**.

## Your device too

Support may also ask for your system. On Windows, open **Settings**, then **System** and **About**, and note the edition and version under Windows specifications. On a Mac, click the Apple menu at the top left and choose **About This Mac**. **If an update was waiting, install it and try the site again first**, since that fixes many problems on its own.

## What the wallet does

The Gravatar wallet lists your payment links on your public profile under a **Send money** button. It does not take payments itself. Each link sends donors to your provider.

## PayPal.me

Choose **PayPal.me** and enter only your username, not the full link.

## Ko-fi

Ko-fi is not in Gravatar's list, so choose **Add custom payment**. Name it "Ko-fi" and paste your full page link, starting with `https://`.

## Where else to share it

Your Gravatar profile shows on sites that use Gravatar, such as WordPress. Add the same link to your website and social profiles so donors find it wherever they meet you.

## Your page name

Your page name becomes your link, `ko-fi.com/yourname`. Use the same name as your other profiles so donors know it is you.

## Payouts

Ko-fi does not hold your money. **Donations go to the Stripe or PayPal account you connect**, and Stripe pays out to your bank.

## Keep it simple

Ko-fi also offers memberships, shop items and goals. Leave them off until one-off donations are working.

## Taxes

Donations to a person can still count as income. Ask an accountant if you are not sure how to report them.

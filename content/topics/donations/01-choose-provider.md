## Start with one

One clear donation link gets more use than five. Pick one now and add others only when donors ask for them.

## The two good options in Canada

**PayPal.me** (recommended) gives you a short link like `paypal.me/yourname`. Most people already have PayPal or can pay with a card, and money moves to a Canadian bank.

**Ko-fi** gives you a small page like `ko-fi.com/yourname` with a donate button. It pays out through Stripe or PayPal, so it works if you would rather not open a PayPal account.

## Options to skip for now

**Venmo works only in the US.** Patreon suits monthly memberships rather than one-off donations. Crypto addresses work, but few donors use them and the value moves around.

## Fees

Every provider takes a processing fee, and some add a platform fee. Fees change, so check each provider's current fee page before you choose.

  - Note your PayPal.me username

## Personal or Business

A **Personal** account is fine for donations. Choose Business only if you are collecting for a registered business and want its name on the account.

## Your bank

Linking a bank lets you move donations out of PayPal. PayPal confirms the account with small deposits, which can take a few days.

## Your username

**Your PayPal.me username is hard to change later.** Use your own name or the name people already know you by.

## Taxes

Donations to a person can still count as income. Ask an accountant if you are not sure how to report them.

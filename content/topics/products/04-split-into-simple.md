## Pick the product

Open the **Split** tab and search for the variable product, or use the **Split into simple products** link under it in **Products > All Products**.

## Check the names

You get one row per variation with a suggested product name, like "Classic Tee Black". Edit any name that should read differently and untick variations you don't want as separate products.

## Split it

Click **Split into 2 simple products** (the number matches the variations you checked). Each checked variation becomes a simple product with its SKU, prices, stock, weight, dimensions and image, plus the variable product's descriptions, categories and tags. The variable product is hidden, not deleted.

A variable product that Variation Converter made by combining offers **Undo** instead of a split, so you get your original products back rather than new copies.

## Back up first

Variation Converter hides products instead of deleting them, and every change can be undone. A backup is still the safe habit before changing many products at once. Most hosts offer a one-click backup or snapshot.

## Install the plugin

Download the plugin zip from the Variation Converter site. In your WordPress admin, go to **Plugins > Add New Plugin > Upload Plugin**, choose the zip, then click **Install Now** and **Activate**.

You need WooCommerce 8.0 or later.

## Find it

The plugin adds **Products > Variation Converter**, with three tabs: **Combine**, **Split** and **History**. Opening Combine scans your published simple products. Nothing changes until you approve something.

---
title: Allow pop-ups or notifications for a site
summary: Some sites need a pop-up window for a payment, sign-in or download, or need permission to send you alerts.
time: 5 minutes
cost: Free
checklist:
  - Open the site you trust in the browser you normally use
  - Click the icon at either end of the web address to see the site's permissions
  - Set pop-ups or notifications to Allow for this site only
  - Reload the page and try again
  - Check your computer or phone allows notifications from the browser itself
---

## Start on the site

Open the website that needs the pop-up or notifications. When a browser blocks a pop-up, it usually shows a small icon or bar near the web address. **Only allow sites you know and trust.** Scam sites often ask to send notifications so they can show fake warnings later.

## Chrome and Edge on a computer

If Chrome blocked a pop-up, click the blocked pop-up icon at the right end of the address bar, choose **Always allow pop-ups and redirects** from the site, and click **Done**. For notifications, or if you don't see that icon, click the icon left of the web address, then **Site settings**, and set **Pop-ups and redirects** or **Notifications** to **Allow**. Edge works the same way: click the icon left of the web address, then **Permissions for this site**.

## Firefox and Safari on a computer

In Firefox, a yellow bar appears under the address bar when it blocks a pop-up. Click **Options** (or **Preferences**) in that bar, then **Allow pop-ups for** the site. If you blocked notifications earlier, click the permissions icon left of the web address and clear the blocked setting, then reload.

In Safari on a Mac, open the site, click the **Safari** menu, then **Settings for** the site, and set **Pop-up Windows** to **Allow**. For notifications, open **Safari**, **Settings**, **Websites**, then **Notifications**, and set the site to **Allow**.

## iPhone, iPad and Android

On an iPhone or iPad, Safari can only allow pop-ups for every site at once: open the **Settings** app, then **Apps**, **Safari**, and turn off **Block Pop-ups**. Turn it back on when you're done. Websites can only send notifications once you add them to your Home Screen: tap the share icon, then **Add to Home Screen**, open the site from its new icon and allow notifications when it asks.

In Chrome on Android, open the site, tap the icon left of the web address, then **Permissions** or **Site settings**, and allow **Pop-ups and redirects** or **Notifications**.

## Still no notifications?

Your device must also allow the browser to show notifications. On a Mac, open **System Settings**, then **Notifications**, and check your browser is allowed. On Windows, open **Settings**, then **System** and **Notifications**. On a phone, check the browser or Home Screen app in the phone's notification settings. Focus or Do Not Disturb modes can also hide them.

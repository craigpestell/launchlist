---
title: Clear cache and cookies for one site
summary: Delete one website's saved data so it loads fresh, while every other site stays signed in.
time: 5 minutes
cost: Free
checklist:
  - Open the website that isn't working in the browser you normally use
  - Reload the page without the cache (Ctrl+Shift+R, or Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac) and check again
  - Open the site's settings from the icon to the left of the web address
  - Delete the cookies and site data for that one site
  - Close the tab, open the site again and sign back in
  - Tell support which browser and device you used if it still doesn't work
---

## Start on the site

Open the website that isn't working, in the browser and on the device where you saw the problem. **Clear only that one site**, not your whole browsing history. Clearing everything signs you out of every website you use.

Try a hard reload first. Press **Ctrl+Shift+R** on Windows or a Chromebook, or **Cmd+Shift+R** on a Mac. This fetches fresh copies of the page and is often enough to see a site's latest changes.

## Chrome and Edge on a computer

Click the icon just left of the web address (it looks like two sliders, or a padlock). In Chrome, click **Site settings**, then **Delete data** and confirm. In Edge, open **Settings** from the three dots menu, search the settings for **cookies** and open **See all cookies and site data**. Search for the site and click the bin icon beside it.

## Safari and Firefox on a computer

In Firefox, click the padlock left of the web address, then **Clear cookies and site data** and confirm. In Safari on a Mac, open the **Safari** menu, then **Settings**, **Privacy** and **Manage Website Data**. Search for the site's name, select it and click **Remove**.

## iPhone, iPad and Android

On an iPhone or iPad, open the **Settings** app, then **Apps**, **Safari**, **Advanced** and **Website Data**. Search for the site, swipe left on it and tap **Delete**. On older iOS versions, Safari is in the main Settings list instead of under Apps.

In Chrome on Android, open the site, tap the icon left of the web address, then **Cookies and site data**, and tap the bin icon. If you don't see it, open Chrome's **Settings**, then **Site settings** and **All sites**, choose the site and tap **Delete & reset**.

## After you clear it

The site signs you out and forgets anything saved in it, like a cart or a remembered setting. Sign in again and check the problem. If it is still there, tell support which browser and device you used, and what you see.

  - Open the page or app with the problem so it is on your screen
  - Take a screenshot with your device's keys, or record the screen if it takes several steps to show
  - Find the image or video where your device saved it
  - Check it for passwords, card numbers or other private details and crop them out
  - Attach the file to your email or support message, with the web address and the time it happened

## Get the problem on screen

Open the page or app where the problem happens, so the error message or the broken part is showing. **Capture the whole browser window, including the web address**, unless it shows something private. Support can then see which page you were on.

A screenshot is enough for an error message. Record the screen when the problem only shows over several steps, such as a checkout that fails after you click Pay.

## Windows and Mac

On **Windows**, press **Windows key + Shift + S**, then drag over the part of the screen you want. The shot is copied, so you can paste it into an email, and Snipping Tool saves it in Pictures, then Screenshots. To record, open **Snipping Tool** and choose the video camera button.

On a **Mac**, press **Cmd+Shift+4** and drag over an area, or **Cmd+Shift+3** for the whole screen. Press **Cmd+Shift+5** to record the screen. Screenshots and recordings save to your Desktop.

On a **Chromebook**, press **Ctrl + Show windows** (the key with stacked rectangles). Choose screenshot or screen record in the bar that opens. Files save to Downloads.

## iPhone and Android

On an **iPhone**, press the side button and the volume up button at the same time. On an older iPhone with a Home button, press the side or top button and the Home button. To record, open Control Centre and tap the Screen Recording button. Shots and recordings save to the Photos app.

On **Android**, press the power button and the volume down button at the same time. To record, swipe down twice from the top of the screen and tap **Screen record**. If you don't see it, tap the pencil icon to add it. Files save to your Photos or Gallery app.

## Before you send it

Look over the image before you send it. Crop out or cover passwords, card numbers and anything you would not want others to read. Then attach the file to your email or support message, and add the web address, roughly when it happened, and what you clicked just before. Long recordings can be too big to email, so keep them under a minute.

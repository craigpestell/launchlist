checklist:
  - Add the number to your website's contact page and footer
  - Update your Google Business Profile and social media profiles
  - Add it to your email signature, invoices and business cards
  - Set a calendar reminder to use the number regularly so it stays active
---

## Everywhere customers look

Customers copy your number from wherever they found you. Update every listing at once so an old number doesn't linger on one of them.

## Make it tappable

On your website, link the number with `tel:` and the country code, for example `tel:+16045550142`. Phones then dial it with one tap.

## Keep it working

Both free services can take back a number that goes unused (step 2 and step 3). **Set a repeating reminder** to make a call or send a text from it, and check now and then that it still rings.

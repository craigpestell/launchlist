## Why a separate number

A business number keeps your personal cell private. It goes on your website, invoices and listings, and you can change how it rings without changing the number customers already have.

## The two free options in Canada

**Fongo Works** (recommended) is a free business phone system from a Canadian company. It forwards calls to your cell for free and adds a voicemail and a greeting. Texting needs its paid Pro plan.

**TextNow** is a free calling and texting app. Calls and texts ring inside the app on your phone over Wi-Fi or mobile data. The free plan shows ads, and you must use the number often or it can be taken back.

## Options to skip

**Skype is gone.** Microsoft retired it in May 2025, and Skype Numbers ended in May 2026. Google Voice is for US users only.

## Prices change

Both services are free today, with paid upgrades. Check each provider's current plan page before you choose.

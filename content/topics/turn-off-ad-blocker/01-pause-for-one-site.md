---
title: Turn off ad blockers or extensions for one site
summary: Ad blockers and other extensions can stop a checkout, form or sign-in from working without showing an error.
time: 5 minutes
cost: Free
checklist:
  - Open the site that isn't working in a private or incognito window to test it
  - Find your ad blocker's icon beside the web address, or under the puzzle piece icon
  - Turn the blocker off for this site only, not everywhere
  - Reload the page and try the checkout, form or sign-in again
  - Pause other extensions one at a time if it still fails
  - Tell support which extension caused it if you find one
---

## Test in a private window first

Open the site in a private window: in Chrome, click the three dots and choose **New Incognito window**. In Edge, it's **New InPrivate window**, and in Firefox and Safari, **New private window**. Extensions usually don't run there. **If the site works in a private window, an extension is the likely cause.** If it still fails, the problem is somewhere else, so tell the site's support.

## Chrome, Edge and Firefox on a computer

Go back to a normal window on the site. Look for your ad blocker's icon at the right end of the address bar. If you don't see it, click the puzzle piece icon to list your extensions. Click the blocker's icon and choose the option to pause or turn it off **on this site** (the wording differs by extension, for example "Don't run on pages on this site" or a power button). Then reload the page.

Firefox also has its own tracking protection. Click the shield icon left of the web address and switch off **Enhanced Tracking Protection** for that site. Brave has the same kind of switch under the lion icon, called Shields.

## Safari on a Mac, iPhone and iPad

On a Mac, open the site, click the **Safari** menu, then **Settings for** the site, and untick **Enable content blockers**. On an iPhone or iPad, tap the page menu icon beside the web address and choose **Turn Off Content Blockers**. Some blockers are separate apps; open the app if you don't see that option.

## It isn't always an ad blocker

Other extensions can break a page too: coupon finders, password managers, translators and privacy tools. Pause them one at a time and reload after each, so you know which one caused it. Some ad blocking happens outside the browser, in a VPN app, antivirus or your home network. Pause those the same way if no extension is to blame.

## Afterwards

The setting stays for that site only, so other sites keep blocking ads. If the site still doesn't work, see the Clear cache and cookies for one site guide, then contact support.

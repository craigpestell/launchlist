## Who has to register

Yukon requires you to register a business name unless you trade under your own given name.

## Reserve, then register

First **reserve the name**. A reservation costs $40 online or $60 on paper. Then file a **declaration of business name**, which costs **$25** and lasts **three years**. Renewing it costs another $25.

## Keep it

Save the registration. You will need it for the bank account, your City licence and your supplier account.

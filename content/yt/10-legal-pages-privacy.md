## Pages you need

- **Privacy policy**
- **Terms of sale**
- **Shipping and returns policy**

## Privacy

You fall under Canada's privacy law, **PIPEDA**. Customer data needs care, so:

- Collect only what you need.
- Never share it with advertising networks.
- Say clearly in your policy what you keep and for how long.

## Marketing email

Canada's anti-spam law, **CASL**, requires clear consent and an unsubscribe link in every marketing email.

## Consumer rules

Yukon's **Consumers Protection Act** may also apply to your sales. Check the current requirements with the Yukon government or a lawyer before you publish your terms of sale.

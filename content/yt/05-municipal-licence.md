## It depends on your municipality

We found no general Yukon business licence for a small retailer. Each community decides what it licenses.

In **Whitehorse**, a business licence costs **$176 for 12 months**, and a home-based business also needs a development permit. The City's rules don't allow retail sales at the lowest home-business level, so ask the City whether online sales shipped from home are allowed before you start.

Look up your own community rather than assuming Whitehorse's answer applies to you.

## Be upfront about what you sell

Some products or business types carry extra by-laws or requirements, including food. Tell your municipality exactly what you will sell so you know before you start, not after.

## Keep the licence

Save it. Banks and suppliers sometimes ask to see it.

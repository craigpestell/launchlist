## City endorsements

Many Washington cities license businesses through the state. If your business is located in, or travels to, a city on the **Department of Revenue's city endorsements list**, you add that city's endorsement to your Business License Application (step 4). Each city sets its own rules and fees, so check the city's entry. A license with endorsements renews every year.

## Cities with their own systems

Some cities, including Seattle, Tacoma, Bellevue and Everett, issue their own city business license through **FileLocal**, a portal those cities share. If your city is on neither list, contact the city directly. Many cities also charge **their own B&O tax**, separate from the state's, so ask your city whether you need to file one. These are local rules, so check the city where you are based and any city where you sell, for example at a market.

## Counties and zoning

County endorsements are rare. The Department of Revenue lists them only for the unincorporated areas of Asotin and Franklin counties (checked November 2026). If you work from home, ask your city or county about **home business and zoning rules**. Some products need their own state license or endorsement, and the Department of Revenue's Business Licensing Wizard can show which apply.

## Keep them

Save copies of any license or endorsement and note the renewal dates. Your insurer (step 7) may ask for them.

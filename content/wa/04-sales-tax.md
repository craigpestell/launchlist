## The business license and tax registration

Washington has **no separate sales tax permit**. You register for sales tax by filing a Business License Application with the Department of Revenue's Business Licensing Service, online through My DOR or by mail. The tax registration itself has no fee, but the application has a processing fee of $50 to open a new business (seen November 2026; confirm current fee). You need a license if you collect sales tax, use a trade name or earn $12,000 or more a year, among other reasons. You get a 9-digit UBI number, which is your account number with the state. Online applications take about 10 business days, longer if you add city endorsements.

## The rate

The state rate is **6.5%**. Cities and counties add their own local rate. You charge the rate for the place where your customer receives the goods, so use the Department of Revenue's tax rate lookup by address. Ask an accountant how rates apply to what you sell.

## Filing and B&O tax

You report sales tax and **B&O tax on one excise tax return**, filed online. Your welcome letter tells you whether to file monthly, quarterly or yearly, based on how much tax you expect to owe. B&O is a tax on your gross receipts, with no deduction for costs. The retailing rate is 0.471% (seen November 2026; confirm current rate). A small business credit reduces or removes it when the amount owed is small, and it is worked out for you when you file. Ask an accountant how B&O applies to you.

## Buying stock to resell

Washington uses a **reseller permit** instead of a resale certificate form. Apply for it in My DOR once you have a business license. No fee is listed for it (seen November 2026; confirm when you apply). Give a copy to suppliers so you do not pay sales tax on stock you resell. Do not use it for things you keep for the business. A permit usually lasts four years, or two years if your business is less than 12 months old. See step 13.

## Sellers outside Washington

Out-of-state sellers must register once they have **more than $100,000 in gross receipts** from Washington in the current or previous calendar year (seen November 2026; confirm current threshold). There is no transaction count. For selling into other states yourself, see step 6.

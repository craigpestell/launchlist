## Sole proprietorship: trade name

In Washington, a trade name is **registered with the Department of Revenue, not a county**. You add it to the Business License Application (step 4) through the Business Licensing Service. The fee is $5 for each trade name (seen November 2026; confirm current fee), plus the application's processing fee. A trade name stays registered until you cancel it, and there is no newspaper notice to publish.

## LLC: Secretary of State

You form an LLC by filing a Certificate of Formation with the Washington Secretary of State, online or by mail. The fee is **$180 (seen November 2026; confirm current fee)**, plus a processing fee online. File the initial report with it, which then costs nothing. Filed later, the initial report costs $10 and is due within 120 days (seen November 2026; confirm current fee). You must name a registered agent with a Washington address.

After the LLC is formed, wait one business day, then file the Business License Application (step 4) so the LLC can trade. If the LLC trades under a name other than its legal name, register that as a trade name too.

## Yearly reports and taxes

- An LLC files an **annual report with the Secretary of State by the last day of the month it was formed**, every year. The fee is $70 (seen November 2026; confirm current fee), with a delinquency fee if it is late. If you don't file, the state can dissolve the LLC.
- A sole proprietor files **no yearly name or report with the Secretary of State**. Your trade name does not expire.
- Both a sole proprietor and an LLC file excise tax returns with the Department of Revenue for sales tax and B&O tax (step 4). A business license with city endorsements also renews every year (step 5).

Ask an accountant to confirm what your business files.

## It depends on your municipality

We found no general PEI business licence for a small retailer. Each municipality sets its own rules.

In **Charlottetown**, a home-based business needs a **home occupation application**, which costs $50 with no renovations. The business can use up to a quarter of your home's floor area. Retail stores may not be allowed as a home occupation, so ask the Planning & Heritage Department whether online sales shipped from home are allowed before you start.

Look up your own municipality rather than assuming Charlottetown's answer applies to you.

## Be upfront about what you sell

Some products or business types carry extra by-laws or requirements, including food. Tell your municipality exactly what you will sell so you know before you start, not after.

## Keep the answer

Save your permit. If the municipality confirms you do not need one, get that in writing, because banks and suppliers sometimes ask.

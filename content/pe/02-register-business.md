## Who has to register

Prince Edward Island requires you to register any business name other than your own name.

## Name approval first

Request approval in the **Online Corporate and Business Registry**. It costs **$40** for an Atlantic Canada search, or $50 Canada-wide. The registry orders the NUANS name search itself and emails you the result.

## Register online

Once the name is approved, you receive a company PIN and key by email. Use them to register the sole proprietorship, which costs **$90**. The certificate is emailed to you.

## Renew every three years

The registration lasts **three years**, and renewing costs $75. There is a six-month grace period, after which the registration becomes inactive.

## Keep it

Save the certificate. You will need it for the bank account and your supplier account.

## Why this helps

If the sign-in page reloads and nothing else happens, you may be looking at an **out-of-date saved copy** of the page. Each sign-in form carries a short-lived security code. When the saved copy is more than a day old, that code has expired, so the site refuses it and shows the same form again, with no message.

Clearing your cookies does not fix this, because you would just be sent the same saved copy again.

## The fresh link

Use this address: `https://ultralove.ca/my-account/?fresh=1`

The `?fresh=1` on the end asks the site for a new copy of the page instead of the saved one. It does not change your account or log anything. If it works, bookmark the page you land on after signing in and use that instead.

## If it still reloads

Carry on to step 2. Keep in mind that deleting cookies signs you out everywhere on ultralove.ca, so have your password ready.

## What to include

- **Your account email.** The one you use to sign in.
- **Your device and browser.** For example, "Chrome on Windows 11" or "Safari on iPhone".
- **What you tried.** Say whether you deleted cookies, tried a private window, or reset your password, and what happened each time.
- **A screenshot.** Show the page after it refreshes, including any message and the address in the address bar.

## What happens next

Ultralove can check your account and the sign-in records on our side. The details above let us tell a browser problem from an account problem without back-and-forth.

**Never send your password.** We will not ask for it.

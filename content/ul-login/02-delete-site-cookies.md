## Why this helps

When you click Log in and the page simply refreshes, your browser is usually holding an old or broken sign-in cookie. The site cannot start a new session, so it sends you back to the same page. Deleting the cookies for ultralove.ca gives you a clean start.

**Delete only the cookies for ultralove.ca.** You do not need to clear everything, and you will stay signed in everywhere else.

## Chrome and Edge (computer)

1. Click the icon at the left of the address bar while on ultralove.ca.
2. Choose **Cookies and site data**, then **Manage on-device site data**.
3. Find ultralove.ca and click the bin icon beside it.

If you cannot find that icon, open Settings, search for "site data", choose **See all site data and permissions**, search for `ultralove.ca` and delete it.

## Safari (Mac)

1. Open Safari, then **Settings**, then **Privacy**.
2. Click **Manage Website Data** and search for `ultralove`.
3. Select the entries and click **Remove**, then **Done**.

## Firefox (computer)

1. Open **Settings**, then **Privacy & Security**.
2. Under **Cookies and Site Data**, click **Manage Data**.
3. Search for `ultralove.ca`, select the entries and click **Remove Selected**, then **Save Changes**.

## iPhone, iPad and Android

- **iPhone or iPad:** open Settings, then Apps, then Safari, then Advanced, then Website Data. Search for `ultralove` and swipe to delete. On older iOS versions, Safari is directly in Settings.
- **Android (Chrome):** open Chrome, tap the three dots, then Settings, then Site settings, then All sites. Search for `ultralove.ca`, tap it and choose **Clear & reset**.

Menu names change between browser versions. If yours looks different, search your browser's settings for "cookies".

## Why this helps

A private window starts with no saved cookies and, in most browsers, with extensions switched off. If sign-in works there, the problem is in your normal browser, not your account.

## How to open one

- **Chrome and Edge:** press Ctrl+Shift+N (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+N (Mac).
- **Safari:** choose File, then New Private Window.
- **Firefox:** press Ctrl+Shift+P (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+P (Mac).
- **Phone:** use the tab switcher and choose a private tab.

## What the result means

- **It works in the private window.** Go back to step 2 and make sure the cookies were deleted for ultralove.ca, then continue to step 4 to find the extension or setting responsible.
- **It still refreshes in the private window.** Your browser settings are probably fine. Try a different browser or device, then go to step 5.

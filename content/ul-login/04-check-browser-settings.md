  - Make sure your browser is not set to block all cookies

  - Check your computer's date and time are correct
  - Try sign-in again

## Cookies must be allowed

Signing in needs the browser to save a cookie from ultralove.ca. If your browser is set to **block all cookies**, the page will refresh every time. Standard settings are fine. Strict or "block all" settings are not.

In your browser's settings, search for "cookies" and look for an option to allow cookies for a specific site. Add `ultralove.ca`.

## Extensions and content blockers

Ad blockers, privacy tools and some antivirus browser add-ons can strip or block the cookie. Pause them for ultralove.ca, or turn them off for a test, then reload the page and try again.

## Date and time

If your computer's clock is wrong by a long way, the browser can treat a new cookie as already expired. Set the date and time to update automatically.

## Still stuck?

Turn your extensions back on if the test changed nothing. Then continue to step 5.

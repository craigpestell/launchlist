## Who has to register

Nova Scotia says **most sole proprietorships must register** with the Registry of Joint Stock Companies. You are exempt only if you trade under just your personal name.

## Reserve the name first

The registry must approve your name before you register. A name reservation with an Atlantic name search costs **$60.52**, or $75.58 if you plan to trade beyond the Atlantic provinces. Once approved, you have **90 days** to register.

## Register

You can register online, by mail or in person at an Access Nova Scotia centre. Registration costs **$68.55**. The province says it takes 1–2 weeks to receive your Certificate of Registration, and the registry has warned of processing delays.

## Renew every year

Unlike most provinces, Nova Scotia renews **every year**, for $68.55, and you must also file an annual return. The registry sends a reminder, but renewing on time is your job.

## Your details are public

Information filed with the registry is a public record. If you work from home and do not want customers to see your address, ask about using a PO box or a mailing address service.

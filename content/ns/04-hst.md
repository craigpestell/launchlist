## One tax, not two

Nova Scotia charges **14% HST**, which combines the federal and provincial portions into a single tax. It is registered, collected and filed with the **CRA**. Unlike BC, it has **no separate provincial sales tax account** to register for.

## When you must register

You must register once your taxable sales pass **$30,000 over four consecutive calendar quarters**.

## Why register earlier

We recommend registering from the start. As a registrant you can **claim back the HST you pay** on inventory and business expenses. You generally must stay registered for at least a year once you opt in.

## Filing

You will choose how often to file. Annual filing is common for small businesses, but check which option applies to you. File even if you collected nothing.

## Selling outside Nova Scotia

The rate you charge depends on where the sale is made, which for goods you ship is **where the customer receives them**, not where you are based. A customer in Ontario pays 13% HST, for example, and some provinces have their own sales taxes. Ask your accountant before you ship outside Nova Scotia, or begin by selling to Nova Scotia customers only.

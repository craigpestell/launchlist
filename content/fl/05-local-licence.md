## Local business tax receipts

Florida has no general state business license for a retailer. Instead, **counties and cities can each charge a local business tax** and issue a receipt. If you are inside city limits, you may need one from both the county and the city. The amount is set locally, so ask your county tax collector and your city. Do not assume another county's rules apply to you.

When you apply, you must show your fictitious name registration (step 2), or a signed statement saying why you don't need one.

## Renewal dates

Receipts are sold from July 1 and **expire on September 30** each year. A late renewal carries a penalty that starts at 10% for October and grows each month, up to 25%. Running a business without a receipt you need carries a 25% penalty (seen November 2026; confirm current amounts).

## Working from home

Florida law lets a qualifying **home-based business operate in a residential area**. Local governments cannot license or regulate it differently from other businesses, apart from their local business tax. There are conditions, for example on parking, outside appearance and employees, and retail sales must happen in the home itself rather than in another building on the property. Ask your city or county how this applies to you.

## Other permits

Some activities need extra permits, for example selling food, alcohol or tobacco, putting up signs or renting a shop. Check with your city or county and the state agency for your product before you start selling.

## Sole proprietorship: fictitious name

If you trade under a name other than your own, you register a **fictitious name with the Florida Division of Corporations**, not with your county. The fee is $50 (seen November 2026; confirm current fee). You can file online on Sunbiz or by mail.

Before you register, you must advertise the name at least once in a newspaper in the county where your business is based. You confirm on the form that you have done this. Ask the newspaper for its price, which varies.

A registration lasts until December 31 of the fifth year. Renew it in that final year, also for $50 (seen November 2026; confirm current fee). Your county or city will ask to see your registration before it issues a business tax receipt (step 5).

## LLC: Division of Corporations

You form an LLC by filing Articles of Organization on Sunbiz. The fee is **$125, covering the articles and the registered agent designation** (seen November 2026; confirm current fee). You must name a registered agent with a Florida street address. Sunbiz records are public, so the addresses you list can be seen by anyone.

An LLC that trades under its own registered name does not need a fictitious name. If it uses a different name, it registers that name for $50 as well.

## Yearly report for an LLC

Every Florida LLC files an annual report online between **January 1 and May 1**, starting the year after it is formed. The fee is $138.75 (seen November 2026; confirm current fee). A report filed after May 1 carries a $400 late fee, and the Division says it cannot waive it. The report asks for your EIN (step 3).

Ask an accountant if you are unsure what your LLC needs to file.

## Sole proprietors

A sole proprietorship has **no annual report** with the state. Your yearly state paperwork is your sales tax returns (step 4) and your local business tax receipt renewal (step 5).

## The certificate

You register as a sales and use tax dealer with the Florida Department of Revenue before you sell taxable goods. Apply online with the Florida Business Tax Application, or on paper Form DR-1. Register each business location. Once registered you receive a Certificate of Registration (Form DR-11) and a Florida Annual Resale Certificate (Form DR-13). The Department's registration pages list **no fee to register** (seen November 2026; confirm when you apply).

## The rate

The state rate is **6%**. Most counties add a discretionary sales surtax, and the surtax that applies is the one for the county where the item is delivered. For 2026, county surtaxes run from none to 2% (seen November 2026; confirm current rates). The Department publishes the rates each year on Form DR-15DSS. On some sales, only the first $5,000 of the price is subject to surtax. Ask an accountant how surtax applies to what you sell.

## Filing

How often you file depends on how much tax you collect. **Most new businesses start on quarterly returns.** Returns are due on the 1st and late after the 20th of the month after each period. File a return even if you made no sales. If you file and pay electronically on time, you can keep a small collection allowance, up to $30 per return (seen November 2026; confirm current amount).

## Buying stock to resell

Florida issues you an **Annual Resale Certificate** with your registration. Give suppliers a copy so you do not pay sales tax on stock you resell. It expires December 31, and the new one is available online each November. If you use stock yourself instead of selling it, you owe use tax on it. See step 13.

## Sellers outside Florida

A business with no physical presence in Florida must register once its **taxable sales into Florida exceed $100,000** in the previous calendar year (seen November 2026; confirm current threshold). Marketplaces collect the tax on sales they handle for you. For selling into other states yourself, see step 6.

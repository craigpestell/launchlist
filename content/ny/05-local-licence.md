## State licenses

New York does not appear to have one general business license for every business (checked November 2026; confirm on Business Express). Instead, some products and activities need their own state license. The **Business Wizard on New York Business Express** asks about your business and lists the state licenses and permits that apply.

## County, city, town and village permits

Business Express says many counties, cities, towns and villages require **their own permits and licenses**. The rules differ from place to place, so check with the local offices where your business is based and where you sell, for example at a market or show. If you work from home, ask about home business and zoning rules.

## New York City

**New York City licenses businesses in more than 45 industries** through its Department of Consumer and Worker Protection, for example electronics stores, secondhand dealers and general vendors. These rules apply only inside the five boroughs. If you are based or sell there, check the city's license list.

## Keep them

Save copies of any license or permit and note the renewal dates. Your insurer (step 7) may ask for them.

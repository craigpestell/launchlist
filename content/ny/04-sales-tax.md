## The Certificate of Authority

New York's sales tax permit is called a **Certificate of Authority**. It comes from the New York State Department of Taxation and Finance, and you apply through New York Business Express. Apply **at least 20 days before you start business**, and do not make taxable sales until you have it. This applies even if you sell from home, online or at a few craft shows. The Tax Department's pages do not list a fee (seen November 2026; confirm before you apply). Display the certificate where you do business.

## The rate

The state rate is **4%**. Counties, cities and some school districts add their own rates, and an extra 0.375% applies in the Metropolitan Commuter Transportation District. The rate depends on where the goods are delivered, so use the Tax Department's rate lookup by address. Clothing and footwear under $110 an item is exempt from the state 4%, but local tax may still apply. Ask an accountant how rates apply to what you sell.

## Filing

Most new sellers **file quarterly**. The quarters run March–May, June–August, September–November and December–February, and returns are due 20 days after each quarter ends. You file a return even if you made no sales, and most sellers must file online.

## Buying stock to resell

Give your suppliers a completed **Form ST-120, Resale Certificate**, so you do not pay sales tax on stock you resell. You need a Certificate of Authority to use it. Do not use it for things you keep for the business. See step 13.

## Sellers outside New York

Out-of-state sellers must register once they have **more than $500,000 in sales and more than 100 sales** delivered into New York in the past four sales tax quarters (seen November 2026; confirm current threshold). For selling into other states yourself, see step 6.

## Sole proprietor: fictitious name with the SCC

If you trade under a name other than your own, file a **Certificate of Assumed or Fictitious Name, Business Conducted by an Individual** with the State Corporation Commission. Since 2020 the SCC is the central filing office for these in Virginia, so you do not file with your county or circuit court. You can file online through the SCC's Clerk's Information System (CIS) or by mail. The fee is **$10 (seen November 2026; confirm current fee)**, and you get a fictitious name receipt. A certified copy costs $6.

Using a business name without filing is a misdemeanor in Virginia, with a fine of up to $2,500. The certificate is public and shows a street address for your office or home.

## LLC: Articles of Organization

If you chose an LLC in step 1, file Articles of Organization with the SCC online in CIS or on Form LLC1011 ($100, seen November 2026; confirm current fee). You need the LLC's name, principal office address, and a registered agent with a Virginia street address. You can be your own agent. If the LLC uses a trade name, it files its own fictitious name certificate ($10) after it is formed.

## LLC: yearly fee

A Virginia LLC does not file an annual report. It pays an **annual registration fee of $50**, due by the last day of the month it was formed, each year after (seen November 2026; confirm current fee). The SCC sends the notice to your registered agent about two months before. A late payment adds a $25 penalty, and an LLC that still has not paid three months after the due date is canceled.

## Sole proprietors

A sole proprietor has **no yearly state filing or fee** for the business name or the business itself, beyond sales tax returns (step 4) and your income tax return. Ask an accountant about anything else that applies to you.

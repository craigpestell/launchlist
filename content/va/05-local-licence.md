## No general state license

**Virginia does not issue one general business license.** Instead, most counties, cities and towns require a local **Business, Professional and Occupational License (BPOL)**, run by the local commissioner of the revenue or finance office. Some products still need a state license or permit, for example food or alcohol.

## How BPOL works

Each locality sets its own rules. State law lets a locality charge a flat license fee, capped at $50 in a locality of 25,000 people or more and $30 in a smaller one, or a tax on gross receipts above a threshold (seen November 2026; confirm locally). Many localities renew licenses each year, often by March 1, and ask new businesses to apply soon after they start. Some can exempt a business with no profit. **Check with your own locality**, and with any town inside your county, since rules differ from place to place. Do not assume another locality's rules apply to you.

## Other local rules

If you work from home, ask about home occupation and zoning rules. Some localities also tax business equipment as personal property. If you sell at a market or show in another locality, ask whether it needs its own license.

## Keep them

Save copies of any license and note the renewal dates. Your insurer (step 7) may ask for them.

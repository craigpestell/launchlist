## The certificate of registration

Virginia's sales tax permit is the **Sales Tax Certificate of Registration (Form ST-4)**, from the Virginia Department of Taxation (Virginia Tax). You register online. When you finish, you get a 15-digit sales tax account number and the certificate, which you display at your registered location. Virginia Tax's pages do not list a fee (seen November 2026; confirm when you apply).

## The rate

The general rate is **5.3% in most of Virginia**: 4.3% state plus 1% local. It is 6% in Central Virginia, Hampton Roads and Northern Virginia, 6.3% in some southern counties and cities, and 7% in James City County, Williamsburg and York County (seen November 2026; confirm current rates). Groceries and some personal hygiene items are taxed at 1%. The rate depends on where the sale is made or the goods are received, so check Virginia Tax's list for each place. Ask an accountant how this applies to items you ship.

## Filing

Virginia Tax decides whether you **file monthly or quarterly**, based on how much tax you collect. Returns are due on the 20th of the month after the period ends, for example April 20 for January–March. You must file and pay online, and file even if you made no sales. Late returns carry a penalty of at least $10 (seen November 2026; confirm current amounts).

## Buying stock to resell

Give your suppliers a completed **Form ST-10, Sales and Use Tax Certificate of Exemption**, so you do not pay sales tax on stock you resell. Do not use it for things you keep for the business. See step 13.

## Sellers outside Virginia

Out-of-state sellers must register once they have **more than $100,000 in gross sales or 200 or more transactions** with Virginia customers in the previous or current calendar year (seen November 2026; confirm current threshold). Sales through a marketplace such as Etsy or Amazon are taxed by the marketplace. For selling into other states yourself, see step 6.

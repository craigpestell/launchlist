State-specific steps for a solo retailer in Maryland. Federal steps (EIN, Schedule C/SE, quarterly federal estimates, nexus in other states, insurance, banking, FTC, CPSC/FDA) are in `us-federal.md` and `content/shared/us/`. Researched from official pages only: the State Department of Assessments and Taxation (SDAT) fee schedule and forms, Maryland Business Express (MBE), COMAR (dsd.maryland.gov), Maryland statutes (mgaleg.maryland.gov) and the Comptroller's legal library. The Comptroller moved its tax pages to the "Maryland Tax Connect" portal (services.marylandcomptroller.gov), which renders only with JavaScript and could not be read; sales tax facts come from statutes, COMAR and legal library pages instead.

Maryland oddities for a retailer: every business, including a sole proprietor, gets an SDAT identification number; anyone holding business property or needing a license files a yearly personal property return with SDAT by April 15; and a **trader's license** from the county Clerk of the Circuit Court is required for each place of business selling goods, renewed every May 1.

## Business structure options

- **Sole proprietorship:** one owner, no distinction between owner and business, owner liable for all debts (MBE "Choose a Business Structure"). Not a legal entity, but MBE says a sole proprietor must still follow state and local licensing and tax rules and must get an **SDAT identification number** to open a business bank account. Recommended to start.
- **LLC (SDAT Charter Division):**
  - Articles of Organization: **$100** (SDAT "Fee Schedule for Documents Relating to Corporate Charters", revised May 2024, seen 2026-11-26).
  - Processing: non-expedited 4–6 weeks on paper, 6–8 weeks online; expedited service **$50** (7–10 business days); rush same day **$425** paper or **$325** online. MBE online payments add a 3% service fee (same fee schedule).
  - Articles must give the principal office address in Maryland and a resident agent's name and address (Corps. & Ass'ns §4A-204).
  - Name must include "Limited Liability Company", "L.L.C.", "LLC", "L.C." or "LC" (Corps. & Ass'ns §1-502(b), applied by §4A-208).
  - Name reservation: **$25** (fee schedule).
  - **Annual Report (Form 1):** LLC fee **$300**, due **April 15** for every entity in existence on January 1, whether or not it owns property; a 60-day extension to June 15 is available online. Failure to file can lead to forfeiture. Fee waived for employers enrolled in MarylandSaves (SDAT businesses page; MBE FAQ; Form 1 instructions, seen 2026-11-26).
  - Yearly cost for a single-member LLC: $300 annual report.
- Partnerships and corporations: out of scope for a solo retailer.
- **Recommendation:** start as a sole proprietor.

## Name search and registration

- **Trade name (Corps. & Ass'ns §1-406):** anyone doing business under a name other than their own must, before starting, file a trade name certificate with SDAT. It must include the SDAT identification number of the associated business, so a sole proprietor first gets an SDAT ID.
  - Fee **$25**; expedited +$50; amendment and cancellation $25 each. Effective **5 years**; renew within the last 6 months (Trade Name Application and Instructions, Rev. 08/2026; §1-406).
  - SDAT checks the name only against other trade names; registration confers no greater rights to the name. Acknowledgement normally within 4 weeks (application instructions).
  - Filed at state level (SDAT), not the county. No newspaper publication found.
- **SDAT identification number for a sole proprietor:** apply through MBE, or on paper with BPP Form 20 "Application for Identification Number, Sole Proprietorship or General Partnership". No fee shown on the form or MBE pages (**Not confirmed** as free). IDs start with D, F, W, L, T or Z and end with eight digits (MBE "Register a Business in Maryland").
- **Business personal property return (Form 2, sole proprietors):** sole proprietors who possess business personal property or need a business license file Form 2 with SDAT by **April 15** (extension to June 15). No filing fee shown. Since July 1, 2022, a business whose personal property (including inventory) has a total original cost under **$20,000** statewide is exempt from the tax but must still file each year and check the attestation box (TP §7-245; 2026 Form 2 instructions). Late penalty: 1/10 of 1% of the county assessment plus interest. Form 2 instructions note that trader's license problems arise from not filing the return or not reporting inventory.
- LLCs report property on Form 1 (above).
- Entity name search: MBE name search (MBE "Select a Business Name").

## Federal IDs

Same as federal. Maryland adds the SDAT identification number (from SDAT) and a Central Registration Number (CRN) from the Comptroller for state tax accounts (MBE "Register a Business in Maryland"). A sole proprietor applying only for a sales tax license may not need an FEIN (secondary snippet of the old CRA help; **Not confirmed**).

## Sales tax

- **Sales and use tax license** from the **Comptroller of Maryland**, applied for with the Combined Registration Application (CRA) through MBE or Maryland Tax Connect (MBE "Maryland Tax and Insurance Accounts"). Required before engaging in business as a retail vendor (Tax-General §11-702); one license per place of business (§11-703; BTT-2); effective until surrendered or revoked, so no renewal (§11-706).
- Fee: not stated in the statute or any page read. **Not confirmed.**
- **Rate: 6%** state (§11-104 bracket schedule; Comptroller Business Tax Tips #8 and #26 in search results). No local sales taxes found in any official page read; **Not confirmed** (third-party sources only).
- **Filing (COMAR 03.06.03.03):** returns due monthly by the 20th of the following month; the Comptroller may assign a less frequent basis when tax is expected to be under $100 a month. Returns are required even when no tax is due. What a new small seller is assigned: **Not confirmed** (portal unreadable).
- **Resale (COMAR 03.06.01.14; TG §11-408):** the resale certificate "need not be in a particular form" but must show the buyer's name, address, Maryland sales and use tax registration number (eight digits starting with 0 or 1) and that the goods are for resale. A blanket certificate is allowed. A vendor may not accept one for a purchase under $200 paid by cash, check, card or EFT unless the goods are delivered to the buyer's retail place of business.
- Records kept 4 years (BTT-2).
- **Remote sellers (COMAR 03.06.01.33B(5)):** out-of-state sellers must collect when, in the previous or current calendar year, gross revenue from deliveries into Maryland exceeds **$100,000** or they make **200 or more** separate transactions (effective October 1, 2018). Marketplace facilitators collect from October 1, 2019 (legal library SUT-TA-SEP-25-2019).
- **Transient vendor license:** MBE lists it as a CRA account "required if you sell at roadside or temporary spots in Maryland." Fee and exemptions **Not confirmed**.

## State taxes on the business and owner

- **State income tax (TG §10-105, current text):** single filers 2% to 6.50% in brackets (4.75% on $3,001–$100,000; 6.25% over $500,000; 6.50% over $1,000,000); joint filers 4.75% on $3,001–$150,000 rising to 6.50% over $1,200,000. Extra 2% on net capital gain for federal AGI over $350,000. Corporations 8.25%. Tax year of effect not read; rates quoted as "2%–6.5%" only.
- **County income tax (TG §10-106):** every county and Baltimore City sets a local income tax of **2.25%–3.30%** of Maryland taxable income, collected on the state return; nonresidents pay a special rate equal to the lowest county rate (§10-106.1). Individual county rates not read.
- **Estimated tax:** quarterly estimated returns exist (TG §10-902, 25% per installment). The threshold for who must pay: **Not confirmed**.
- **Business personal property tax:** assessed by SDAT on the Form 1 / Form 2 return, levied locally; under-$20,000 exemption above.
- LLC: $300 annual report fee. No franchise or minimum tax for an LLC found.

## Licenses and permits

- **Trader's license (Business Regulation Title 17, Subtitle 18):** required for a person who "operates a room or other place of business for selling goods" (§17-101(h), §17-1803). A separate license for each store or place of business (§17-1803(b)). Issued by the **Clerk of the Circuit Court** of the county (§17-1808; MBE licenses page; Form 2 instructions). Exempt: growers, makers or manufacturers of their own goods, and a private individual's one yearly sale of personal effects of up to 14 days (§17-1803(d)). Exhibitors at shows need one unless an exemption or affidavit applies (§17-1803(e)).
  - Fee (§17-1807): in a county with the uniform fee, $15 ($20 in Baltimore City or Baltimore County). Otherwise based on stock-in-trade value: outside Baltimore City and County $15 for up to $1,000, scaling to $800 over $750,000; Baltimore City and County $20 for up to $1,000, scaling up.
  - New and renewal licenses need an SDAT certification of inventory value from the personal property return in counties without the uniform fee; renewal also needs certifications of no unpaid property taxes (§17-302).
  - Licenses take effect **May 1** and expire the next **April 30**; fees are prorated quarterly for later applications (§17-303, §17-305).
  - Zoning approval needed before a first license in Baltimore County, Howard County, Cecil County (where its planning office has jurisdiction) and Calvert County (§17-1805, §17-302(e)).
  - Whether a home-based online-only seller is a "trader": **Not confirmed** (definition says "room or other place of business"; ask the clerk).
  - mdcourts.gov (clerk pages) returns a JavaScript bot check to curl; not read.
- MBE: state occupational licenses through the Maryland OneStop portal; local business licenses through county inspections or licensing offices or the Clerk of the Circuit Court (MBE "Business Licenses and Permits").
- Local zoning and home-occupation rules: county and city, not researched.

## Registered agent / address privacy

- LLC articles must name a resident agent with a Maryland address and give a principal office address in Maryland (§4A-204). Entity records are public on MBE.
- Trade name applications are public record and include the business address (application form).

## Privacy law

- **Maryland Online Data Privacy Act (Commercial Law Title 14, Subtitle 47):** applies to a person conducting business in Maryland or targeting Maryland residents that in the preceding calendar year controlled or processed personal data of at least **35,000 consumers** (excluding data processed solely to complete a payment), or at least **10,000 consumers** while deriving more than **20%** of gross revenue from selling personal data (§14-4702). Effective date (believed October 1, 2025) not read on an official page: **Not confirmed**.
- **Maryland Personal Information Protection Act (Commercial Law §14-3504):** any business that owns, licenses or maintains computerized personal information of Maryland residents must investigate a breach and notify affected individuals as soon as reasonably practicable and within **45 days**, unless misuse is not likely. Applies regardless of size.
- marylandattorneygeneral.gov privacy pages redirected to the home page; not read.

## Employer registrations (brief)

Employers register for withholding through the CRA and for unemployment insurance with the Maryland Department of Labor (MBE "Maryland Tax and Insurance Accounts"). Out of scope for a solo retailer.

State-specific steps for a solo retailer in Wisconsin. Federal steps (EIN, Schedule C/SE, quarterly federal estimates, nexus in other states, insurance, banking, FTC, CPSC/FDA) are in `us-federal.md` and `content/shared/us/`. Researched from official pages only: the Department of Financial Institutions (dfi.wi.gov), the Department of Revenue (revenue.wi.gov), the Legislature (docs.legis.wisconsin.gov), onestop.wi.gov and dsps.wi.gov. All loaded with curl; nothing was blocked.

## Business structure options

- **Sole proprietorship:** no state formation filing. DOR's New Business page says sole proprietors register directly with DOR (for the seller's permit). Recommended to start.
- **LLC (Department of Financial Institutions, Division of Corporate and Consumer Services):**
  - Articles of Organization (Form 502, ch. 183): **$130 online, $170 on paper** (DFI Fees page, LLC column, seen 2026-11-26). Expedite fee $100.
  - **Annual report:** **$25 online, $40 on paper** (DFI Fees page). DFI FAQ: a domestic entity delivers its annual report "in each year following the calendar year in which the entity registered, during the calendar year quarter in which the anniversary date of the registration occurs". Due by the end of that quarter (March 31, June 30, September 30 or December 31). A delinquent entity risks administrative dissolution.
  - Registered agent: must be a Wisconsin resident or entity; the agent's business office is the "registered office" (DFI FAQ). Change of registered agent $25 on paper, $10 online.
  - Name rule (Wis. Stat. 183.0112): must contain "limited liability company", "limited company", "LLC" or "LC" (or abbreviations such as "Ltd." and "Co."), and must be distinguishable from names on DFI records.
  - Wisconsin LLCs can register with DFI, DOR and DWD together through the **One Stop Business Registration portal** (onestop.wi.gov; DOR New Business page).
  - No franchise tax or LLC minimum tax for a single-member LLC found. Yearly state cost for an LLC: the $25 online annual report.
- Partnerships and corporations: out of scope for a solo retailer.
- **Recommendation:** start as a sole proprietor.

## Name search and registration

- **Trade names:** registered with **DFI** as a trademark or trade name. DFI General Information: "Trademark registration is not required". Fee **$15**, lasts 10 years, renewable in the 6 months before expiry. The form must be notarized. Registration gives no ownership rights; rights come from use. File online (Trademark File Online) or on paper; free search. Filing a trade name does not register or reserve an entity name (Trademarks FAQ).
- DFI FAQ: "Sole Proprietorships can register their business name by filing a registration of Tradename."
- **No county DBA filing** found on any official page. That there is no county requirement is **Not confirmed** (DFI only says sole proprietors "can" register).
- No newspaper publication rule found for trade names or LLCs.
- Entity name availability: DFI's CRIS search (DFI FAQ).

## Federal IDs

Same as federal. Wisconsin issues no separate business number; the seller's permit comes with a DOR account.

## Sales tax

- **Seller's permit** from the **Wisconsin Department of Revenue**. Required for anyone with a Wisconsin sales location making retail sales of taxable products or services, unless all sales are exempt. Not needed if you sell only through a marketplace provider that collects the tax (pcs-seller, updated Nov 17, 2026).
  - Apply online through Business Tax Registration (BTR) or paper Form BTR-101, **at least three weeks before** opening. Online: number by email in 1–2 business days, paper permit 7–10 business days later.
  - Display the permit prominently; a wallet copy is sent for temporary locations. Not transferable when you buy a business; a change of entity needs a new permit.
  - DOR may require a **security deposit up to $15,000**, often where there is a history of delinquent taxes; returned after 24 consecutive months of compliance.
- **Fee:** BTR fee **$20**, covering two years, regardless of the number of locations; **$10** renewal for each further two years (pcs-btr, updated Nov 20, 2026).
- **Rates (pcs-taxrates, updated Nov 23, 2026):** state **5%**. County tax **0.5%** in most counties; **Milwaukee County 0.9%** from January 1, 2024; Waukesha and Winnebago have no county tax. **City of Milwaukee 2%** from January 1, 2024 (the only city tax); combined 7.9% in Milwaukee. Retailers collect county and city tax on sales sourced there; DOR has an address rate lookup.
  - Baseball stadium tax ended March 31, 2020 (Pub 201); no stadium tax now.
  - Premier resort area taxes (Pub 201 Part 20.K): 0.5% in Bayfield, Eagle River, Stockholm, Rhinelander, Sister Bay, Ephraim, Sturgeon Bay and Minocqua (last two from July 1, 2026); 1.25% in Wisconsin Dells and Lake Delton. Applies only to certain business types (by SIC code).
- **Filing (Pub 201 10/26 Part 8.A; pcs-sales, Mar 11, 2026):** generally **quarterly** unless DOR assigns monthly or annual; due the last day of the month after the period. File even with no sales. Electronic filing required (My Tax Account) unless a waiver (Form EFT-102) is granted. Retailer's discount exists. Keep records 4 years.
- **Exemption / resale certificate:** **Form S-211, Wisconsin Sales and Use Tax Exemption Certificate** (also S-211E electronic and S-211-SST). Resale is one of the exemption reasons on the form (form fields not read in detail: **Not confirmed** beyond the title and use for resale).
- **Economic nexus (remote-sellers, Feb 10, 2026):** remote sellers must collect if gross sales into Wisconsin exceed **$100,000** in the previous or current calendar year. The 200-transaction test was removed February 20, 2021. Gross sales include exempt and resale sales. Wisconsin is a Streamlined Sales Tax member.

## State taxes on the business and owner

- **State individual income tax:** rates **3.5%–7.65%** (pcs-taxrates). 2026 single brackets: 3.5% to $15,110; 4.4% to $51,950; 5.3% to $332,720; 7.65% above. Sole-proprietor and single-member-LLC profit is reported on the owner's return.
- **Estimated tax** (pcs-estpay): required if you expect to owe $500 or more; Form 1-ES; due April 15, June 15, September 15 and January 15.
- **Local income tax:** none found; no official page states there is none: **Not confirmed**.
- No franchise or minimum tax for a single-member LLC found.

## Licenses and permits

- No general state business license found. DOR links to DSPS for professional and occupational licenses; local permits come from municipalities. No official page found that says there is no general license: **Not confirmed**.
- No single official page covers local licensing, so no 05 capture script.

## Registered agent / address privacy

- An LLC must have a Wisconsin registered agent and registered office (DFI FAQ). Entity records are searchable in CRIS. Trade name registrations are public DFI records.

## Privacy law

- **No comprehensive consumer privacy law.** AB 172 / SB 166 (proposed Wis. Stat. 100.80, consumer data protection; would have applied at 100,000 consumers) "Failed to pass" on March 23, 2026 (docs.legis.wisconsin.gov).
- **Breach notice, Wis. Stat. 134.98:** an "entity" (defined to exclude individuals) that conducts business in Wisconsin and maintains personal information must notify affected people within a reasonable time, not more than 45 days. Personal information: name plus SSN, driver's license or state ID, financial account or card number with access code, DNA profile or biometric data. Whether a sole proprietor (an individual) is covered: **Not confirmed**; the guide words it cautiously.

## Employer registrations (brief)

Employers register for withholding with DOR and for unemployment insurance with DWD (One Stop portal). Out of scope for a solo retailer.

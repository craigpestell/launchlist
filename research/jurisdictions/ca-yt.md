## Business structure

- **Sole proprietorship** (recommended for a solo retailer to start). Yukon's Start a business page lists corporations, extra-territorial corporations, partnerships, limited partnerships, limited liability partnerships and "Business name (sole proprietorship)". Source: https://yukon.ca/en/doing-business/businesses-societies-and-securities/start-business
- "A business name, in general terms, is the title under which a sole proprietor carries on business." Source: https://yukon.ca/en/doing-business/starting-business-and-self-employment/file-declaration-business-name
- Corporate Affairs staff "cannot provide any interpretation of legislation or operational advice" on sole proprietorships, partnerships or corporations; it points to a lawyer or accountant. Same page.

## Name search and registration

- **Who must register:** "As a sole proprietor, you can use your given name without needing to register it. If you want your business to be different than your given name, you have to register your business name." Law: Partnership and Business Names Act (named on the page). Source: https://yukon.ca/en/doing-business/starting-business-and-self-employment/file-declaration-business-name
- **Name search / reservation:** required before filing. You obtain a **certificate of name reservation** (Form 1, or online in the Yukon Corporate Online Registry, YCOR, https://ycor-reey.gov.yk.ca/). The name on Form 8 must match the certificate. Fee: **$60** on paper or in person, **$40** online by credit card through YCOR. The certificate is valid **90 days**; review takes up to 5 business days; if it lapses you resubmit and pay again. Naming rules: 5 to 120 characters, must not mislead or duplicate another organization's name, some names need consent (for example ones suggesting government affiliation). Same page, fetched 2026-10-17.
- **Filing:** Form 8, Declaration of business name (delivery address must be a physical address, not a PO box; the date business began cannot be in the future). Submit to Corporate Affairs by drop-in, drop box, mail, fax or email, with payment. Office: 307 Black Street, 1st floor, Whitehorse, Monday to Friday 8:30 am to 4:30 pm; 867-667-5314; corporateaffairs@yukon.ca. Whether Form 8 itself can be filed online (not just the name reservation and renewal) is not stated on the page. Same page.
- **Fee:** **$25** to file the declaration. Source: same page, fetched 2026-10-17. The web-search summary agreed ($25 filing, $40/$60 reservation).
- **Time:** up to 5 business days after documents and payment are accepted. Same page.
- **Renewal:** registration is valid **3 years**. Renewal is **$25**, for another 3 years, and can be filed at any time within 3 months before expiry, on paper or online in YCOR as a guest using the private filing key from the confirmation or renewal notice. "You cannot renew an expired business name"; if it expires you must register again. Source: https://yukon.ca/en/doing-business/businesses-societies-and-securities/renew-your-business-name-declaration (fetched 2026-10-17)
- Screenshot candidate for step 2: https://yukon.ca/en/doing-business/starting-business-and-self-employment/file-declaration-business-name. Content is in a stepped "Next" widget, so the first view shows only "What is a business name?". Click "Next" to reach the reservation step, the Form 8 step and the "$25" fee text.

CRA Business Number, shared with the other Canadian guides: https://www.canada.ca/en/services/taxes/business-registration.html. The Yukon filing page sends users to the CRA for business identifier numbers and taxes.

## Sales tax

- **GST 5%, provincial/territorial sales tax 0%.** Yukon row of the CRA rates table: GST/HST 5%, PST 0%, unchanged from April 2013 through today. There is no territorial sales tax, so no territorial tax account to open. Source: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html (page date modified 2025-04-01, fetched 2026-10-17)
- Registration threshold: $30,000 of taxable supplies, federal and shared with every other guide. Source: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html (fetched 2026-10-17)
- Goods shipped to other provinces are taxed at the destination's rate (place-of-supply rules; see the Ontario research).

## Licences

- **Territorial general business licence: none found.** Checked carefully:
  - The Government of Yukon Permits and licensing page (https://yukon.ca/en/doing-business/permits-and-licensing, fetched 2026-10-17) lists only sector licences (gaming, highway signs, parks, food, liquor, cannabis, natural resources, waste). There is no general business licence.
  - The Corporate Affairs name-filing page says "For information about business licences, contact the City of Whitehorse 867-668-8346", i.e. it treats business licences as municipal.
  - laws.yukon.ca "Legislation by title", letters B and C (fetched 2026-10-17), shows no "Business Licence Act" and no territorial general licensing act. The Consumers Protection Act and Business Corporations Act are listed. This is evidence from a listing, not an official statement that no such licence exists, so it is recorded under Not confirmed.
  - Sector licences found in search results (not relevant to general goods retail unless selling that product): pawnbroker and second-hand dealer licence, $10 a year, expiring March 31 (https://yukon.ca/en/doing-business/professional-licensing/apply-pawnbroker-and-second-hand-dealer-licence, seen in search result only); liquor and cannabis.
- **Whitehorse business licence (municipal step).** Anyone conducting business "for profit or gain" in Whitehorse needs a licence; storefront, non-local, street vendor and home-based businesses are all named. Source: https://www.whitehorse.ca/business-in-whitehorse/doing-business/business-license/ (fetched 2026-10-17)
  - Standard licence, 12 months: **$176**. Late renewal within 30 days of expiry: 10% of the base fee; after 30 days a $55 reactivation fee (or 10% if higher). Name or address change: $29.04. Same page, fetched 2026-10-17 (fee table shown on the page; the page's own effective date was not visible).
  - Retail or wholesale over 220 m2 adds $0.73 per m2 (not relevant to a home business).
  - Application: Business Licence Application Form; a development permit (home-based development permit for a home business) through the City's e-permitting; a certificate of trade name if not trading under your own name. Pay online; forms also at Land and Building Services. The page lists the Land and Building Services office as 187A Range Road and mail payments go to 2121 Second Avenue; search results showed another office address on older City documents, so confirm before visiting. Phone 867-668-8340.
  - The page does not say whether online-only sales need a licence. Business Licence Bylaw: https://www.whitehorse.ca/whitehorse_bylaws/business-license-bylaw/ (consolidated to Bylaw 2019-13, found by search, not read).
- **Whitehorse home-based business rules** (zoning). The licence page requires a home-based development permit. Zoning Bylaw 2025-37, section 5.13 and Table 12 (the page itself still cites sections 6.8 to 6.10 of the older Bylaw 2012-20):
  - Level One (secondary use, generally no traffic): inside the dwelling only, no outside storage, no non-resident employees, one client at a time, and **Retail Sales: "Not allowed"**.
  - Level Two (up to one non-resident employee, limited traffic): "Goods prepared as part of the business may be sold." Not allowed in multiple housing.
  - One non-illuminated fascia sign up to 0.37 m2.
  - Source: https://www.whitehorse.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/403b-2025-37-Proposed-Full-Zoning-Bylaw-2025-37-Amended.pdf (a 159-page PDF titled "Proposed Full Zoning Bylaw 2025-37 Amended"; read from the file text, fetched 2026-10-17; adoption status of this version not confirmed). The 2012-20 minor home-based checklist (https://www.whitehorse.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/MinorHomeBasedBusinessPack.pdf) likewise lists "retail sales" as not permitted for a minor home business.
  - For the guide: a home-based reseller in Whitehorse should check with Land and Building Services before applying. Whether online-only sales shipped from home count as prohibited "retail sales" is not stated.
  - Development permit information: https://www.whitehorse.ca/business-in-whitehorse/zoning/development-permits/ (found by search, not read; a 2024 City information sheet reportedly listed a $40 home-based business permit, not verified).
- Screenshot candidate for the municipal step: https://www.whitehorse.ca/business-in-whitehorse/doing-business/business-license/. The site blocks scripted fetches (Cloudflare); use a real browser. The page may collapse sections, so check that "Who requires a business license" and the Payment fee table are visible and expand them if not.
- **Inter-Municipal Business Licence:** the Association of Yukon Communities describes a licence covering occasional sales in other participating Yukon municipalities, bought on top of a home-municipality licence (https://ayc-yk.ca/inter-municipal-business-licence/, seen in search results only, not fetched). Relevant only if selling in person outside Whitehorse.
- Other Yukon municipalities and areas outside any municipality: check locally. Licensing outside municipalities was not confirmed.
- Territorial licence screenshot candidate: none applies. If wanted, https://yukon.ca/en/doing-business/permits-and-licensing shows the absence of a general business licence.

## Consumer protection and privacy

- **Privacy:** Yukon has no private-sector privacy law deemed substantially similar to PIPEDA (only BC, Alberta and Quebec do). The OPC states "PIPEDA also applies to organizations in the Northwest Territories, Yukon and Nunavut, as they are considered federally- regulated." So PIPEDA applies to a Yukon online retailer. Source: https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/r_o_p/prov-pipeda/ (date modified 2020-05-11, fetched 2026-10-17). The Yukon Access to Information and Protection of Privacy Act covers public bodies only.
- **Consumer protection:** Consumers Protection Act, RSY 2002, c. 40, and Consumers Protection Regulations. Listed as current on https://laws.yukon.ca/cms/legislation-by-title.html (letter C). The Act text could not be fetched (HTTP 403 for scripted requests, and the PDF could not be extracted from the browser), so whether it has any internet-sales or distance-contract provisions is **Not confirmed**. Search-result summaries suggest it is oriented to sales agreements, time sales and repossession, not online sales, but that is not verified. Act PDF: https://laws.yukon.ca/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/2002/2002-0040/2002-0040.pdf

## Ordered checklist

1. Structure and name (sole proprietorship)
2. Register a business name with Corporate Affairs (name reservation first; not needed under own given name)
3. CRA Business Number
4. GST (5%)
5. Whitehorse business licence and home-based development permit (zoning limits retail sales from a home)
6. Insurance
7. Bank account
8. Bookkeeping and income tax
9. Payment processor
10. Legal pages and privacy (PIPEDA applies)
11. Product compliance
12. Supplier account

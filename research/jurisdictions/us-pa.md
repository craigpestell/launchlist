State-specific steps for a solo retailer in Pennsylvania. Federal steps (EIN, Schedule C/SE, quarterly federal estimates, nexus in other states, insurance, banking, FTC, CPSC/FDA) are in `us-federal.md` and `content/shared/us/`. Researched from official pages only: pa.gov (Department of State and Department of Revenue), the PA Business One-Stop Shop (hub.business.pa.gov), DCED, phila.gov and palegis.us all loaded with curl. attorneygeneral.gov returned 403 to curl and WebFetch.

Pennsylvania's oddity: a sole proprietor's fictitious name is registered with the **state** Department of State, not the county, and must be **advertised in two newspapers** in the county. Local taxes are unusually important: almost every municipality levies a local earned income tax that covers a sole proprietor's net profits, and Philadelphia has its own business taxes and license. These are marked **local** or **Philadelphia only** below and must never be presented as statewide.

## Business structure options

- **Sole proprietorship:** one owner, personal liability, income on the owner's return. No state filing needed when trading under your own name. Recommended to start.
- **LLC (Department of State, Bureau of Corporations and Charitable Organizations):**
  - Certificate of Organization (DSCB:15-8821) with a docketing statement (DSCB:15-134A). Filed online via Business Filing Services (file.dos.pa.gov) or on paper.
  - Fee: **$125** (DOS Fees page, seen 2026-11-26). Fees are nonrefundable. Veteran- and reservist-owned small businesses may be exempt from some fees (Act 135 of 2016).
  - "No advertising is required when forming a domestic limited liability company" (DOS LLC page).
  - Registered office: a Pennsylvania street address (no PO box alone) or a Commercial Registered Office Provider (CROP) (DOS register-a-business FAQ).
  - **Annual report** (Act 122 of 2022, form DSCB:15-146): required from 2025. **$7** for an LLC; filing window **January 1 – September 30** each year; online at file.dos.pa.gov. **Beginning with reports due in 2027**, failure to file leads to administrative dissolution six months after the due date and loss of the name; reinstatement is possible ($35 online / $40 paper plus $15 per unfiled report). DOS warns that third parties charge $100–$200 for the same filing. The decennial report is repealed. (DOS Annual Reports page and Fees page, seen 2026-11-26.)
  - No franchise tax or minimum tax for a single-member LLC found. Capital stock/franchise tax not re-checked: **Not confirmed** that nothing else applies.
  - Name rule: must contain "company", "limited" or "limited liability company" or an abbreviation (15 Pa.C.S. § 204(c)). Read from a search summary of the statute, not the statute page (palegis.us statute pages load in an iframe that did not render): **partly confirmed**. Must be distinguishable from names on the DOS register.
- **Recommendation:** start as a sole proprietor. The LLC costs $125 plus $7 a year, and no newspaper ad is required for it.

## Name search and registration

- **Fictitious Names Act (54 Pa.C.S. Ch. 3):** any individual, sole proprietorship, LLC or other entity doing business under a name other than its real name must register the name with the **Pennsylvania Department of State** on **Registration of Fictitious Name, DSCB:54-311**. Fictitious names have not been filed at the county seat since the early 1980s. A name that does not readily identify the owner (for example adding "& Sons") is fictitious. (DOS Fictitious Names page, seen 2026-11-26.)
  - Fee: **$70** (DOS Fees page). Each ancillary filing (amendment, withdrawal, cancellation) $70.
  - Form needs: the name, a brief description of the business, a principal place of business street address (PO box alone not accepted), and the name and address of each owner. No Pennsylvania address is required for the owner (FAQ).
  - **Advertising:** if an individual is listed on the form (Box 4), the registrant must advertise in **two newspapers of general circulation in the county** where the business is located, **one of which must be a legal newspaper** (one paper is enough if only one circulates there). The notice may say it has filed or intends to file. Proof of publication is not sent to the Bureau; keep it with your records (54 Pa.C.S. § 311(g); 15 Pa.C.S. § 102). DOS publishes a county-by-county list of legal newspapers. Newspaper charges: **Not confirmed** (set by the papers).
  - Effect: no exclusive right to the name and no liability protection. Cannot include "corporation", "incorporated", "limited", "LLC" and similar unless the owner is that type of entity; "company" is allowed.
  - Failing to register: you cannot enforce a contract made under the name in a Pennsylvania court until registered, and the court may impose a **$500** penalty.
  - Fictitious names do not file annual reports (Annual Reports FAQ). Any renewal or expiry for a fictitious name: none found, **Not confirmed**.
- **LLC using another name** also files DSCB:54-311 ($70).
- Name availability: DOS business entity search at file.dos.pa.gov (link seen; not used).

## Federal IDs

Same as federal. Pennsylvania issues its own tax account (Account ID) when you register in myPATH. The Philadelphia BIRT account needs an EIN or SSN.

## Sales tax

- **Sales, Use and Hotel Occupancy Tax License** from the **Pennsylvania Department of Revenue**, through the "Pennsylvania Online Business Tax Registration" in **myPATH**. New customers can register without logging in; existing myPATH users (including personal income tax filers registering a sole proprietorship) log in and use "Register New Business Tax Accounts" (Register My Business for Taxes page). Apply before making taxable sales.
- **Fee:** "There is no fee to apply" (PA Business One-Stop Shop FAQ, revised 12/17/2025; Clothing Stores and Boutiques guide). Renewed automatically every five years if returns and payments are up to date. Display the license at each location (REV-717).
- **Rate:** state **6%**; **plus 1% local in Allegheny County (7% total)** and **2% in Philadelphia (8% total)**. These are the only local sales taxes. Local tax applies to sales originating in, or for use in, those places; vendors located there collect it (SUT page; Retailer's Information Guide REV-717, 02-26). Not due on deliveries outside Pennsylvania.
- Exempt examples: most clothing (not formal wear, sporting goods or accessories such as jewelry), food not ready to eat (candy and gum are taxable), textbooks. Digital products are taxable. (SUT page, REV-717.)
- **Filing frequency** (REV-717): set by actual tax liability. Semi-annual if under $300 a year (due August 20 and February 20); quarterly if under $600 a quarter (due the 20th of the month after the quarter); monthly if $600 or more a quarter (due the 20th of the next month); Accelerated Sales Tax prepayments at $25,000 or more a quarter. File even with no sales. myPATH filing is free.
- Vendor discount for filing and paying on time: the lesser of 1% or $25 (monthly), $75 (quarterly), $150 (semi-annual) (REV-717).
- **Resale certificate:** **Pennsylvania Exemption Certificate, REV-1220**, with your sales tax license number (Account ID).
- **Economic nexus for out-of-state sellers:** more than **$100,000** in gross sales into Pennsylvania in the prior calendar year, through all channels; no transaction count (Department of Revenue Online Retailers page; Act 13 of 2019).
- Other licenses from Revenue: Transient Vendor Certificate (sellers without a fixed place of business in PA who sell at shows), Promoter License (show organizers).

## State taxes on the business and owner

- **Personal income tax:** flat **3.07%** on taxable income, including net profits from a business, for individuals and LLCs not taxed as corporations (Department of Revenue Personal Income Tax page, seen 2026-11-26). No standard deduction or personal exemption. Filed on the PA-40.
- **Estimated tax:** required if Pennsylvania taxable income not subject to withholding is expected to exceed **$9,500 ($292 in tax)** for the year (PA Personal Income Tax Guide, Estimated Payments section, seen 2026-11-26).
- **Local earned income tax (local):** under Act 32, municipalities and school districts levy an earned income tax on earned income and **net profits**, collected by a local tax collector. Rates vary by address; DCED has a PSD code and EIT rate lookup and the collector's contacts. Self-employed people may need quarterly local estimates and an annual local return (DCED Local Income Tax Information, seen 2026-11-26). Rates not quoted.
- **Local Services Tax and business privilege/mercantile taxes (local):** some municipalities levy these; rules vary. Not researched place by place.
- **Philadelphia only:**
  - **Business Income & Receipts Tax (BIRT):** every individual, LLC or other business doing business for profit in Philadelphia files, even with no profit. 1.410 mills on gross receipts and 5.71% on taxable net income; due April 15 for the prior year. The $100,000 exemption no longer applies. New businesses skip the estimated payment in their first filing year (phila.gov BIRT page, seen 2026-11-26).
  - **Net Profits Tax:** sole proprietors and LLCs; 3.74% (resident) / 3.43% (nonresident) for tax year 2025, due April 15, with estimates April 15 and June 15; a credit for part of the BIRT net income portion may apply (phila.gov NPT page).
- LLC: $7 annual report only (above).

## Licenses and permits

- "There is no general business license that all Pennsylvania businesses are required to obtain"; specific activities may need a license (PA Business One-Stop Shop, Clothing Stores and Boutiques guide). The One-Stop Shop has a Business Registration Checklist and industry reference guides.
- DOS FAQ: contact your county, township, city or borough about local licensing.
- **Philadelphia only:** **Commercial Activity License** from the Department of Licenses and Inspections, required for any person or entity doing business in Philadelphia (including businesses outside the city doing business there). **No cost**. Needs an EIN or SSN and a BIRT account; apply through eCLIPSE (phila.gov CAL page, seen 2026-11-26). Holding a CAL means filing a BIRT return each year.
- Local zoning and home-occupation rules: local, not researched.

## Registered agent / address privacy

- An LLC needs a Pennsylvania registered office street address or a CROP; the address is public on the DOS record.
- The fictitious name registration is public and shows the principal place of business and the owner's name and address.

## Privacy law

- **No comprehensive consumer privacy law in force.** House Bill 78 (Consumer Data Privacy Act) passed the House on October 1, 2025 and was re-reported as amended from the Senate Communications and Technology Committee on June 24, 2026; "Executive Action: has not yet reached this milestone" (palegis.us HB 78 history, seen 2026-11-26). A Senate amendment reportedly raises the threshold to 100,000 consumers: secondary source only, **Not confirmed**.
- **Breach of Personal Information Notification Act** (73 P.S. § 2301 et seq.): applies to entities that maintain computerized personal information of Pennsylvania residents and requires notice after a breach. The Attorney General's site blocked curl and WebFetch, so the details (scope for small businesses, AG notice thresholds) are **Not confirmed** on an official page. Guide words this cautiously.

## Employer registrations (brief)

Employer withholding, unemployment compensation and the Local Services Tax apply once you hire. Out of scope for a solo retailer.

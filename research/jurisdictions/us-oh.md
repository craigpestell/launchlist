State-specific steps for a solo retailer in Ohio. Federal steps (EIN, Schedule C/SE, quarterly federal estimates, nexus in other states, insurance, banking, FTC, CPSC/FDA) are in `us-federal.md` and `content/shared/us/`. Researched from official pages only. tax.ohio.gov loaded with curl when browser Accept headers were sent. **ohiosos.gov (all pages and PDF forms) and ohiobusinesscentral.gov returned a Cloudflare check or 403 to both curl and WebFetch**, so no Secretary of State page was read directly. The Secretary of State fees below come from search-engine result snippets of the ohiosos.gov "Business Filing Forms & Fee Schedule" page (two separate searches gave the same figures). They are marked **snippet only** and need checking in a normal browser before publishing. codes.ohio.gov and lsc.ohio.gov timed out, so no statute text was read.

Local income taxes (municipal net profit tax, school district income tax) are set place by place and must never be presented as statewide figures.

## Business structure options

- **Sole proprietorship:** one owner, unlimited personal liability, profit reported on the owner's federal and Ohio returns. No state filing to form one. Recommended to start.
- **LLC (Ohio Secretary of State, Business Services):**
  - Articles of Organization, Form 610 (replaces Form 533A), filed online through Ohio Business Central or by mail: **$99** (snippet only, ohiosos.gov fee schedule, seen 2026-11-26). The ohiosos.gov page itself could not be loaded. **Not confirmed** on a fetched page.
  - Yearly report or fee: Ohio does not appear to require an LLC annual report or charge a yearly LLC fee (secondary sources only). **Not confirmed.**
  - Name rule: the ending an Ohio LLC name must carry ("LLC", "limited liability company" or similar) and the distinguishability rule: **Not confirmed** (ORC 1706 not reachable).
  - A single-member LLC is taxed like a sole proprietor for Ohio income tax unless it elects otherwise (follows federal treatment; see us-federal.md). Ohio's Commercial Activity Tax (below) does not apply below $6 million.
  - Switching from sole proprietor to LLC needs a new vendor's license: the Erie County Auditor's page says a new license is needed if you incorporate, add a partner or move county (seen 2026-11-26).
- **Recommendation:** start as a sole proprietor.

## Name search and registration

- Names are filed with the **state** (Ohio Secretary of State), not the county. No newspaper publication requirement was found (none mentioned on any page read; **Not confirmed** on an SOS page).
- **Trade name registration, Form 534A: $39**; **renewal, Form 523A: $25**; trade or fictitious name amendment (Form 524A) and change of registrant name (Form 558): $25 each (snippet only, ohiosos.gov fee schedule, seen 2026-11-26). **Not confirmed** on a fetched page.
- Term: a trade name registration lasts **five years** and is renewed within the six months before it expires (ORC 1329.01/1329.04 via search summary only). **Not confirmed.**
- Whether a sole proprietor using a non-legal name must register a trade name or file a fictitious name report, and the consequences of not doing so (ORC 1329.01, 1329.10): secondary sources only. **Not confirmed.** Guide wording is cautious.
- Name search: the SOS business search (ohiosos.gov) could not be loaded. **Not confirmed.**

## Federal IDs

Same as federal. Ohio issues no separate general business number; the vendor's license number comes with the sales tax registration. Tax accounts are managed through OH|TAX eServices (myportal.tax.ohio.gov) and the Ohio Business Gateway.

## Sales tax

- **Vendor's license** from the **Ohio Department of Taxation**. "Ohio law requires any person or business making taxable sales or providing taxable services to first obtain a vendor license." Apply through **OH|TAX eServices** (license issued immediately, but you must first set up an eServices account) or with your **county auditor** (caao.org directory) (tax.ohio.gov/business/sales-and-use-tax, seen 2026-11-26).
- **Fee: $50** for new applications, up from $25 effective **April 9, 2025** (same page, plus the Erie County Auditor's vendor's license page, seen 2026-11-26).
  - **Vendor, Form ST-1, $50:** selling taxable goods from a fixed location in Ohio; each location needs a separate license; "This includes physical storefront locations and selling items online within Ohio."
  - **Transient Vendor, Form ST-1T, $50:** temporary locations such as shows and flea markets; valid statewide.
  - **Out-of-State Seller, Form UT 1000: no fee.**
- License stays valid until canceled (Vendors brochure, older edition that still shows the $25 fee). Erie County Auditor: a new license is needed if you move to another county, incorporate or add a partner; a trade name change does not need one.
- **Rate:** state rate **5.75%**. Counties and regional transit authorities add up to 3% in multiples of 0.05%; combined maximum **8.75%**. Rate changes take effect only at the start of a quarter (example on the page: Warren County, October 1, 2026). "The Finder" looks up rates by address, ZIP or GPS.
- **Sourcing:** Ohio is origin-based for most sales by Ohio vendors to Ohio consumers (Information Release ST 2009-03, as linked from the sales tax pages). Delivery and marketplace rules vary. Guide tells readers to check the rate with The Finder and ask an accountant.
- **Filing:** monthly by default, due the 23rd of the following month. **Semi-annual** allowed if liability is under $1,200 per six-month period, due the 23rd after each period. Quarterly is for direct pay and consumer use tax accounts only. File through OH|TAX eServices, and file even with no sales until the license is closed. Timely filing discount 0.75%, capped at $750 per license per month for returns due on and after January 1, 2026 (tax.ohio.gov sales tax and filing pages, seen 2026-11-26).
- How the Tax Department assigns a new small vendor's frequency (monthly vs semi-annual at registration): **Not confirmed**; guide says to check the frequency shown when you register.
- **Resale/exemption:** **Form STEC B, Sales and Use Tax Blanket Exemption Certificate** (continuing claim with one vendor); STEC U for a single purchase. Ohio also accepts the MTC Uniform Sales and Use Tax Certificate for resale and the Streamlined exemption certificate (tax.ohio.gov exemption certificates page, seen 2026-11-26).
- **Economic nexus (out-of-state sellers):** more than **$100,000** in sales to Ohio customers, **or 200 or more** separate sales, in the current or previous calendar year. Register for a seller's use tax license (UT 1000, no fee) through eServices or the Streamlined registration system. Marketplace-only sellers are excluded if the marketplace collects (same page). Ohio is a Streamlined Sales Tax member state.

## State taxes on the business and owner

- **Ohio individual income tax:** applies to a sole proprietor's or single-member LLC owner's profit, reported on the Ohio IT 1040.
  - For tax year 2026 the top nonbusiness rate is **2.75%**; the first $26,050 of nonbusiness income is taxed at 0% (tax.ohio.gov "What's New" and annual rates, seen 2026-11-26).
  - **Business income** is taxed at a flat **3%** after a **business income deduction** of the first $250,000 ($125,000 each for married filing separately). The 3% rate was read on tax.ohio.gov's annual tax rates page; the $250,000 deduction came from a search summary of the IT 1040 instructions and was not re-read. **Partly confirmed.** The guide only says business income gets its own treatment and points to an accountant.
  - **Estimated payments** are required if estimated Ohio tax minus withholding is more than **$500**. 2026 due dates: April 15, June 15, September 15 and January 15, 2027 (tax.ohio.gov estimated payments page, seen 2026-11-26).
- **School district income tax:** about a third of districts levy one, based on where you **live**. "Earned income" districts tax self-employment net earnings; "traditional" districts tax modified adjusted gross income. File Form SD 100. Use The Finder to check your district (tax.ohio.gov school district income tax page, seen 2026-11-26). The share of districts is **Not confirmed**; the guide does not state it.
- **Municipal net profit tax:** a local income tax on business net profit, administered by each municipality (or a third party such as a regional agency). Businesses can opt in to state administration through the Ohio Business Gateway (election by April 15 for calendar-year filers). Rates and rules are local; tax.ohio.gov keeps a list of municipal websites (tax.ohio.gov municipal net profit tax page, seen 2026-11-26).
- **Commercial Activity Tax (CAT):** applies to all business structures, including sole proprietors, but only above **$6 million** a year of Ohio taxable gross receipts from 2025 ($3 million in 2024). Rate 0.26%. Register within 30 days of crossing the threshold. Annual minimum tax eliminated from 2024 (tax.ohio.gov/business/commercial-activity-tax, seen 2026-11-26). A new small seller is well below it.
- No franchise tax or LLC minimum tax found.

## Licenses and permits

- No general statewide business license was found for a retailer. The vendor's license (sales tax) is the main state registration. **Not confirmed** on an official "no general license" statement (Ohio business portal pages returned 404).
- Some products and activities need their own state license (not researched product by product).
- Cities, villages and townships may require local permits; zoning rules apply to home businesses. Varies by place. The guide tells readers to ask their city or village, county and township offices.
- No single official page covers local licenses, so no step 05 capture script.

## Registered agent / address privacy

- An Ohio LLC must appoint a statutory agent with an Ohio address (secondary sources; Form 610 not readable). **Not confirmed.**
- Trade name registrations and LLC filings are public. The guide reminds readers that the address they file is public.

## Privacy law

- Ohio does not appear to have a comprehensive consumer privacy law in force (secondary sources only; no official page read). **Not confirmed.**
- Ohio's data breach notification law (ORC 1349.19) applies to businesses that own or license Ohio residents' computerized personal information (secondary sources; codes.ohio.gov unreachable). **Not confirmed.** The guide says it "may apply" and points to the Ohio Attorney General.

## Employer registrations (brief)

Out of scope for a solo seller with no staff. If hiring: Ohio employer withholding (Department of Taxation), unemployment insurance (Ohio Department of Job and Family Services) and workers' compensation (Ohio Bureau of Workers' Compensation, a state fund). Not researched in detail.

## Ordered checklist

1. Choose sole proprietorship (recommended) or LLC.
2. If using a name other than your own, register it with the Ohio Secretary of State ($39, five years; snippet only). Or file Articles of Organization (Form 610, $99; snippet only).
3. Get an EIN if needed (shared step 3).
4. Get a vendor's license ($50) through OH|TAX eServices or the county auditor before your first taxable sale. Transient vendor's license ($50) for shows and markets.
5. Check city, village, township and county permits and home business zoning.
6. File sales tax returns on the assigned schedule (monthly by default; semi-annual if eligible), even with no sales.
7. Note Ohio income tax, Ohio estimated payments, school district income tax and municipal income tax.
8. Renew the trade name every five years.

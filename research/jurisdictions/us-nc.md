State-specific steps for a solo retailer in North Carolina. Federal steps (EIN, Schedule C/SE, quarterly federal estimates, nexus in other states, insurance, banking, FTC, CPSC/FDA) are in `us-federal.md` and `content/shared/us/`. Researched from official pages only: the Secretary of State (sosnc.gov), the Department of Revenue (ncdor.gov), the Department of Justice (ncdoj.gov), county register of deeds pages, and the Economic Development Partnership of North Carolina (edpnc.com, a nonprofit public-private partner of the NC Department of Commerce, not a .gov site). sosnc.gov, ncdor.gov, ncdoj.gov, edpnc.com and county pages loaded with curl. ncleg.gov (statutes) blocks curl with a Cloudflare page.

## Business structure options

- **Sole proprietorship:** one owner, unlimited personal liability, income on the owner's return (EDPNC Business Structure). No state filing needed when trading under your own name. Recommended to start.
- **LLC (Secretary of State, Business Registration Division):**
  - Articles of Organization (form L-01): **$125** (SOS LLC fees page and form L-01, seen 2026-11-26). Online or by mail.
  - **Annual report:** due **April 15** of the year after formation and every year after; **$200**, plus a $3.00 credit card or $2.00 ACH transaction fee when filed online (SOS "Maintaining Registration", seen 2026-11-26). Required even with no activity. Non-filing can lead to administrative dissolution. The SOS warns about third-party solicitations offering "priority" annual report filing.
  - Registered agent required: an NC resident individual or a qualifying business entity, with a street address in NC (SOS registered agent pages). Public record.
  - Name rule: must contain "Limited Liability Company", "L.L.C.", "LLC" or another ending allowed by N.C.G.S. § 55D-20; must be distinguishable on the SOS records; restricted words such as "Bank", "Trust", "Wholesale" and "Insurance" need approval or proof (SOS feasible use of the name page).
  - **Franchise tax:** applies to corporations. An LLC is subject only if it elects to be taxed as a C corporation (NCDOR corporate filing requirements page). No franchise tax for a disregarded single-member LLC found; inferred, **Not confirmed** by an explicit statement.
  - Yearly cost for a single-member LLC: $200 annual report.
- Partnerships and corporations: out of scope for a solo retailer.
- **Recommendation:** start as a sole proprietor.

## Name search and registration

- **Where filed:** an **assumed business name certificate** is filed with the **county Register of Deeds**, not the Secretary of State (SOS Assumed Business Names FAQ: "You can file an assumed business name certificate in your local register of deeds' office."). Applies to sole proprietors and to LLCs trading under another name. Governed by Article 14A, Chapter 66 (effective December 1, 2017).
  - One certificate can list every county (or all 100) where you trade, for a single fee. No notary requirement. The certificate does not expire. Amend or withdraw within **60 days** of a change (SOS ABN FAQ and form).
  - Form (N.C.G.S. § 66-71.5, SOS form): up to 5 names per certificate; street address of the principal place of business required, no PO box. Public record.
  - **Fee: $26** to record, amend or withdraw, per county register of deeds pages (Wake, Guilford, Craven, Brunswick, Catawba, Orange, Durham, Wilkes; seen 2026-11-26). Guilford adds a $15 e-file convenience fee and cites N.C.G.S. 161-10. The SOS does not state the fee and points to the NC Association of Registers of Deeds. Statute not read (ncleg.gov blocked): the $26 figure is from county pages only, **Not confirmed** on the statute.
  - Statewide search: SOS hosts a database of assumed names filed since December 1, 2017. Older filings are only at the county. Assumed names are not unique and give no exclusive rights.
- No newspaper publication requirement for an assumed name or an LLC found.

## Federal IDs

Same as federal. North Carolina issues no separate business number; the sales tax account ID comes with the Certificate of Registration (Form NC-BR or online registration accepts SSN or EIN).

## Sales tax

- **Certificate of Registration** from the **North Carolina Department of Revenue**. Required for anyone engaged in retail sales of tangible personal property in NC, including online sellers and vendors at flea markets, specialty markets and events (NCDOR "Who should register"). Register online or with Form NC-BR.
- **Fee: none.** NCDOR registration page heading: "There is No Fee to Apply for a Certificate of Registration in North Carolina"; FAQ also warns about third-party sites charging for registration (seen 2026-11-26).
- Most online applicants receive their account ID immediately; the certificate is mailed within about 10 business days (up to 4 weeks for paper). The certificate becomes void after 18 months with no returns or no sales (FAQ).
- A sole proprietor who incorporates needs a new certificate (FAQ). Whether a sole proprietor forming an LLC needs a new registration is not stated: **Not confirmed**; guide says to ask NCDOR.
- **Rates:** state **4.75%**; county local rate **2.00% or 2.25%**; **0.50%** transit tax in Wake, Durham, Orange and Mecklenburg; Mecklenburg adds a further **1%** (transportation) from July 1, 2026. Combined totals 6.75%–8.25% (Wake 7.25%, Durham and Orange 7.5%, Mecklenburg 8.25%) (NCDOR current rates page, Directive SD-25-1, seen 2026-11-26). Qualifying food: 2% local only.
- Sourcing of shipped/delivered sales to the delivery county: not read on an official page, **Not confirmed**. Guide tells readers to ask.
- **Filing frequency (NCDOR filing frequency page):** quarterly if consistently under **$100** a month (due the last day of the month after the quarter: January, April, July, October); monthly by the **20th** for $100 to under $20,000 a month; monthly with prepayment at $20,000 or more. File even with no sales. Online filing free.
- **Resale certificate:** **Form E-595E, Streamlined Sales and Use Tax Certificate of Exemption**. NC is a Streamlined Sales Tax full member.
- **Economic nexus for out-of-state sellers:** gross sales in excess of **$100,000** sourced to NC in the previous or current calendar year (transaction count test removed). From July 2, 2026, the obligation starts the first day of the month at least 60 days after the threshold is met (NCDOR remote sales page, seen 2026-11-26). Marketplace facilitators have the same test.

## State taxes on the business and owner

- **State personal income tax:** flat **4.25% for 2025** and **3.99% for tax years beginning after 2025** (NCDOR tax rate schedules, seen 2026-11-26). Sole-proprietor and disregarded-LLC profit goes on Form D-400. No separate registration.
- **Estimated tax:** required if you expect to owe **$1,000 or more** after withholding and credits; due April 15, June 15, September 15 and January 15 (NCDOR estimated income tax page).
- **Local income tax:** none found on any page read. No official page states that none exists: **Not confirmed** as an explicit statement.
- LLC: $200 annual report. No franchise tax for a disregarded LLC found (see above).

## Licenses and permits

- **No general state business license.** EDPNC: "North Carolina no longer has a state-issued generic business license that will ensure compliance with all requirements." EDPNC lists about 950 state licenses by activity and offers free Small Business Advisors.
- **Local privilege license taxes** (general city and county business licenses) were repealed effective July 1, 2015 (S.L. 2014-3). Sources: UNC School of Government blog and Town of Hope Mills page (semi-official, not the statute): **Not confirmed** on the statute. Local permits, zoning and home-occupation rules still vary by place; not researched city by city.
- NCDOR state privilege license tax for professionals ended July 1, 2024 (NCDOR privilege license tax page). Not relevant to retail.

## Registered agent / address privacy

- An LLC must name a registered agent with an NC street address; this and the principal office address are public on the SOS database.
- An assumed business name certificate is public and must give a street address (no PO box), so a home-based seller should decide which address to use.

## Privacy law

- **Identity Theft Protection Act** (N.C.G.S. § 75-60 and following, including § 75-61 definitions and § 75-65 breach notice): applies to any business, including sole proprietors, that owns or licenses personal information of NC residents. After a security breach, notify affected people without unreasonable delay and notify the Consumer Protection Division of the Attorney General's office (NCDOJ security breach pages, seen 2026-11-26).
- **No comprehensive consumer privacy law in force found.** Bills (SB 1022, SB 757, HB 462 in the 2025 session) were reported in committee as of May 2026, by secondary sources only; ncleg.gov blocked. **Not confirmed** on an official page.

## Employer registrations (brief)

Employers register for income tax withholding with NCDOR (Form NC-BR) and unemployment insurance with the Division of Employment Security; workers' compensation applies at 3 or more employees (EDPNC employer requirements page). Out of scope for a solo retailer.

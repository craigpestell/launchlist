State-specific steps for a solo retailer in New Jersey. Federal steps (EIN, Schedule C/SE, quarterly federal estimates, nexus in other states, insurance, banking, FTC, CPSC/FDA) are in `us-federal.md` and `content/shared/us/`. Researched from official pages only: nj.gov (Division of Revenue and Enterprise Services, Division of Taxation), business.nj.gov (New Jersey Business Portal), njportal.com, the Business Action Center and county clerk pages. njconsumeraffairs.gov (Incapsula) and the Legislature's site (pub.njleg.gov, connection refused or timed out) could not be read.

## Business structure options

- **Sole proprietorship:** one owner, personal liability, income reported on the owner's NJ-1040. No formation filing. **Must still register for tax and employer purposes (Form NJ-REG)** with DORES: "All companies doing business in New Jersey need to register with the State" (business.nj.gov Register Your Business; DORES Getting Registered: "All individuals or companies doing business in New Jersey must be registered"). Recommended to start.
- **LLC (Division of Revenue and Enterprise Services, DORES):**
  - Certificate of Formation: **$100** for all for-profit entities (DORES Getting Registered, updated 07/01/26; DORES fees page). Secondary sources say this fell from $125 on July 1, 2026. File online at njportal.com/DOR/BusinessFormation. Then file NJ-REG.
  - Needs a **registered agent with a New Jersey address** (can be the owner) (business.nj.gov Register Your Business). Entity records are public; tax records are confidential. The business gets a 10-digit Entity ID.
  - **Annual report: $75**, "due every year on the last day of the month, in the month in which you completed your business formation" (business.nj.gov Taxes and Annual Report; DORES fees page). Missing two consecutive years can lead to revocation (DORES reinstatement page).
  - Alternate name registration $50 (renewal $50); name reservation $50 (optional, 6 months, "not recommended" per business.nj.gov); change of registered agent $25 (DORES fees page).
  - No LLC minimum tax or franchise tax for a single-member LLC found. Single-member LLC income goes on the owner's NJ-1040.
  - LLC name ending (designator) rules: **Not confirmed** on an official page read.
- **Recommendation:** start as a sole proprietor.

## Name search and registration

- **Sole proprietor using a name other than their own:** register the trade name with the **county clerk** in each county where the business operates (Starting a Business in New Jersey tax guide: "Sole Proprietors and Partnerships register trade names with their county"; Business Action Center trade name PDF says registration is compulsory in each county of operation; business.nj.gov: "register a business name at your local county clerk's office"). Protection covers only the counties registered.
  - Fees vary by county: **Union County $54** (four notarized forms; cancellation $27); **Gloucester County $50**, all four forms notarized, $1 per staff notarization, search the county trade name database first (county pages, seen 2026-11-26). Other counties: **Not confirmed** (Middlesex and Warren returned 403).
  - No newspaper publication requirement found for a trade name: **Not confirmed** either way.
- **LLC trading under another name:** alternate name registration with DORES, $50.
- Business entity name search: njportal.com/DOR/BusinessNameSearch.

## Federal IDs

Same as federal. A sole proprietor may register NJ-REG with an SSN or an EIN. NJ-REG gives a 12-digit New Jersey Tax ID and access to the Business Registration Certificate (BRC), needed for public contracting, state grants and tax credits.

## Sales tax

- **Certificate of Authority for Sales Tax** (business.nj.gov also says "Certificate of Authorization to Collect Sales Tax"), issued after registering on **Form NJ-REG** with DORES (online at njportal.com/DOR/BusinessRegistration). Register **at least 15 business days before** starting business (Starting a Business tax guide). Required for occasional and seasonal sellers, including flea markets and craft shows (su_12). Display it at the place of business.
- **Fee:** no fee listed on any page read. **Not confirmed** that it is free.
- **Rate:** **6.625%** statewide (business.nj.gov; Division of Taxation). No general local sales taxes (S&U-4). Exceptions: Urban Enterprise Zone certified sellers (UZ-2) may charge 50% of the rate on certain in-person sales; Salem County reduced rate on some in-person sales; Atlantic City luxury tax and Cape May County tourism sales tax on certain businesses (S&U-4).
- Exempt items include most clothing and footwear and most food for home consumption (S&U-4). Details: see the publication.
- **Filing:** quarterly **Form ST-50**, due by the **20th day of the month after the quarter** (April 20, July 20, October 20, January 20). File even with no sales, online. Monthly payments (ST-51) only if the business collected more than $30,000 in the prior year and more than $500 in the month (su_12; business.nj.gov).
- **Resale certificate:** **Form ST-3, Resale Certificate** (S&U-6). Keep certificates at least four years.
- **Economic nexus:** gross revenue from sales delivered into New Jersey **over $100,000, or 200 or more separate transactions**, in the current or prior calendar year (remotesellers.shtml). Marketplaces collect on marketplace sales. NJ is a full Streamlined Sales and Use Tax member.
- **Litter Control Fee:** retailers of litter-generating products pay 0.0225% of those retail sales (Form LF-5, due March 15); retailers with less than $500,000 of such sales are exempt (Division of Taxation, other taxes page).

## State taxes on the business and owner

- **Gross Income Tax:** graduated rates from **1.4%** to **10.75%** (Division of Taxation tax rate schedules and tax tables, updated 09/28/26). Sole proprietor and single-member LLC profit reported on NJ-1040.
- **Estimated tax:** required if you expect to owe more than **$400** after withholdings and credits; Form NJ-1040-ES or online (njit20, updated 06/29/26).
- **Local income tax:** none found. Small Business Manual: "Typically, cities or towns do not charge business taxes directly". Not confirmed on a tax-specific page.
- No LLC franchise or minimum tax for a single-member LLC found.

## Licenses and permits

- No general statewide business license found (**Not confirmed**). business.nj.gov Licensing and Certification Guide lists 551 state licenses, permits and certifications by industry.
- Municipal: Small Business Manual (Feb 2024) says there may be municipal fees, "including fire protection or mercantile licenses", and to contact the Municipal Clerk. Rules vary by town; zoning and home-occupation rules are local.

## Registered agent / address privacy

- An LLC needs a registered agent with a New Jersey address; corporate records are public (business.nj.gov). Annual report keeps the agent and address up to date.
- County trade name certificates are public records (county pages).

## Privacy law

- **New Jersey Data Privacy Act** (P.L.2023, c.266). Applies to controllers that, in a calendar year, control or process personal data of **at least 100,000 consumers** (excluding data processed solely to complete a payment transaction), **or at least 25,000 consumers and derive revenue or receive a discount from selling personal data**. Thresholds read only from a search-result snippet of the Division of Consumer Affairs FAQ (page blocked) and secondary summaries: **Not confirmed** on a page read directly. Effective January 15, 2025 (secondary). No revenue threshold (secondary).
- **Breach notice law** (N.J.S.A. 56:8-161 to -166) applies to businesses that hold New Jersey residents' personal information; requires notice to the State Police before notifying customers. Secondary sources only: **Not confirmed** on an official page.

## Employer registrations (brief)

NJ-REG also registers employers for withholding and unemployment and disability contributions. Out of scope for a solo retailer.

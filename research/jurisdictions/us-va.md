State-specific steps for a solo retailer in Virginia. Federal steps (EIN, Schedule C/SE, quarterly federal estimates, nexus in other states, insurance, banking, FTC, CPSC/FDA) are in `us-federal.md` and `content/shared/us/`. Researched from official pages only: the State Corporation Commission (scc.virginia.gov), Virginia Tax (tax.virginia.gov), the Code of Virginia (law.lis.virginia.gov) and virginia.gov. All loaded with curl.

**Correction to the starting brief:** a sole proprietor's assumed name is **not** filed with the circuit court. Since **January 1, 2020** the SCC Clerk's Office is the central filing office in Virginia for all certificates of assumed or fictitious name, including sole proprietors (Form SCC59.1-70-IN instructions, Rev. 02/26; SCC Fictitious Names FAQ). Circuit courts only handle releases of older names filed there.

## Business structure options

- **Sole proprietorship:** no state filing to form one. Owner personally liable; profit on the owner's return. Recommended to start.
- **LLC (State Corporation Commission, Clerk's Office):**
  - Articles of Organization (Form LLC1011): **$100** (SCC Forms and Fees, Virginia LLCs, seen 2026-11-26). File online in the Clerk's Information System (CIS) or on paper. Online without attachments gets an acceptance email after checkout; attachments add about five business days.
  - Optional name reservation: $10 for 120 days, renewable.
  - **Annual registration fee: $50**, due the last day of the month the LLC was organized, each year after formation. SCC sends the assessment to the registered agent two months before; it can't be paid before assessment. Late penalty **$25**; unpaid by the last day of the third month after the due date, the LLC is canceled (SCC Annual Registration Fees FAQ, seen 2026-11-26).
  - **No annual report for LLCs:** the SCC Annual Reports FAQ says annual reports are filed by corporations. LLCs pay only the annual registration fee.
  - Registered agent: required; must be a Virginia resident with a Virginia physical office (no PO box except tiny towns), and either a member/manager of the LLC or a Virginia State Bar member, or a third-party business. You can be your own agent. The registered office address is public record (SCC Registered Agent FAQ).
  - Name rule: must include "limited company" or "limited liability company", or "LC", "L.C.", "LLC" or "L.L.C."; must be distinguishable from active entity names on SCC records (core-name test); restricted words (bank, trust, engineer, architecture, etc.) (SCC Business Entity Names FAQ; LLC FAQs).
  - Yearly cost for a single-member LLC: $50 annual registration fee. No state franchise tax or LLC minimum tax found.
- Partnerships and corporations: out of scope.
- **Recommendation:** start as a sole proprietor.

## Name search and registration

- **Certificate of Assumed or Fictitious Name, Business Conducted by an Individual (Form SCC59.1-70-IN)**, filed with the **SCC Clerk's Office**, online in CIS or by mail. **Fee $10** (form Rev. 02/26 and Fictitious Names FAQ, seen 2026-11-26). Required for anyone doing business in Virginia under a name other than their own legal name. Not notarized (the form only asks for a signature). The form asks for a post office address including a street address of the individual's office or residence; filings are public.
- Using a fictitious name without filing: misdemeanor, fine up to $2,500, and you can't bring a court action in Virginia (Fictitious Names FAQ).
- Fictitious names don't need to be unique and give no exclusive rights; SCC doesn't decide who may use a name.
- Certified copy of the receipt: $6. Release of a name: $10.
- No newspaper publication requirement found (none on the SCC pages or form).
- No renewal or expiry of a fictitious name certificate mentioned on the SCC pages or form: **Not confirmed** that none exists.
- **LLC trading under another name:** files the entity version (SCC59.1-70-BE) after the LLC exists, $10.

## Federal IDs

Same as federal. Virginia Tax's online registration asks for an FEIN (a sole proprietor without one may use an SSN; the FAQ lists "You don't have a FEIN" as a reason you may need paper Form R-1 instead). Shared EIN step applies.

## Sales tax

- **Registration:** register with **Virginia Tax** online ("Register a Business in Virginia"). You receive a 15-digit sales tax account number and a **Sales Tax Certificate of Registration (Form ST-4)**, which must be displayed prominently at your registered location. In-state dealers' accounts begin with 10 (Retail Sales and Use Tax page, seen 2026-11-26).
- **Fee:** no fee listed on the registration pages. **Not confirmed** that it is free.
- **Rates:** state **4.3%** plus **1%** local = **5.3%** everywhere; **6%** in Central Virginia, Hampton Roads and Northern Virginia (regional 0.7%); **6.3%** in Charlotte, Danville, Gloucester, Halifax, Henry, Northampton, Patrick, Pittsylvania; **7%** in James City County, Williamsburg and York County. Food for home consumption and essential personal hygiene items: **1%** statewide. (Retail Sales and Use Tax page; 4.3% + 1% split from Tax Commissioner ruling 13-103.) Rate applies by locality where the sale was made or the goods were received.
- **Filing:** Virginia Tax sets monthly or quarterly based on tax liability; due the **20th** of the month after the period (quarters due April 20, July 20, October 20, January 20). File even with no sales. Must file and pay electronically (waiver available). Late: 6% per month, max 30%, minimum $10.
- **Resale certificate:** **Form ST-10, Sales and Use Tax Certificate of Exemption**, for a Virginia dealer buying for resale (can't be used for cigarettes since January 1, 2018; ST-10C for those).
- **Economic nexus:** remote sellers with **more than $100,000** in gross retail sales **or 200 or more** transactions to Virginia customers in the previous or current calendar year must register. Marketplace sales are handled by the facilitator (Remote Sellers, Marketplace Facilitators, and Economic Nexus page).

## State taxes on the business and owner

- **Individual income tax:** 2% to **5.75%** (5.75% over $17,000 of Virginia taxable income) (2026 Form 760ES tax rate schedule).
- **Estimated tax:** required if Virginia liability after withholding and credits is expected to be **more than $1,000**; due May 1, June 15, September 15 and January 15 (Individual Estimated Tax Payments page; 2026 Form 760ES).
- **Local income tax:** none. The local income tax article (Va. Code § 58.1-540) was repealed by Acts 2013, c. 766; secondary sources (Commonwealth Institute) say localities may not levy one. Treated as confirmed-by-statute but no Virginia Tax page states it plainly.
- LLC: $50 annual registration fee. A single-member LLC taxed as a disregarded entity reports on the owner's return. No franchise or minimum tax found.

## Licenses and permits

- **No general statewide business license.** Localities (counties, cities and towns) may levy the **Business, Professional and Occupational License (BPOL)** tax or fee, administered by the local commissioner of the revenue (Va. Code § 58.1-3703). State law caps the license fee at **$50** for a locality over 25,000 people (the over-50,000 cap was reduced to $50 by January 1, 2000) and **$30** under 25,000; localities may instead levy a tax on gross receipts above a threshold, and may exempt businesses with no after-tax profit (§ 58.1-3703 A and E). Amounts, thresholds and due dates (often March 1, or within 30 days of starting, per Richmond and Prince William County) are local.
- Examples (local, never statewide): Fairfax County requires a BPOL for every business with a physical location in the county, including home-based; Loudoun County requires one for businesses outside incorporated towns (search summaries of county pages, not re-read).
- Some localities also tax business tangible personal property (equipment). Local; not researched.
- Home occupation and zoning: local.
- virginia.gov/local-government lists every county, city and town.

## Registered agent / address privacy

- LLC: registered agent and registered office address are public record. A sole proprietor's fictitious name certificate shows a street address of the office or residence and is public.

## Privacy law

- **Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act** (Va. Code § 59.1-575 et seq.): applies to businesses that, in a calendar year, control or process personal data of **at least 100,000** Virginia consumers, or **at least 25,000** consumers and derive **over 50%** of gross revenue from selling personal data (§ 59.1-576 A). Attorney General enforces, with a 30-day cure period (§ 59.1-584). Most small stores fall below the threshold.
- **Breach notice (Va. Code § 18.2-186.6):** any individual or entity that owns or licenses computerized personal information must notify the Attorney General and affected Virginia residents without unreasonable delay after a breach. Applies regardless of size.

## Employer registrations (brief)

Employers register with the Virginia Employment Commission and for withholding with Virginia Tax. Out of scope.

State-specific steps for a solo retailer in Illinois. Federal steps (EIN, Schedule C/SE, quarterly federal estimates, nexus in other states, insurance, banking, FTC, CPSC/FDA) are in `us-federal.md` and `content/shared/us/`. Researched from official pages only. The Illinois Secretary of State's site (ilsos.gov, including cyberdriveillinois.com and apps.ilsos.gov) could not be reached by curl or WebFetch, so LLC facts come from the Limited Liability Company Act itself (805 ILCS 180, read on ilga.gov). tax.illinois.gov, dceo.illinois.gov and cookcountyclerkil.gov loaded with curl.

Cook County and City of Chicago details are examples only and are marked **Cook County only** or **Chicago only**; they must never be presented as statewide.

## Business structure options

- **Sole proprietorship:** one owner, unlimited personal liability, income on the owner's return. No state filing. DCEO's *Starting Your Business in Illinois Handbook* (2026) says sole proprietors and general partnerships file an assumed name with the county clerk and this "does not involve the Secretary of State". Recommended to start.
- **LLC (Secretary of State, Department of Business Services):** fees from 805 ILCS 180/50-10 (as amended by P.A. 102-49, eff. 1-1-22), seen 2026-11-26:
  - Articles of organization: **$150** ($400 for a series LLC).
  - Name reservation: $25. Amendment: $50. Change of registered agent or office: $25. Certificate or copies: $25 (the Act's figure for certificates of good standing and copies).
  - **Annual report: $75**, due before the first day of the LLC's anniversary month; it may be delivered in the 60 days before that date (180/50-1). If not filed before the first day of the anniversary month, the LLC is not in good standing; a **$100 penalty** applies if not fixed before the first day of the second month after the anniversary month, plus $100 for each further year (180/50-15). Reinstatement: $200.
  - Assumed name for an LLC (filed with the SOS, not the county): prorated to the 5-year renewal cycle: $150 in years ending 0 or 5, $120 in 1 or 6, $90 in 2 or 7, $60 in 3 or 8, $30 in 4 or 9; renewal $150 (180/50-10).
  - Online filing, any online processing fee and the expedite fee: **Not confirmed** (only third-party pages list them; ilsos.gov blocked).
  - Name rule (180/1-10): must contain "limited liability company", "L.L.C." or "LLC"; may not contain "Corporation", "Corp.", "Incorporated", "Inc.", "Ltd.", "Co.", "Limited Partnership" or "L.P."; "trust", "trustee" and "fiduciary" restricted; must be distinguishable on SOS records.
  - Yearly cost for a single-member LLC: $75 annual report. No state LLC franchise or minimum tax found for an LLC.
- Partnerships and corporations: out of scope for a solo retailer.
- **Recommendation:** start as a sole proprietor.

## Name search and registration

- **Sole proprietor using a name other than their own** files an assumed name certificate with the **county clerk** of each county where business is conducted (Assumed Business Name Act, 805 ILCS 405/1). The certificate states the assumed name, the owner's true name and post office address, and every business address; it is signed and acknowledged (notarized).
  - **Newspaper publication (405/1):** notice published in a newspaper of general circulation in the county, **once a week for 3 consecutive weeks**, first publication within **15 days** of filing; proof of publication filed with the clerk within **50 days** or the certificate is void.
  - Changes of name or address, or doing business in another county, need a new or amended filing (405/1).
  - Statute fee: $5 (405/3), but counties charge more in practice: **Cook County $50** (Cook County Clerk, seen 2026-11-26; apply online or in person/by mail, notarized, same steps for publication, certificate issued after proof; public search available) and **DuPage $10** (DuPage County Clerk, seen earlier in this session). **Cook County only / DuPage only.** Newspaper charges set by the papers: **Not confirmed**.
  - Does not apply to LLCs or corporations (405/4); Cook County also says LLCs, LPs, LLPs and corporations do not register there.
  - Failing to file is a Class C misdemeanor, each day a separate offense (405/5).
  - Renewal or expiry of a county certificate: **Not confirmed** (the Act read does not set a renewal period; ask the county clerk).
- No statewide database of sole proprietor assumed names; search each county.

## Federal IDs

- EIN: see shared step. IDOR registration (REG-1) asks for an FEIN or SSN; a sole proprietor without employees can use an SSN for federal purposes.

## Sales tax

- Permit name: **Certificate of Registration** (Retailers' Occupation Tax), issued by the **Illinois Department of Revenue (IDOR)**. Register online through **MyTax Illinois** ("Register a New Business (Form REG-1)"), about 1–2 business days; paper REG-1 4–6 weeks (another line on the same page says 6–8 weeks). Register "before you make any purchases, sales, or hire an employee" (IDOR Business Registration page).
- Certificates for ROT are now electronic only: print from MyTax Illinois ("View Account Letters"). Display the certificate at the place of business (PIO-117, from a search summary; PDF not read).
- Fee: IDOR FAQ 286 says there is **no registration fee** (certain license fees apply to specific businesses, for example liquor or tobacco). Seen 2026-11-26.
- Pub-113 says the certificate expires after one year and renews automatically for retailers; craft show and fair sellers must register.
- **Rate:** state **6.25%** general merchandise, 1% qualifying drugs and medical appliances (IDOR FAQ 139, ST-1 instructions). The state 1% grocery tax ended January 1, 2026 (P.A. 103-0781); counties and municipalities may impose a local 1% grocery tax. Local taxes add (home rule, non-home rule, business district, mass transit, county, park district and others). Local rates generally change January 1 and July 1. Rates by address: MyTax Illinois Tax Rate Finder.
- Sourcing: Illinois retailers generally use the rate at their location (origin); remote retailer sales are destination-sourced. Ask an accountant.
- **Returns:** Form ST-1. IDOR assigns frequency: monthly if average monthly liability over $200, quarterly if $50–$200, annual if under $50 (ST-1 instructions). Monthly due the 20th of the next month; quarterly the 20th after the quarter; annual January 20. Filing frequency for a brand-new seller with no history: **Not confirmed**. Many filers must file electronically.
- **Resale:** Form **CRT-61, Certificate of Resale**. The buyer needs an active Illinois account or resale number; sellers check it with IDOR's "Verify a Registered Business". Resale numbers for resellers renew every 3 years.
- **Remote sellers:** from January 1, 2026, a remote retailer must register once it has **$100,000 or more in gross receipts** from sales to Illinois purchasers in the preceding 12 months, checked quarterly; the 200-transaction test was removed (IDOR informational bulletin FY 2026-12, P.A. 104-0006).

## State taxes on the business and owner

- **Individual income tax: flat 4.95%** of net income (IDOR Income Tax Rates page, effective July 1, 2017; seen 2026-11-26). A sole proprietor's or single-member LLC owner's profit goes on Form IL-1040.
- **Estimated tax:** IL-1040-ES (2026) says you must make estimated payments if you reasonably expect your tax liability to exceed **$1,000** after withholding and credits. Installments due April 15, June 15, September 15 and January 15.
- **Personal property replacement tax:** 1.5% for partnerships, trusts and S corporations; 2.5% for corporations (IDOR rates page). Whether it applies to a disregarded single-member LLC: **Not confirmed** (generally treated as its owner; ask an accountant). Not applicable to a sole proprietor.
- **Local income tax:** none found on IDOR pages; Illinois cities do not appear to tax individual income (**Not confirmed** from an explicit official statement). Chicago has other local taxes (not income).

## Licenses and permits

- No general state business license (DCEO handbook). Some activities need state licenses (for example liquor, tobacco, food). DCEO points readers to the county clerk, city or village hall, and (Chicago only) the City of Chicago for local business licenses, zoning and building or health permits.
- Chicago business licensing pages on chicago.gov returned "page not found" for every URL tried, so no Chicago license detail is recorded. **Chicago only.**

## Registered agent / address privacy

- An LLC must keep a registered agent and registered office in Illinois (805 ILCS 180; reported on the annual report). Assumed name certificates are public and show every business address (405/1); Cook County's public search is online.

## Privacy law

- **No comprehensive consumer privacy law** found for Illinois (checked November 2026; **Not confirmed** against an official list, Attorney General pages not read).
- **Biometric Information Privacy Act (740 ILCS 14):** any private entity that collects biometric identifiers (for example face geometry or fingerprints) must have a public written retention policy and get written consent first (14/15). Applies regardless of size. Rare for a small online store, but relevant to virtual try-on tools or face or fingerprint scanning.
- **Personal Information Protection Act (815 ILCS 530):** any "data collector", including retail operators, that owns or licenses Illinois residents' personal information must notify them after a breach in the most expedient time possible (530/5, 530/10). Applies to every business size.

## Employer registrations (brief)

- Withholding: register through MyTax Illinois (REG-1). Unemployment insurance: IDES, also via MyTax Illinois. Out of scope until hiring.

## Ordered checklist

1. Choose a sole proprietorship (recommended) or LLC ($150 Articles to the SOS).
2. Sole proprietor with a trade name: file an assumed name with the county clerk (fee set by county, for example $50 Cook, $10 DuPage), publish the notice 3 weeks, file proof within 50 days. LLC: file Articles, then a $75 annual report each year before the anniversary month.
3. EIN (shared step).
4. Register with IDOR on MyTax Illinois for a Certificate of Registration before buying stock or selling; no registration fee; collect 6.25% plus local; file ST-1 on the assigned schedule; give suppliers CRT-61.
5. Check state, county and city licenses.
6. Shared steps 6–13.

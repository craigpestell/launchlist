## Business structure options

- **Sole proprietorship:** no state filing. FTB: "You can establish a sole proprietorship without registering with the California Secretary of State." Income goes on the owner's Form 540 using federal Schedule C. Owner is personally liable. Fee: none. (FTB sole proprietorship page, seen 2026-11-26.)
- **General partnership:** optional Statement of Partnership Authority with SOS; not relevant to a solo seller.
- **LLC:** formed with the California Secretary of State by filing Articles of Organization (Form LLC-1), online through bizfileOnline (5 days or less) or by mail (10+ days). **Fee $70** (seen 2026-11-26). The articles name an agent for service of process. (SOS LLC forms page.)
  - **Statement of Information (Form LLC-12): $20**, due within 90 days of registration and every two years after. SOS charges a **$250 penalty** for not filing (FTB collects it). (SOS LLC Statement of Information page; FTB LLC page.)
  - **Annual LLC tax: $800** to the Franchise Tax Board every year the LLC is organized or doing business in California, even with no business, until cancelled. First payment due the 15th day of the 4th month after filing with SOS (example on FTB page: formed June 18, due September 15), with Form FTB 3522. The first-year exemption applied only to tax years beginning 2021 through 2023 and has ended. (FTB LLC page, last updated 03/05/2026, seen 2026-11-26.)
  - **LLC fee** on California total income of $250,000 or more: $900 ($250,000–$499,999), $2,500 ($500,000–$999,999), $6,000 ($1,000,000–$4,999,999), $11,790 ($5,000,000 or more). Estimated by the 15th day of the 6th month with FTB 3536. LLC files Form 568. (FTB LLC page.)
  - Short form cancellation (LLC-4/8) within one year of organizing avoids the first-year $800 tax. (FTB LLC page.)
  - Yearly minimum for a small LLC: $800 FTB tax every year plus $20 every two years for the Statement of Information.
- **Corporation:** out of scope for a first-time solo seller; FTB minimum franchise tax not researched.
- **Recommendation:** start as a sole proprietorship. California's $800 yearly LLC tax makes an LLC noticeably more expensive than in most states; ask an accountant before forming one.

## Name search and registration

- **LLC names:** must be "distinguishable in the records" from existing LLCs with SOS and not "likely to mislead the public" (rules since January 1, 2021). Must include "Limited Liability Company", "LLC" or "L.L.C." ("Limited" and "Company" may be "Ltd." and "Co."). May not include bank, trust, trustee, incorporated, inc., corporation, corp., insurer or insurance company. (SOS Name Reservations page; SOS Business Entity Names Guidelines & Restrictions PDF, Rev. 04/2022.)
- SOS checks names only against the same entity type, **not against fictitious business names or trademarks**. Preliminary search: Business Search on bizfileOnline. (SOS Name Reservations page.)
- **Name reservation:** $10 for 60 days, online via bizfileOnline; can't be renewed for consecutive periods (one day gap). (SOS LLC forms page; Name Reservations page.)
- **Fictitious business name (FBN), the California DBA:** filed with the **county** where the principal place of business is, not the state. "There is no State-wide registry" (Sacramento County FAQ). SOS FAQ: "Fictitious business names are filed with the county in which the principal place of business is located."
  - Who must file: an individual whose business name does not include their surname, or implies other owners ("and Company", "Associates"). "Joyce Smith Catering" is not an FBN; "Smith and Company Catering" is. An LLC trading under any name other than its exact SOS name must file. (Sacramento County FAQ.)
  - Deadline: no later than 40 days from starting business (Fresno County page). State statute not read directly (leginfo blocked).
  - Publication: once a week for four consecutive weeks in a newspaper of general circulation in the county, starting within 30 days of filing (Sacramento County FAQ; Los Angeles County page). Search results quoted San Luis Obispo County as 45 days; not confirmed directly. Guide says "usually within 30 days; check your county".
  - Expires five years from filing; re-file to renew. Renewal without changes needn't be republished (Sacramento, Los Angeles).
  - Residential address of each owner is required and is public; PO boxes and mail services not allowed for residential or business address (Sacramento County FAQ). Los Angeles requires a notarized Affidavit of Identity (LA County page).
  - **Fees vary by county:** Fresno $35 first name plus $7 each additional name or registrant (Fresno County page via WebFetch, seen 2026-11-26); Los Angeles renewal $26 plus $5 each additional (LA County renewals page, seen 2026-11-26). Newspaper publication is a separate cost, not confirmed.
  - The county does not check for duplicates (Sacramento).

## Federal IDs

No California difference. EIN as in the shared step. The CDTFA application asks for an SSN (or substitute documents) and driver license number.

## Sales tax: seller's permit (CDTFA)

- **Name and issuer:** seller's permit, issued by the **California Department of Tax and Fee Administration (CDTFA)**. Required when you sell tangible goods in California, including through websites and online auctions. Selling without one "violates the law and subjects you to fines and penalties". Occasional sellers (no more than two sales in 12 months) don't need one. (CDTFA Publication 107, revision July 2024.)
- **Fee:** "There is no charge for a seller's permit." CDTFA may ask for a **security deposit** depending on business type and expected taxable sales. Can be issued the same day. Register online (Register a New Business Activity) or at an office; needs bank account details, estimated income, driver license and SSN. (Pub 107, "Applying for a Seller's Permit", seen 2026-11-26.)
- **Rate:** statewide **7.25%**; district taxes add **0.10% to 2.00%**, some areas more than one. Look up by address at maps.cdtfa.ca.gov. Current rate table effective October 1, 2026. (CDTFA rates page, seen 2026-11-26.)
- **District tax on deliveries:** any retailer with combined sales of tangible goods in or for delivery in California over **$500,000** in the current or previous calendar year must collect district use tax on all deliveries into any district (RTC 7262, AB 147). Below that, district tax depends on where the retailer is engaged in business; guide tells readers to ask CDTFA. (CDTFA Wayfair page.)
- **Filing frequency:** CDTFA assigns quarterly prepay, quarterly, monthly, fiscal yearly or yearly based on expected taxable sales. Quarterly returns due April 30, July 31, October 31, January 31. Monthly: last day of the next month. Yearly sales tax accounts: January 31. **File even with no sales.** (CDTFA filing dates page, seen 2026-11-26.)
- **Resale certificate:** **CDTFA-230, General Resale Certificate**, given to suppliers for goods you will resell. Misuse is subject to penalties, and issuing one without intent to resell is a misdemeanor. (CDTFA Publication 103; Pub 107.)
- **Marketplace sellers:** a marketplace seller isn't required to register if all its California sales are marketplace sales through a registered facilitator; sales on your own site require registration. Marketplace sales count toward the $500,000 threshold. (CDTFA "Online Retailers: Registration and Local Tax".)
- **Economic nexus for sellers elsewhere:** retailers outside California must register and collect use tax if combined sales of tangible goods for delivery in California exceed **$500,000** in the preceding or current calendar year (RTC 6203, AB 147, from April 1, 2019). Counts exempt sales and sales of related persons. No transaction count. (CDTFA Wayfair page, seen 2026-11-26.) California is not an SST member (us-federal.md).

## State taxes and fees on the business

- **State income tax:** yes. California taxes individual income at **1% to 12.3%** (2025 Form 540 tax rate schedules, single: 12.3% over $742,953). A sole proprietor reports business profit on Form 540 using federal Schedule C; no separate registration with FTB. California estimated tax (Form 540-ES) generally required if you expect to owe at least $500 ($250 married/RDP filing separately); the FTB page seen still cites the 2024 year, so the guide doesn't quote the figure. (FTB sole proprietorship and estimated tax pages; FTB 2025 rate schedule PDF.) The 1% Mental Health Services Tax on income over $1 million is **Not confirmed** on a fetched page and left out.
- **LLC:** $800 annual tax, LLC fee over $250,000, Form 568, Statement of Information $20 every two years (above).
- No state gross receipts tax for a sole proprietor. City business taxes are local (below).

## State consumer privacy law

- **CCPA (as amended by CPRA):** applies to for-profit businesses doing business in California that meet any of: gross annual revenue of **$26.625 million or more** for the preceding calendar year (effective January 1, 2025); buy, sell or share personal information of **100,000 or more** California residents or households; or derive 50% or more of revenue from selling or sharing it. (California Privacy Protection Agency FAQ, seen 2026-11-26.) The Attorney General's CCPA page still shows "over $25 million" (older figure). The threshold is adjusted periodically; guide says to check cppa.ca.gov.
- **CalOPPA (Business and Professions Code 22575–22579):** an operator of a commercial website or online service that collects personally identifiable information about California residents must conspicuously post a privacy policy and follow it; there is no size threshold. Violation only if not posted within 30 days after notice. 2013 amendments require saying how the site responds to Do Not Track signals. (Attorney General, "Making Your Privacy Practices Public" PDF.)

## State, county and city licenses

- No general state business license. SOS: "The Secretary of State does not issue licenses or permits"; refers to **CalGold** (calgold.ca.gov, run by GO-Biz), which lists permits by city and business type. CalGold notes it is newly launched and may be incomplete. (SOS FAQ; CalGold home page, seen 2026-11-26.)
- **City:** most cities require a business license or business tax certificate, including for home businesses; rules and fees vary. Not researched city by city.
- **County:** unincorporated areas are licensed by the county (Los Angeles County business license office; Sacramento County Business Licenses Unit). Sacramento notes cities may require a license even if the business isn't based in the city.
- **Home occupation permits / zoning:** local; not researched. FTB lists "licenses, permits, and zoning clearance" as part of setting up a sole proprietorship.
- A filed FBN is not a license (Sacramento County FAQ).

## Registered agent / address privacy

- LLC Articles name an agent for service of process. Statement of Information lists addresses; SOS records are public. Detailed agent rules **Not confirmed** on a fetched page.
- FBN statements require each owner's **residential address**, which is public record; a PO box can't replace it (Sacramento).

## Employer registrations

Out of scope for a solo seller. If hiring: register with the Employment Development Department (EDD) (Pub 107 mentions this).

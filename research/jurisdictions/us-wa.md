State-specific steps for a solo retailer in Washington State. Federal steps (EIN, Schedule C/SE, quarterly federal estimates, nexus in other states, insurance, banking, FTC, CPSC/FDA) are in `us-federal.md` and `content/shared/us/`. Researched from official pages only (sos.wa.gov, dor.wa.gov, atg.wa.gov, app.leg.wa.gov, filelocal-wa.gov). All loaded with curl; nothing was blocked.

Washington differs from the template states in three ways: there is no separate sales tax permit (sales tax registration comes with the state business license and its UBI number from the Department of Revenue's Business Licensing Service); trade names are registered with the Department of Revenue, not a county; and most businesses owe the **business and occupation (B&O) tax**, a gross receipts tax, on top of collecting retail sales tax.

## Business structure options

- **Sole proprietorship:** no Secretary of State filing. Recommended to start. The owner still needs a Washington business license in many cases (see Licenses and permits).
- **LLC (Secretary of State, Corporations and Charities Division):**
  - Certificate of Formation: **$180** (SOS LLC filing resource page and fee schedule, seen 2026-11-26). Online instructions say "$180 in addition to an online processing fee"; the processing fee amount is **Not confirmed**. Optional expedite $100; same-day $150.
  - Initial Report: no fee when filed with the Certificate of Formation; otherwise $10, due within 120 days of formation.
  - **Annual Report: $70** (seen 2026-11-26), due by the last day of the month the LLC was formed (its "expiration date"), can be filed up to 180 days early. SOS sends a reminder about 60 days ahead. Late: $25 delinquency fee ($95 total, per fee schedule). Not filing leads to administrative dissolution. Reinstatement $140 plus missed annual report fees.
  - Name reservation $30 for 180 days. Amendment $30.
  - Name rule (RCW 23.95.305 and SOS page): must include "Limited Liability Company", "Limited Liability Co.", "L.L.C." or "LLC"; must be distinguishable on SOS records; certain words (bank, trust, cooperative and similar) are restricted.
  - Registered agent with a Washington address required (RCW 23.95.415).
  - After formation, SOS says to wait one business day and then file the DOR Business License Application, which links the UBI number.
- **Recommendation:** start as a sole proprietor.

## Name search and registration

- **Trade names are registered with the Department of Revenue Business Licensing Service** (RCW 19.80), not a county. Required for a sole proprietor using any name other than the owner's full legal name, and for an LLC using a name other than its SOS name.
- Fee: **$5 per trade name** plus the Business License Application processing fee (DOR trade names page, seen 2026-11-26). Filed on the Business License Application in My DOR. No expiry; cancel at no charge. Registering does not give exclusive rights. No newspaper publication.
- Name searches: DOR business lookup, SOS Corporations data search, USPTO.

## Federal IDs

- EIN optional for a sole proprietor with no employees (see us-federal.md). Washington issues its own 9-digit **UBI number** with the business license.

## Sales tax

- **No separate permit.** Retail sales tax registration ("Tax Registration" state endorsement, $0) is part of the **Business License Application** to DOR's Business Licensing Service. Processing fee **$50** to open a new business; $10 for other changes such as adding a trade name; renewal $5 plus endorsement fees; late renewal penalty up to $150 (variable processing fees page, seen 2026-11-26). Online processing about 10 business days, longer with endorsements; mail up to 6 weeks.
- A license is required if any apply: you need endorsements; you trade under a name other than your full legal name; you hire within 90 days; you collect sales tax; gross income is $12,000 a year or more; you owe DOR taxes or fees.
- **Rate:** state **6.5%** plus local rate by city/county; destination-based (where the customer receives goods). DOR Tax Rate Lookup Tool. Washington is a Streamlined Sales Tax member.
- **Filing frequency:** assigned in the welcome letter from estimated liability: annual ($1,050 or less), quarterly ($1,051–$4,800), monthly ($4,801 or more). Monthly returns due the 25th of the next month; quarterly by the end of the month after the quarter; annual by April 15. One combined Excise Tax Return (sales tax and B&O) filed electronically in My DOR. Active non-reporting status is not available to businesses that must collect retail sales tax.
- **Reseller permit:** Washington's resale document. No fee. Requires a WA business license. Valid generally four years, two years if open under 12 months. Apply in My DOR. Misuse penalty 50%. Not for items used in the business.
- **Remote sellers:** more than **$100,000** in combined gross receipts sourced to Washington in the current or prior calendar year; no transaction count (removed 2019). Register and collect from the first day of the month starting at least 30 days later.

## State taxes on the business and owner

- **No individual income tax** (DOR income tax page, "Washington does not currently have an individual income tax"). SB 6346 (2026) creates a 9.9% tax on individual income over $1 million starting January 1, 2028, first returns April 2029; not relevant to a small seller now. No city income tax found.
- **B&O tax:** gross receipts tax, no deduction for costs, not passed to customers as a separate tax. Retailing rate **0.471%**; wholesaling 0.484%; service classifications higher (classification rates page, seen 2026-11-26). **Small Business B&O Credit** reduces or removes B&O when liability is below about $110 a month, $330 a quarter or $1,320 a year (credit table, from 2023), calculated automatically on the return.
- **City B&O:** many cities levy their own B&O tax, administered by the city (DOR city B&O page). Seattle, Tacoma, Bellevue, Everett and others file through FileLocal.
- Personal property listing with the county assessor by April 30 each year (DOR BLS FAQ); details by county **Not confirmed**.

## Licenses and permits

- State business license from DOR BLS (above). Renews annually if it carries city or state endorsements.
- **City endorsements:** many cities license through BLS (DOR city endorsements page lists them). Cities on FileLocal (Auburn, Bellevue, Des Moines, Everett, Kent, Lake Forest Park, Poulsbo, Renton, Seattle, Shoreline, Tacoma) license and collect city B&O through filelocal-wa.gov. Other cities: contact the city. Fees vary by city: **Not confirmed** (not researched city by city).
- County endorsements only for unincorporated Asotin and Franklin counties (DOR county endorsements page).
- Home business zoning: set by each city or county.

## Registered agent / address privacy

- LLC must have a Washington registered agent with a physical address; the agent's address is public on SOS records. A sole proprietor's license address appears in DOR's business lookup.

## Privacy law

- **No comprehensive consumer privacy law.** HB 1671 (2025–26) was "By resolution, reintroduced and retained in present status" in the 2026 session (bill summary, seen 2026-11-26).
- **My Health My Data Act** (RCW 19.373): applies to any business, of any size, that collects consumer health data; small businesses comply from June 30, 2024. Requires a separate consumer health data privacy policy linked from the homepage. The AG notes purchases can produce health data when used to infer health status.
- **Breach notice (RCW 19.255.010):** applies to any person or business that conducts business in Washington and owns or licenses personal information.

## Employer registrations (brief)

- Hiring within 90 days triggers the business license requirement; the BLA also registers for Employment Security (unemployment insurance), Labor & Industries (workers' comp) and Paid Family and Medical Leave. Out of scope for a solo seller.

## Ordered checklist

1. Choose sole proprietorship (recommended) or LLC; pick a name; check DOR and SOS lookups.
2. LLC only: file Certificate of Formation ($180) with SOS, with the free initial report; note the annual report month. Sole proprietor using a trade name: register it on the BLA ($5).
3. EIN if needed (shared).
4. File the Business License Application with DOR BLS ($50 processing): tax registration, trade name, city endorsements. Get the UBI. Apply for a reseller permit in My DOR (no fee). File excise returns (sales tax and B&O) on the assigned schedule.
5. Check city endorsements, FileLocal city license and city B&O, county endorsement (Asotin/Franklin), zoning.
6. Other states (shared).
7. Insurance (shared).
8. Bank account (shared).
9. Bookkeeping and taxes (shared); note B&O.
10. Payment processor (shared).
11. Legal pages and privacy (shared); MHMDA if health data.
12. Product compliance (shared).
13. Supplier account using the Washington reseller permit (shared).

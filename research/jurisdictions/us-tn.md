State-specific steps for a solo retailer in Tennessee. Federal steps (EIN, Schedule C/SE, quarterly federal estimates, nexus in other states, insurance, banking, FTC, CPSC/FDA) are in `us-federal.md` and `content/shared/us/`. Researched from official pages only: the Secretary of State (sos.tn.gov, Division of Business and Charitable Organizations), the Department of Revenue (tn.gov/revenue) and the Attorney General (tn.gov/attorneygeneral). sos.tn.gov and tn.gov load with curl. The TNCaB filing portal (tncab.tnsos.gov) sits behind Cloudflare and was not read.

Tennessee's oddities for a solo seller: no tax on wage or business income for individuals, but an **LLC pays franchise and excise tax** (minimum $100 a year) even with one owner, plus a $300 minimum annual report. Every in-state business with $3,000 or more in gross sales needs a county (and often city) **business license**, $15 each.

## Business structure options

- **Sole proprietorship:** no state formation filing. Registration with the Department of Revenue is under the owner's legal name; a DBA "may be entered during the registration process" (DOR General Business Set-Up Information, Sole Proprietorship). Sole proprietors are not subject to franchise and excise tax. Recommended to start.
- **LLC (Secretary of State, Division of Business and Charitable Organizations; file online through TNCaB or on paper):**
  - Articles of Organization, Form **SS-4270**: **$50 per member, minimum $300, maximum $3,000** (SS-4270 form instructions; Forms & Fees page lists "$300*", seen 2026-11-26). A single-member LLC pays $300. A convenience fee applies to card and e-check payments online.
  - Name reservation, Form SS-9425: **$20** (Forms & Fees page).
  - Registered agent and registered office with a physical Tennessee street address are required (SS-4270). Information on filed forms is public record (T.C.A. § 10-7-503, noted on SOS forms).
  - **Annual report:** due on or before the first day of the fourth month after the fiscal year ends (April 1 for a December year-end). Fee **$300 minimum up to $3,000**, plus $50 per member over 6 (SOS FAQs, "most common reasons for rejection of an Annual Report"). Failure to file can lead to administrative dissolution. SOS warns of third-party solicitations about annual reports.
  - **Franchise and excise tax (DOR):** applies to LLCs registered or doing business in Tennessee. A single-member LLC is not disregarded for F&E unless its single member is a corporation (F&E Tax Manual June 2026; Notice 13-16), so an individual's single-member LLC files Form FAE 170. Franchise tax 0.25% of Tennessee net worth, **minimum $100**; excise tax **6.5%** of Tennessee taxable income. Due the 15th day of the fourth month after year end (April 15 for a calendar year), 7-month extension available. Quarterly estimates required when combined liability is $5,000 or more in both the prior and current year. Some LLCs (for example obligated member entities) can claim an exemption on Form FAE 183. (DOR Franchise & Excise Tax page and manual.)
  - Yearly cost for a single-member LLC: $300 annual report plus at least $100 franchise tax (plus excise tax on any profit).
  - Name rule: must contain "Limited Liability Company", "LLC" or "L.L.C." (T.C.A. § 48-249-106) and be distinguishable on the records of the Secretary of State. Words such as "bank" or "trust" need Department of Financial Institutions approval.
- Partnerships and corporations: out of scope for a solo retailer.
- **Recommendation:** start as a sole proprietor; weigh the LLC's $400+ yearly floor (annual report plus franchise minimum) before forming one.

## Name search and registration

- **Sole proprietor:** no state DBA filing. The SOS FAQ says "Tennessee does not recognize 'DBAs' (doing business as) or fictitious names"; its Assumed Name filing is for entities only. A sole proprietor's trade name is entered when registering with the Department of Revenue (TNTAP) and, per secondary sources, on the county clerk business license. Whether any county keeps a separate trade-name register: **Not confirmed** (no official county page read). Guide tells readers to ask their county clerk.
- SOS Business Name Availability search covers chartered entities (LLCs, corporations) only.
- **LLC using another name:** Application for Registration of Assumed Name, Form **SS-4402, $20**, good for **5 years**, renewable (SS-4481, $20) (SOS FAQs; Forms & Fees page).

## Federal IDs

Same as federal. Tennessee issues no separate business number beyond the TNTAP account and sales tax account number.

## Sales tax

- **Certificate of Registration** from the **Tennessee Department of Revenue**, registered online through **TNTAP** (tntap.tn.gov). Required before conducting business, one per location (Sales and Use Tax Manual, August 2026).
- **Fee:** no fee listed for a regular sales tax registration. **Not confirmed** that it is free. (Flea market dealers not otherwise registered pay $45 a year, $15 a quarter or $5 a month: manual.)
- **Rates:** state **7%** for most tangible personal property and taxable services; food **4%** (Due Dates and Tax Rates page). Every local jurisdiction adds a local rate, up to **2.75%**, in 0.25% steps. Local tax applies only to the first $1,600 of a single article; an extra **state single article tax of 2.75%** applies to the portion from $1,600.01 to $3,200.
- **Sourcing:** within Tennessee, sales are sourced to the seller's place of business for local tax (origin), T.C.A. § 67-6-903 per the manual. Sales delivered outside Tennessee are not Tennessee sales. Ask an accountant for edge cases.
- **Filing frequency:** monthly is the default for most taxpayers; quarterly allowed where average liability is $1,000 or less a month for 12 consecutive months; annual mainly for some manufacturers, wholesalers and marketplace-only sellers. Due on the 20th of the following month (quarterly: January 20, April 20, July 20, October 20). File electronically, even with no sales.
- **Resale certificate:** the **Tennessee Sales and Use Tax Certificate of Resale** is issued automatically on registration and printed from TNTAP; give a copy to suppliers (Resale Certificate page).
- **Economic nexus for out-of-state sellers:** more than **$100,000** in sales to Tennessee customers in the previous 12 months (no transaction count). Marketplace facilitators collect for their sellers.
- Tennessee is an associate member of Streamlined Sales Tax (since 2005).

## State taxes on the business and owner

- **No individual income tax on wages or business income.** The Hall income tax on interest and dividends was repealed for tax periods beginning on or after January 1, 2021 (DOR Hall Income Tax page). No estimated state income tax for a sole proprietor.
- No local income tax found in Tennessee (none on any DOR page read).
- **Business tax (DOR, collected with county and city clerks):** gross-receipts tax for in-state businesses with a standard business license (over $100,000 in gross sales). Minimum tax $22. Retail rates depend on classification (for example 0.0015 for Class 2, 0.001875 for Class 3). Due the 15th day of the fourth month after year end. City business tax applies only where the city has enacted it.
- LLC: franchise and excise tax, above.

## Licenses and permits

- **County and city business license** (DOR "Step 2: Getting Your Licenses and Permits", seen 2026-11-26): "Licenses are obtained from the city clerk and county clerk where the business is located. Each license is $15."
  - Under $3,000 in gross sales: no business license.
  - $3,000–$100,000: minimum activity license; no business tax account or filing; renew with the county clerk.
  - More than $100,000: standard business license; register for business tax in TNTAP and file yearly, which renews the license.
  - Threshold raised to $100,000 by Public Chapter 377 (2023).
- Alcohol: Alcoholic Beverage Commission licenses. Wholesale tobacco: DOR license.
- Local zoning and home-occupation rules: local, not researched.

## Registered agent / address privacy

- An LLC must name a registered agent at a physical Tennessee address (no PO box). Information on SOS filings is public record.
- Business license and business tax records with the clerks: public status not read.

## Privacy law

- **Tennessee Information Protection Act** took effect **July 1, 2025** (Attorney General press release pr25-25). It applies to businesses with revenue over **$25 million** that either control or process data of **25,000** or more Tennessee consumers and get more than 50% of gross revenue from selling personal information, or control or process data of **175,000** or more consumers in a calendar year. A small online store will not meet it.
- **Breach notification:** T.C.A. § 47-18-2107 applies to any "information holder" (any business holding computerized personal information). The notice deadline (reported as 45 days) is from secondary sources only: **Not confirmed** on an official page.

## Employer registrations (brief)

Employers register for unemployment insurance with the Department of Labor and Workforce Development and may need workers' compensation coverage. Out of scope for a solo retailer.

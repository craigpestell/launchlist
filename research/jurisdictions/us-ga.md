State-specific steps for a solo retailer in Georgia. Federal steps (EIN, Schedule C/SE, quarterly federal estimates, nexus in other states, insurance, banking, FTC, CPSC/FDA) are in `us-federal.md` and `content/shared/us/`. Researched from official pages only. sos.ga.gov and ecorp.sos.ga.gov (Secretary of State) sit behind a Cloudflare bot check that blocks curl and returns 403 to WebFetch, so Secretary of State facts come from georgia.gov (the state portal, which loaded) and from search-result excerpts of SOS pages, marked as such. dor.georgia.gov, georgia.gov, legis.ga.gov, gov.georgia.gov and county clerk sites loaded with curl.

## Business structure options

- **Sole proprietorship:** owner is the business; personal assets at risk; income on the owner's return. "Sole proprietorships are not registered with the Georgia Secretary of State" (georgia.gov starting a business guide, seen 2026-11-26). Recommended to start.
- **LLC (Secretary of State, Corporations Division):**
  - Articles of Organization (form CD 030 plus transmittal form 231 by mail; online via ecorp.sos.ga.gov). georgia.gov/register-llc (last updated June 2026): **$100 online by credit card, $110 by mail** (check or money order). Processing 7 business days online, 15 by mail. Expedite: +$100 (2 business days), +$250 (same day), +$1,000 (1 hour, not online).
  - **Conflict:** the SOS fee schedule ("Rev. 8/2025", effective September 6, 2025; seen only as a search excerpt, PDF blocked) lists Articles of Organization at $110 online and paper ($100 fee plus $10 service charge). Guide says "$100 online or $110 by mail" per georgia.gov with a confirm nudge.
  - Registered agent must be located in Georgia (physical street address).
  - **Annual registration:** every year, deadline **April 1**; window January 1–April 1; first due the year after formation (SOS excerpt). Multi-year option (2 or 3 years). Mail adds a $10 service charge; mail must be postmarked by April 1 to avoid a **$25 late fee**. Non-filing risks administrative dissolution (georgia.gov/renew-llc). Fee: **$50 base, $60 with the $10 service charge** per SOS fee schedule excerpt; georgia.gov does not state the base fee. Guide says "$50 plus a $10 service charge".
  - No franchise tax or LLC minimum tax found for a single-member LLC (Georgia has a corporate net worth tax for corporations, out of scope).
  - Name reservation: **$25 online, $35 paper**, held 30 days (georgia.gov/reserve-business-name). SOS business search is for research.
  - Name rule (must contain LLC designator, distinguishable on records): wording **Not confirmed** on an official page (SOS name availability standards page blocked). Guide wording kept general.
- Partnerships and corporations: out of scope.
- **Recommendation:** start as a sole proprietor.

## Name search and registration

- **Trade name registration (O.C.G.A. 10-1-490):** anyone carrying on business under a trade name that does not disclose the owner files a verified (notarized) registration statement with the **clerk of superior court in the county where the business is chiefly carried on**. Timing differs by clerk: Fayette says within 30 days of commencing; Gwinnett says before commencing.
- **Publication:** notice in the county legal organ **once a week for two weeks** (Gwinnett, Fayette, DeKalb clerks).
- Unregistered trade name use is a misdemeanor (O.C.G.A. 10-1-493, per Gwinnett clerk). Registration gives no exclusive rights.
- Fees (county-set, seen 2026-11-26):
  - Gwinnett: $172 filing plus $40 publication fee for the Gwinnett Daily Post.
  - DeKalb (fee sheet dated 7/1/2024): $174, e-filed through eFileGA only; publication paid directly to the Champion Newspaper; search the Trade Name Index first.
  - Thomas County: $175 (search excerpt only).
  - Jones County: clerk does not collect publication fees.
  - Other counties: **Not confirmed**. Guide gives "about $172–$175" with a varies-by-county nudge.
- **Watch: HB 158 (2025–26)** would create a statewide trade name registry through GSCCCA effective January 1, 2027. Passed the House March 2026; not in the Legislative Counsel 2026 summary of enacted acts, so treated as **not law**. Recheck before 2027.

## Federal IDs

Same as federal. Georgia issues no separate business number; the sales tax account number comes from GTC registration. Sole proprietors register on GTC with SSN.

## Sales tax

- Name: **sales and use tax number and certificate of registration** ("Georgia Sales & Use Tax certificate"), Georgia Department of Revenue, via the **Georgia Tax Center (GTC)**. Account number usually by email within 15 minutes. Every "dealer" (O.C.G.A. 48-8-2) must register even if all sales are online, out of state, wholesale or exempt (O.C.G.A. 48-8-59). Certificate must be conspicuously displayed. No renewal needed.
- Fee: DOR lists "registration fees or taxes (depending on the tax types required)" generally; no sales tax registration fee stated. **No fee listed**.
- Sole proprietor registration: as an individual with SSN, most recent federal AGI and NAICS code.
- Rate: state **4%** plus local (LOST, SPLOST, ESPLOST, MARTA, TSPLOST, Atlanta MOST). Rate chart effective October 1, 2026: combined about 6% (e.g. Cobb, Gwinnett) to 9%; City of Atlanta 8.9%. Chart updated quarterly. Destination sourcing: county where the customer takes delivery; collect local rate even without presence there (O.C.G.A. 48-8-77).
- Filing: returns due the **20th of the month** after the period; "for most taxpayers, sales tax returns are due every month"; frequency change by written request. File zero returns. E-file required if more than $500 due on any return; otherwise paper ST-3. Keep records at least 3 years. Prepaid estimated tax if prior-year liability exceeded $60,000.
- Resale: **Form ST-5 Certificate of Exemption**; most do not expire; DOR has a Sales Tax ID Verification Tool.
- Economic nexus: **more than 200 separate sales transactions or at least $100,000** in Georgia sales in the previous calendar year (DOR "How do I know if I need to collect", Policy Bulletin SUT-2019-02). Marketplace-only sellers may not need to register.
- Casual sales not taxable (Rule 560-12-1-.07).

## State taxes on the business and owner

- Individual income tax: flat **4.99% for 2026**; standard deduction $15,000 single / $30,000 joint (DOR Important Tax Updates). HB 463 (Act 465) continues step-downs toward 3.99%.
- Single-member LLC: disregarded; income on owner's return.
- Estimated tax: Form 500-ES, due April 15, June 15, September 15, January 15; required when income not subject to withholding exceeds exemptions plus deductions plus $1,000 (500-ES instructions, search excerpt).
- City or county income tax: none found; **Not confirmed** on an official page. Guide does not mention local income tax.
- Local occupation tax (business license) under O.C.G.A. 48-13; HB 141 (Act 435, effective July 1, 2026) lets businesses give CPA affidavits instead of tax returns to local governments for occupation tax.

## Licenses and permits

- No general state business license found. georgia.gov: some businesses need specialized permits at various levels; points to SOS licensing information (blocked).
- Local: city or county (if unincorporated) business license / occupation tax certificate. Rules and fees vary; no single official page covers all. No step 05 capture script.
- Home-based: local zoning; varies.

## Registered agent / address privacy

LLC registered agent must have a physical street address in Georgia where a person can be found. Trade name registrations are public court records and are published in the newspaper with the owner's name and address; ask the clerk what address may be used.

## Privacy law

- No comprehensive consumer privacy law. SB 111 (2025–26) privacy act provisions were stripped; the signed SB 111 (Act 462, signed May 11, 2026) only amends the rural hospital tax credit (gov.georgia.gov signed text). Not in the 2026 enacted summary.
- Breach notice law (O.C.G.A. 10-1-910 to 912) applies to "information brokers" and "data collectors": scope from memory, **Not confirmed** on an official page. Guide says it covers certain kinds of businesses and to ask.

## Employer registrations (brief)

Withholding and unemployment through DOR (GTC) and the Georgia Department of Labor; workers' compensation required with 3 or more employees (georgia.gov). Out of scope for a solo seller.

## Ordered checklist

1. Decide sole proprietorship (recommended) or LLC; LLC: Articles of Organization ($100 online / $110 mail).
2. Trade name: search county index, notarize, file with clerk of superior court (about $172–$175), publish twice in the legal organ. LLC: annual registration by April 1 each year.
3. EIN (shared).
4. Register for sales and use tax on GTC; display certificate; file monthly by the 20th; ST-5 for resale.
5. City or county business license / occupation tax; zoning; any product license.
6–13. Shared steps.

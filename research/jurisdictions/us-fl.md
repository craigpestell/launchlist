## Business structure options

- **Sole proprietorship:** no state filing to exist. The owner is personally liable. Florida exempts sole proprietorships and individuals from the corporate income tax return (DOR GT-300015, R. 11/25).
- **LLC:** formed by filing Articles of Organization with the Division of Corporations (Department of State), called Sunbiz. Fee **$125**: $100 for the articles plus $25 for the registered agent designation (s. 605.0213(2) and (7), F.S.; form CR2E047 "FILING FEES: $125.00 Filing Fee for Articles of Organization and Designation of Registered Agent"). Optional certified copy $30, certificate of status $5. Seen 2026-11-26.
- **LLC yearly cost:** annual report **$138.75** ($50 report fee under s. 605.0213(5) plus $88.75 supplemental corporate fee under s. 607.193(1)), filed online **January 1 to May 1** each year, starting the year after formation (s. 605.0212(3)). **$400 late fee** after May 1 (s. 607.193(2)(b)); the form says there is no provision to waive it. Seen 2026-11-26.
- **Registered agent:** an LLC must name a registered agent with a Florida street address (form CR2E047).
- **Corporation:** $70 to file articles and designate an agent ($35 + $35), annual report $150 (form CR2E010). Not recommended for a solo retailer starting out.
- **Recommendation:** start as a sole proprietorship; consider an LLC once liability or growth justifies $125 plus $138.75 a year.

## State income tax

- **No personal income tax.** Florida Constitution, Art. VII, s. 5(a): no tax on the income of natural persons who are residents or citizens beyond what can be credited against a federal tax. (flsenate.gov constitution page, seen 2026-11-26.)
- **Corporate income/franchise tax:** 5.5% for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2022 (GT-300015). Applies to corporations and to LLCs classified as corporations for federal and Florida purposes. **A single-member LLC disregarded for federal tax purposes does not file a separate Florida corporate income tax return** (unless owned by a corporation). Sole proprietorships and individuals are exempt. An LLC taxed as a partnership files Form F-1065 only if an owner is a corporation. (GT-300015, R. 11/25.)
- Effect on the guide: a sole proprietor or single-member LLC owner has no Florida income tax to register for. An LLC that elects corporate tax treatment would file Form F-1120 with DOR: point to an accountant.

## Name search and registration

- **Fictitious name (DBA):** required for any business transacted under a name other than the person's legal name (s. 865.09(2)(c), (3)(a)). Filed with the Division of Corporations, online or on form CR4E001 (10/20).
- **Fee: $50** to register, $50 to renew, $30 certified copy, $10 certificate of status (s. 865.09(12); form CR4E001). Seen 2026-11-26.
- **Newspaper notice:** before registering, the registrant certifies the intention to register has been **advertised at least once in a newspaper in the county of the principal place of business** (s. 865.09(3)(a)5). Newspaper cost varies: Not confirmed, local.
- **Term:** valid until December 31 of the fifth calendar year (counting the year of registration as the first); renew between January 1 and December 31 of the expiration year; the Division sends a notice by September 1 (s. 865.09(5), (6)).
- **No name protection:** registration is for public notice only and does not reserve the name (s. 865.09(8)).
- **Name restrictions:** a fictitious name may not contain "Inc.", "Corp.", "LLC" and similar unless the registrant is that kind of entity (s. 865.09(14)).
- **LLCs:** an LLC registered with the Division needs no fictitious name filing unless it trades under a different name (s. 865.09(7)). The LLC name must contain "limited liability company", "LLC" or "L.L.C." and be distinguishable on the Division's records; differences in suffix, articles, "and"/"&", plural/possessive or punctuation don't count (s. 605.0112).
- **Penalty:** a business that fails to register can't bring a lawsuit in Florida courts until it complies; failure is a noncriminal violation (s. 865.09(9)).
- **Name search:** Sunbiz search, search.sunbiz.org (blocked to automated fetch; URL from official links).
- **Online processing time:** a third-party search summary of the Division's e-file page says registrations post in 24–48 hours. **Not confirmed** (page blocked).

## Federal IDs

- No Florida difference. The LLC annual report asks for the company's EIN, or whether one has been applied for (s. 605.0212(1)(d)).

## Sales tax

- **Name of the permit:** Florida's **Certificate of Registration (Form DR-11)**, issued by the **Florida Department of Revenue** when you register as a sales and use tax dealer. Registration also issues the **Florida Annual Resale Certificate for Sales Tax (Form DR-13)**. (GT-300015.)
- **How:** online through the Florida Business Tax Application (taxapps.floridarevenue.com/taxregistration, needs a user profile) or paper Form DR-1. Register each Florida business location. (DOR Account Registration page.)
- **Fee:** none listed on DOR's registration pages, GT-300015 or DR-1N. **Not confirmed** that registration is free. (Failing to register when required carries a $100 registration fee under s. 212.18(3)(d).)
- **State rate: 6%** general rate (DOR sales tax page, seen 2026-11-26).
- **Local rates add:** most counties impose a **discretionary sales surtax** on top of 6%, based on the county where the item is delivered. For calendar 2026 (DR-15DSS, R. 11/25) county totals range from none (e.g. Citrus, Collier) to 2% (Hamilton). For certain transactions only the first $5,000 of a sale is subject to surtax. The 2027 form had not loaded on 2026-11-26 (DOR updates it each November).
- **Filing frequency (GT-300015):** based on annual sales tax collections. More than $1,000 monthly; $501–$1,000 quarterly; $101–$500 semiannual; $100 or less annual. **Most new businesses start on quarterly** unless they request otherwise. Returns are due on the 1st and late after the 20th of the month after each period. File even when no tax is due (DOR sales tax page).
- **Collection allowance:** filing and paying electronically on time gives 2.5% of the first $1,200 of tax due, up to $30 (GT-300015).
- **Resale certificate:** the Annual Resale Certificate (DR-13), issued automatically, expires December 31 and is reissued each year (available online each November). Give a copy to suppliers; don't use it for business supplies (use tax applies). Sellers can verify it by phone 877-357-3725, online or app. (DOR Annual Resale Certificate page.)
- **Economic nexus (out-of-state sellers):** since July 1, 2021, a business with no physical presence must collect if it made **taxable remote sales into Florida over $100,000 in the previous calendar year**. Marketplace providers collect on sales they facilitate. (DOR sales tax and registration pages.) No transaction count test is stated.

## State taxes and fees on the business itself

- LLC annual report $138.75, January 1–May 1, $400 late fee (above).
- Fictitious name renewal $50 every five years (above).
- Corporate income tax only for LLCs taxed as corporations (above).
- No franchise tax or gross receipts tax for a general retailer found. (Florida has gross receipts taxes on utilities, communications and dry-cleaning only, per DR-1N.)

## Local licenses and permits

- **Local business tax receipt:** counties (s. 205.032) and cities (s. 205.042) may levy a local business tax. Where both levy, you may need a receipt from each. Amount set by each county or city: **local, Not confirmed**.
- **Dates:** receipts go on sale July 1, are due by September 30 and expire September 30 the following year. Late renewal penalty 10% for October plus 5% a month, capped at 25%. Operating without a required receipt: penalty of 25% of the tax due. (s. 205.053.)
- **Fictitious name link:** to get a receipt you must show your current fictitious name registration or a signed statement why you don't need one (s. 205.023).
- **Home-based businesses:** s. 559.955 bars local governments from licensing or regulating a qualifying home-based business differently from other businesses; it is subject only to local business taxes in its county and city. Conditions include residential character, parking, at most two non-resident employees working at the home, and no retail transactions at a structure other than the dwelling.
- **Zoning, signs, health permits:** local; check with the city or county.
- No statewide general business license for a retailer of ordinary goods was found. Some products (alcohol, tobacco, regulated professions) need state licenses; not researched.

## Registered agent / address privacy

- An LLC's articles list a registered agent with a Florida street address; annual reports list the principal office street address, mailing address and a manager (s. 605.0212(1)). Division of Corporations records are searchable online (Sunbiz). A fictitious name registration lists the business mailing address and each registrant's name and address (s. 865.09(3)(a)).
- DOR registration information is confidential (DOR registration app notice).

## Consumer privacy law

- **Florida Digital Bill of Rights** (ss. 501.701–501.722, F.S.): its main duties apply to a "controller" that, among other things, makes **more than $1 billion in global gross annual revenue** and meets one of several further tests (s. 501.702(9)). A small online store won't meet it.
- **Exception with no size threshold:** s. 501.715 applies to any for-profit business that conducts business in Florida and collects consumer personal data. If it **sells sensitive personal data** it needs prior consent and must post "NOTICE: This website may sell your sensitive personal data."
- **Data breach law** (s. 501.171): applies to any commercial entity, including sole proprietorships, that holds personal information. It must take reasonable measures to protect it, notify affected Florida individuals no later than 30 days after determining a breach, and notify the Department of Legal Affairs if 500 or more Floridians are affected.

## Employer and other registrations

- Reemployment (unemployment) tax registration with DOR if you hire (GT-300015). Out of scope for a solo retailer.

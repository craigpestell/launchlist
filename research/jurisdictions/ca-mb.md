## Business structure

- **Sole proprietorship** (recommended for a solo retailer to start). "The owner is personally liable for all business debts." "If you operate your business under your own given name (first name and last name), you do not have to register a business name." Source: https://www.gov.mb.ca/jec/busdev/business/business_structures.html
- Partnerships and Manitoba corporations must register with the Companies Office.

## Name search and registration

- Who: sole proprietors need not register when operating under their own surname, as long as the name "does not indicate a plurity [sic] of persons in the business". Source: https://companiesoffice.gov.mb.ca/starting_business_name.html
- Name reservation: "A Name Reservation is required, in most cases, before a name can be registered." $45 regular, $90 expedited; filed online; rejected names need a new request and fee; approved names are held 90 days. Sources: https://companiesoffice.gov.mb.ca/forms_business.html, https://companiesoffice.gov.mb.ca/name_reservations.html
- Registration: Business Name Registration, online or paper, $60 regular, $120 expedited. "Online filing provides the fastest processing times." Source: https://companiesoffice.gov.mb.ca/forms_business.html
- Renewal: every three years, $60. Source: same page.
- Name guidelines: https://companiesoffice.gov.mb.ca/instruction_sheets/choosing_business_name.pdf

## Federal ID

CRA Business Number through Business Registration Online, signed in with a CRA account. Shared with the other Canadian guides: https://www.canada.ca/en/services/taxes/business-registration.html

## Sales tax

- **GST 5%** (federal) plus **Manitoba Retail Sales Tax (RST) 7%**. "The tax rate is 7 per cent", calculated on the price before GST. Source: https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/register.pdf (dated January 2026)
- GST: must register past $30,000 over four consecutive calendar quarters; voluntary registration allowed. Source: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html (fetched 2026-10-07 for Alberta)
- RST registration is required for businesses selling taxable goods at retail in Manitoba. Exception: "your business's annual taxable sales volume is under $30,000, and you buy all goods from sellers registered for Manitoba retail sales tax." The exception does not apply to businesses that "use out of province suppliers that do not collect sales tax". Registration is online through TAXcess (https://taxcess.gov.mb.ca/) and "There is no charge for registering for a tax account." Source: register.pdf above.
- Resale: "Vendors must quote their 7-digit RST number when buying goods or services tax-free for resale." Source: Bulletin RST 004, Information for Vendors (revised June 2024), https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/004.pdf

## Licences

No provincial general business licence.

- **Winnipeg** licenses 11 business activities under the Community Safety Business Licensing By-law (bicycle dealer, flea market, precious metals dealer, used goods dealer and others). "You do not require a business licence if you work from home." Source: https://legacy.winnipeg.ca/cms/BLES/LS/business_licenses/default.stm
- Winnipeg requires a **home-based business development permit** under the Zoning By-law if business mail comes to the home or administrative work is done there. Source: https://legacy.winnipeg.ca/ppd/permits/Residential/HomeBusiness.stm
- Other municipalities: check locally.

## Consumer protection and privacy

- Manitoba's Consumer Protection Act (https://web2.gov.mb.ca/laws/statutes/ccsm/c200.php) has rules for internet agreements. Details were not reviewed; step 11 only points to them.
- Manitoba has no private-sector privacy law in force, so the guide uses PIPEDA as for Ontario. Not separately verified on a Manitoba page.

## Ordered checklist

1. Structure and name
2. Reserve and register a business name (if not trading under own name)
3. CRA Business Number
4. GST (5%)
5. Manitoba RST (7%)
6. Municipal licence and zoning (Winnipeg: home-based business development permit)
7. Insurance
8. Bank account
9. Bookkeeping and income tax
10. Payment processor
11. Legal pages and privacy
12. Product compliance
13. Supplier account

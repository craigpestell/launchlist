State-specific steps for a solo retailer in Missouri. Federal steps (EIN, Schedule C/SE, quarterly federal estimates, nexus in other states, insurance, banking, FTC, CPSC/FDA) are in `us-federal.md` and `content/shared/us/`. Researched from official pages only. sos.mo.gov, dor.mo.gov, stlouis-mo.gov and revisor.mo.gov loaded with curl. kcmo.gov (Kansas City) returns 403 Access Denied (Akamai) to curl and WebFetch; Kansas City facts below come from search-result summaries of kcmo.gov pages only.

Kansas City and the City of St. Louis each levy a 1% earnings tax and license businesses. These are marked **KC only** or **St. Louis City only** and must never be presented as statewide.

## Business structure options

- **Sole proprietorship:** "Sole proprietorships and general partnerships can be formed or created without the involvement of the Secretary of State. In fact, the Secretary of State does not receive or accept filings related to the creation of these business types." (SOS Starting a Business page.) Recommended to start.
- **LLC (Secretary of State, Corporations Division):**
  - Articles of Organization (Form LLC 1): **$50 online, $105 on paper** (SOS fee schedule fees.pdf; Corporations FAQ: "The savings for filing articles of organization for a limited liability company online is $55.00 ($50.00 online versus $105.00 by paper)"; LLC 1 form "Submit with filing fee of $105.00"; seen 2026-11-26). Filed online at bsd.sos.mo.gov.
  - **No annual report for LLCs.** Corporations FAQ: "Do limited liability companies have to file an annual report?" "No." (seen 2026-11-26).
  - No LLC franchise tax or minimum tax found. Missouri's franchise tax applies to corporations only (not relevant).
  - Registered agent required, with a Missouri street address (SOS general information; LLC 1 asks for the agent's street address, no PO Box alone). The agent and organizers' names and addresses appear on the public filing.
  - Name rule (LLC 1 form; RSMo 347.020): must include "Limited Liability Company", "Limited Company", "LC", "L.C.", "L.L.C." or "LLC", and be distinguishable from names on the SOS records.
  - Name reservation: **$25** for 60 days, renewable up to 180 days in total (SOS fee schedule and FAQ).
  - Yearly state cost for a single-member LLC: none found beyond keeping a registered agent.
- Partnerships and corporations: out of scope for a solo retailer.
- **Recommendation:** start as a sole proprietor; an LLC is cheap to form and has no annual report.

## Name search and registration

- **Fictitious name registration** is filed with the **Secretary of State (state level, not the county)**. RSMo 417.200: anyone transacting business under a name other than their true name must register. SOS Starting a Business page: "Missouri law requires any person or business entity which transacts business in the state under a name other than their own “true name” to register that business name with the Secretary of State’s Office as a Fictitious Name Registration."
  - Online at bsd.sos.mo.gov, or by mail/in person with **Form Corp. 56** (business name, business address, owners' names and percentage of ownership, signed by all owners).
  - Fee: **$7.00** (Fictitious Name FAQ; fee schedule "Fictitious Name Creation, Renewal, Correction or Amendment $7.00"; cancellation no fee; seen 2026-11-26).
  - Term: **good for five years**; renew in the six-month period before expiration. An expired registration can't be renewed; file a new one (new charter number).
  - Gives no exclusive right to the name. Failure to register is a misdemeanor (FAQ).
  - No newspaper publication requirement found.
  - An LLC trading under a name other than its registered name also files a fictitious name registration (RSMo 347.020 refers to chapter 417).
- Entity name search: bsd.sos.mo.gov Business Entity Search (fictitious names are searchable there).

## Federal IDs

Same as federal. Missouri issues a Missouri Tax ID (MOID) and a 4-digit PIN with the sales tax license welcome letter.

## Sales tax

- **Missouri Retail Sales Tax License** (DOR also says "retail sales license") from the **Missouri Department of Revenue**. Required before making retail sales of tangible personal property (Business Tax Registration page; Registration FAQ). Apply through Online New Business Registration (MyTax Missouri) or on **Form 2643, Missouri Tax Registration Application** (rev 02-2026). Needs each owner's SSN, date of birth and home address. Physical address is printed on the license (no PO Box). Form 2643 item 16 asks for temporary locations such as craft shows.
- Penalty for selling without a license: $500 for the first day, $100 per day after, up to $10,000; misdemeanor (Section 144.118; Registration FAQ).
- **Fee:** no fee is listed on any DOR page read or on Form 2643. **Not confirmed.** Guide says "No fee listed (confirm when you apply)".
- **Bond:** "Effective August 28, 2018, bonds are no longer required for businesses that collect and pay sales or vendors use tax. The department may require a bond in instances when the business is delinquent." (Registration FAQ.) Form 2643: bond not required unless requested by DOR.
- Processing: allow 10 business days for a paper retail sales application; online registration says 2–3 business days.
- **Rates:** state **4.225%** (Sales/Use Tax page). Cities, counties and special taxing districts (such as fire districts) add local sales tax. DOR has a Sales and Use Tax Lookup Tool and a county, city and district rate breakdown. Sellers with more than $500,000 a year in sales must show the total rate on receipts. Combined rates for specific places: not quoted.
- **Filing frequency** (Sales Tax Filing FAQ), based on state tax only: monthly if $500 or more a month (due the last day of the following month); quarterly if less than $500 a month (calendar quarters, due the last day of the month after the quarter); annual if less than $200 a quarter (due January 31). DOR assigns the frequency in the welcome letter and reviews it yearly. File even with no tax due. 2% timely payment allowance. Late filing 5% a month up to 25%. Sellers with 3 or more locations must e-file.
- **Resale certificate:** **Form 149, Sales and Use Tax Exemption Certificate**, with a box for purchases of tangible personal property for resale; Missouri retailers give their Missouri Tax ID number.
- **Economic nexus for out-of-state sellers** (Remote Seller and Marketplace Facilitator FAQ): gross receipts from taxable sales into Missouri over **$100,000** in the preceding 12 months, measured at the end of each calendar quarter; collect vendor's use tax no later than three months after the quarter closes. Effective January 1, 2023. Sellers selling only through a marketplace facilitator need not register.
- **Certificate of No Tax Due:** Section 144.083 RSMo; cities, counties and state agencies require it before issuing or renewing certain licenses. Free, printed online with MOID and PIN. Businesses making no retail sales are exempt (No Tax Due page).

## State taxes on the business and owner

- **State individual income tax:** Missouri taxes residents' income, including sole-proprietor and single-member-LLC profit, on Form MO-1040. 2026 rate chart (MO-1040ES_2026): $0 on the first $1,348 of Missouri taxable income, then 2.0% rising to a top rate of **4.7%** on income over $9,436. Year-changes page confirms 4.7% top rate for tax year 2025; 2025 law (HB 594) added a 100% capital gains subtraction. The 2026 chart is from the estimated tax form, not an announcement page: treat 2026 brackets as **Not confirmed** for the final return.
- **Estimated tax:** declaration required if Missouri estimated tax is expected to be **$100 or more** (Section 143.521.1; MO-1040ES). Due April 15, June 15, September 15 and January 15.
- **St. Louis City only:** 1% earnings tax on residents (wherever they work) and nonresidents working in the city. Businesses located in the city pay 1% of net profit (Form E-234) even with no employees; returns due April 15 (City Collector of Revenue, earnings tax pages).
- **KC only:** 1% earnings tax on residents' income and nonresidents' income earned in Kansas City; self-employed residents file a profits return (RD-108/108B) by April 15, even at a loss. Source: search summaries of kcmo.gov pages (site blocked): **Not confirmed** by direct reading.
- LLC: no annual report fee or franchise tax for a single-member LLC.

## Licenses and permits

- **No general statewide business license found.** mo.gov's business portal lists Professional Licensing (pr.mo.gov) and DOR registration but no general license. **Not confirmed** on an official page that none exists.
- Specific professions and activities are licensed by state boards (pr.mo.gov) or other agencies.
- Local: cities and counties may require a business license. DOR's No Tax Due page says cities and counties check the No Tax Due certificate before issuing or renewing licenses.
  - **KC only:** every business operating in Kansas City needs a city business license, sole proprietors included, renewed yearly (RD-105, or RD-103 for flat-rate industries). Search summary of kcmo.gov only: **Not confirmed** by direct reading.
  - **St. Louis City only:** has a License Collector; the business license page URL tried returned 404. Details **Not confirmed**.
- Local zoning and home-occupation rules: local, not researched.

## Registered agent / address privacy

- LLC: registered agent with a Missouri street address required; organizers' names and street addresses go on the LLC 1. Filings are public on bsd.sos.mo.gov.
- Fictitious name registration lists the owners' names, the business address and ownership percentages; public record.

## Privacy law

- **Breach notice law (RSMo 407.1500)** applies to any person that owns or licenses personal information of Missouri residents or conducts business in Missouri: notice to affected consumers after a breach. No size threshold.
- **No comprehensive consumer privacy law found.** Source: secondary trackers only. **Not confirmed** on an official page.

## Employer registrations (brief)

Employers register for withholding with DOR (same Form 2643 / online registration) and unemployment insurance with the Division of Employment Security (uinteract.labor.mo.gov). Out of scope for a solo retailer.

State-specific steps for a solo retailer in Arizona. Federal steps (EIN, Schedule C/SE, quarterly federal estimates, nexus in other states, insurance, banking, FTC, CPSC/FDA) are in `us-federal.md` and `content/shared/us/`. Researched from official pages only. The Arizona Corporation Commission (azcc.gov) and the Arizona Revised Statutes (azleg.gov) loaded with curl. The Secretary of State (azsos.gov), the Department of Revenue (azdor.gov), AZTaxes.gov, Business One Stop (businessonestop.az.gov), the Arizona Commerce Authority (azcommerce.com) and the Maricopa County Recorder all returned a Cloudflare 403 to both curl and WebFetch. Facts from those sites come from the statutes, from ACC pages that describe them, or from search-result summaries of their pages, and are flagged.

Arizona's sales tax is the **transaction privilege tax (TPT)**, a tax on the seller for the privilege of doing business, not a tax on the buyer. Cities also levy TPT, and ADOR licenses and collects for most of them.

## Business structure options

- **Sole proprietorship:** one owner, unlimited personal liability, income on the owner's return. No state filing to form one. Recommended to start.
- **LLC (Arizona Corporation Commission, Corporations Division):**
  - Articles of Organization: **$50** regular, **$85** expedited (ACC LLC fee schedule, Rev. 3.2026). ACC FAQ: "You can file Articles of Organization online for $85." Same-day / next-day services $100, $200 or $400 extra.
  - Optional name reservation: $10 ($45 expedited), lasts 120 days, not required (ACC FAQ, fee schedule).
  - **Statutory agent:** every LLC must appoint and keep one with a physical Arizona address; an individual agent must be 18 or older and live in Arizona; the agent must accept the appointment (online in the Arizona Business Center dashboard or on a paper acceptance form). Change of principal address or agent: $5 (ACC FAQ, fee schedule).
  - **Publication (A.R.S. 29-3201(G)):** within **60 days** after the ACC files the articles, publish a notice in a newspaper of general circulation in the county of the statutory agent's street address for **three consecutive publications** (affidavit of publication optional), **unless** the agent's street address is in a county with a population of more than 800,000, in which case the ACC enters the approval in its database (A.R.S. 10-130) and no newspaper notice is needed. ACC FAQ: "the A.C.C. will inform you by letter if your document is required to be published." That Maricopa and Pima are the counties over 800,000 is derived from Census population, **Not confirmed** on an ACC page. Newspaper charges: **Not confirmed** (set by papers).
  - **No annual report for LLCs** (ACC FAQ: "No, LLCs are not required to file annual reports. Only corporations are required to file annual reports."). No state franchise or LLC minimum tax found.
  - Reinstatement $100 (fee schedule).
  - All filings are public; the principal address is public and can be anywhere. The ACC suggests a P.O. Box or the agent's address for online businesses (ACC FAQ).
  - Name rule (A.R.S. 29-3112): must contain "limited liability company", "limited company", "L.L.C.", "LLC", "L.C." or "LC" and be distinguishable on the ACC's and the Secretary of State's records.
- **Recommendation:** sole proprietor to start. An LLC is cheap in Arizona ($50–$85, no annual report), but publication may apply outside Maricopa and Pima.

## Name search and registration

- **Trade name (Secretary of State):** A.R.S. 44-1460 says a person "may register" a trade name, so state registration is **optional**. Term 5 years, renewable within the 6 months before expiry (44-1460.02). Registration gives rights only against later corporate or LLC name filings; common-law rights are kept (44-1460.05). ACC 10-steps page: "A trade name is effective for 5 years. The Secretary of State registers trade names, not the A.C.C."
  - Fee: A.R.S. 41-126 caps the filing or renewal fee at **$10**, certificate $3, expedite up to $25. A search summary of the SOS trade name handbook says $10 and online filing. azsos.gov blocked, so the current fee is **Not confirmed** on an SOS page.
- **County DBA:** ACC FAQ: "The A.C.C. does not register or recognize DBAs... You can record 'DBA' names through the Maricopa County Recorder's Office." Per search summaries, the Maricopa Recorder records a Certificate of Fictitious Name/DBA under A.R.S. 11-480 and supplies no forms. Fee and whether other county recorders do the same: **Not confirmed** (recorder.maricopa.gov blocked).
- The ACC name availability check (Arizona Business Center) covers corporations, LLCs and trade names (ACC 10-steps page).
- Banks often ask for a trade name certificate for a business account under a business name (general; not from an Arizona page).

## Sales tax

- **Transaction privilege tax (TPT) license** from the **Arizona Department of Revenue**. A.R.S. 42-5005: a person "shall not engage or continue in business" until licensed. Apply on AZTaxes.gov, through Business One Stop, or on paper (Form JT-1) per search summaries of ADOR pages (blocked). AZTaxes is said to issue a license number the same day and mail the certificate in 7–10 business days (search summary, **Not confirmed**).
- **Fee (A.R.S. 42-5005):** **$12 per location** state fee, plus each city's municipal license fee of **up to $50 per city** set by city ordinance, collected by ADOR. Licenses run for the calendar year; no state renewal fee; city renewal fees (up to $50) due January 1, delinquent after the last business day of January. A separate license is needed for each location. City fees by city: **Not confirmed** (ADOR's city fee table blocked).
- **Rate:** retail classification **5%** (A.R.S. 42-5010) plus **0.6%** (42-5010.01, Proposition 123 education increment, June 30, 2021 to June 30, 2041), so **5.6%** state. Counties and cities add their own rates; ADOR's TPT Rate Look Up tool gives combined rates (blocked).
- **Filing (A.R.S. 42-5014):** monthly by default, due the 20th of the following month; electronic filers are delinquent after the last business day of that month. Quarterly if estimated annual liability is $2,000–$8,000; annual if under $2,000 (due January 20). Transient sellers at fairs or shows may be required to file per event. Returns are filed on AZTaxes.
- **Resale certificate:** **Form 5000A, Arizona Resale Certificate** (A.R.S. 42-5022); the buyer needs a TPT license; seller may rely on it in good faith. Per search summaries of ADOR PDFs (blocked): valid up to 48 months, Form 5000 is not for resale, sellers deduct with code 503. **Not confirmed** on an ADOR page.
- **Economic nexus (A.R.S. 42-5044):** remote sellers with more than **$100,000** of gross Arizona sales (excluding sales through a marketplace facilitator) in the previous or current calendar year must be licensed. City license fees don't apply to remote sellers or marketplace facilitators.

## State taxes on the business and owner

- **Individual income tax:** flat **2.5%** from tax year 2023 (ADOR forms page and 2026 Form 140ES via search summaries; azdor.gov blocked; azleg 43-1011 page still shows old brackets). Bills in 2026 to cut it to 2.42% or 2.47% found; none confirmed enacted: **Not confirmed**.
- **Estimated tax (A.R.S. 43-581):** required if Arizona gross income was over $75,000 ($150,000 joint) in both the current and prior year; no penalty if the tax due is under $1,000.
- **City income tax:** none found in Arizona. **Not confirmed** on an official page.
- A single-member LLC files nothing extra at the state level beyond the owner's return (no franchise or minimum tax found).

## Licenses and permits

- No general statewide business license found; the ACC 10-steps page (step 8) says to register with your city (or county if unincorporated) "for transaction privilege tax purposes and also for any business or occupational licenses that the city requires." Some cities (for example Phoenix, Tucson, Mesa) have their own requirements; not researched city by city.

## Registered agent / address privacy

- LLCs must have a statutory agent with a physical Arizona street address; all ACC filings are public, including the principal address (ACC FAQ). The ACC suggests a P.O. Box or the agent's address for an online business.
- A trade name registration and a county DBA are public records.

## Privacy law

- **No comprehensive consumer privacy law in force found.** SB 1815 (2026 session) introduced; no evidence of passage (secondary sources). **Not confirmed** on an official page.
- **Breach notice law, A.R.S. 18-552:** applies to any person conducting business in Arizona that owns, maintains or licenses unencrypted computerized personal information. Notice to individuals within **45 days** of determining a breach; if more than 1,000 individuals, also notify the Attorney General and the Arizona Department of Homeland Security (and the credit reporting agencies). Civil penalty up to $10,000 per affected individual, capped at $500,000 per breach.

## Employer registrations (brief)

Employers register for unemployment insurance with the Department of Economic Security and carry workers' compensation. Out of scope for a solo retailer; not researched.

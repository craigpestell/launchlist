## Business structure

- **Sole proprietorship** (recommended for a solo retailer to start). The Provincial Business Registration Starter Guide lists four common structures in PEI: sole proprietorship, partnership, corporation and cooperative, and recommends seeking legal advice before choosing. Registration is governed by the Partnership Act, Limited Partnerships Act, Business Corporations Act, Co-operative Associations Act and Extra-Provincial Corporations Registration Act. Source: https://www.princeedwardisland.ca/sites/default/files/publications/provincial_business_registration_-_starter_guide_en_0.pdf (PDF, undated)
- Provincial help: PEI Business Navigator, businessnavigator@gov.pe.ca, 1-833-335-0536 (starter guide).
- Screenshot candidate for step 1: the Starter Guide PDF above (opens directly, no click needed; page 1 has "Do I need to register my business?" and the structure list). The HTML structure pages on princeedwardisland.ca sit behind a bot check, so the PDF is the safer capture.

## Name search and registration

- **Who must register:** "you can operate a sole proprietorship without registering a business name only if it is operated under your own name. If a prefix or suffix of any kind is added to your own name to create the business name, you are required to register under the Partnership Act." Source: Starter Guide PDF above.
- **Name search / reservation:** required before registering any business name. A name approval request is made in the Online Corporate and Business Registry (OCBR), https://ocbr.princeedwardisland.ca, which needs a free account (desktop/laptop with Chrome or Firefox). The Registry does a preliminary review, then submits the name for a **NUANS** report itself and emails you a copy; an extra NUANS fee "may" apply if it shows a conflict. Source: Starter Guide PDF.
- **Fees** (no effective dates shown on the PDFs; both are undated):
  - Name search/reservation: **$40 (Atlantic Canada only)** or $50 (Canada-wide), non-refundable, by VISA, VISA Debit or MasterCard. Source: Starter Guide PDF.
  - Sole proprietorship registration application: **$90** (after the $40 name reservation). Source: https://www.princeedwardisland.ca/sites/default/files/publications/new_sole_proprietorship.pdf (PDF, undated)
  - Renewal: **$75 every 3 years**. Source: new_sole_proprietorship.pdf above.
  - Fees exclude any tax question; whether HST applies to these fees is Not confirmed.
- **Renewal:** a sole proprietorship, partnership or trade name must be renewed every **3 years**; there is a 6-month grace period after expiry, after which status becomes "inactive due to non-payment". Source: Starter Guide PDF. Renewal steps: https://www.princeedwardisland.ca/sites/default/files/publications/re-registration_sole_prop_partnership_business_name.pdf (needs 9-digit CRA Business Number, company key and PEI registration number from the renewal notice).
- **How to file:** online only through OCBR: Reserve a Name (sole proprietorship, purpose New), receive Company PIN and Key by email, then Register a Business > Register Sole Proprietor. Approved certificate of registration is emailed (and includes the CRA Business Number per the Starter Guide). Contact: Corporate Registry, 902-368-4550, askcorporateregistry@gov.pe.ca. Source: new_sole_proprietorship.pdf.
- Processing time: a search result excerpt of the official guide says name review typically takes 5 business days; not read directly, so Not confirmed.
- Screenshot candidate for step 2: https://www.princeedwardisland.ca/en/information/justice-and-public-safety/register-a-business-name (HTML page behind a bot check; capture may need a real browser; page content not read). Fallback that does download: https://www.princeedwardisland.ca/sites/default/files/publications/new_sole_proprietorship.pdf (shows the $40, $90 and $75 figures).

## Federal ID

CRA Business Number, shared with the other Canadian guides: https://www.canada.ca/en/services/taxes/business-registration.html. PEI states the BN appears on the Certificate of Registration (Starter Guide).

## Sales tax

- **HST 15%** in Prince Edward Island (15% since October 1, 2016; it was 14% from April 1, 2013 to September 30, 2016). Source: CRA GST/HST rates table, https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html (page date modified 2025-04-01, fetched 2026-10-17)
- No separate provincial sales tax account. The $30,000 small-supplier threshold is federal and the same as in the Ontario guide. No provincial sales tax screenshot step (SK and QC only).

## Licences

- **Provincial general business licence:** none found. Not confirmed on an official page that says so. The Starter Guide says "There may be additional Provincial regulations required for the operation of your business" and points to a Business Navigator.
- **Charlottetown** (municipal step): a **Home Occupation** application to the Planning & Heritage Department is required for a home-based business. Fee on the form: **$50 (no renovations)** or $5.50 per $1,000 of construction cost (min. $50) with renovations, plus $400 if a Major Variance is required (form undated, found 2026-10-17). Conditions on the form: in a single-detached, modular, semi-detached or duplex dwelling or accessory building; up to 25% of gross floor area or 46.5 sq m (500 sq ft); proprietor must live there; max 1 full-time or 2 part-time non-resident employees; no external storage; no other home occupation on the property; max 1 commercial vehicle on the lot; building permit if renovations. Listed types: home daycare, tourist accommodations, catering, custom sewing/crafts/visual arts production, multi-level marketing retail sales, home office/clerical/computer or telephone-based services; other uses go through a Major Variance review. Source: https://www.charlottetown.ca/common/pages/DisplayFile.aspx?itemId=16444059
  - A search excerpt of the city's permit checklist/bylaw says retail stores are prohibited as a home occupation; I did not read the Zoning & Development Bylaw itself. For the guide: a reseller shipping online orders from home should confirm with Planning & Heritage (70 Kent Street, 902-629-4158, planning@charlottetown.ca) whether the listed "computer-based" category covers online retail with no customer visits.
  - Screenshot candidate for the municipal step: the Home Occupation Application PDF above (opens directly; page 1 shows the provisions, page 2 the fee).
- Other municipalities: check locally.

## Consumer protection and privacy

- **Privacy:** PEI has no private-sector privacy law deemed substantially similar to PIPEDA (the OPC lists only Alberta, BC and Quebec), so PIPEDA applies as in Ontario. The OPC page does not mention PEI by name. Source: https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/r_o_p/prov-pipeda/ (date modified 2020-05-11)
- **Consumer protection:** the Consumer Protection Act (R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. C-19, consolidation "current to March 30, 2026") is mainly about credit: Registrar, disclosure in credit sales, cost-of-borrowing disclosure, unsolicited credit cards, penalties. I read the full contents list and searched the text and found **no internet sales contract provisions** (no "internet", "online", "electronic" or "distance" provisions). Source: https://www.princeedwardisland.ca/sites/default/files/legislation/C-19-Consumer%20Protection%20Act.pdf. Online contracts also fall under the Electronic Commerce Act (E-4.1), https://www.princeedwardisland.ca/sites/default/files/legislation/e-04-1-electronic_commerce_act.pdf (not read in detail). Federal Competition Act rules also apply.

6. Insurance
7. Bank account
8. Bookkeeping and income tax
9. Payment processor
10. Legal pages and privacy

12. Supplier account
